Q1. The Service Discharge Benefit Scheme (SDBS) introduced for Gramin Dak Sevaks operates upon which institutional platform?
A. The NPS-Lite architecture regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority
B. The Employees Provident Fund framework administered by the Central Board of Trustees, EPF
C. The Postal Life Insurance investment structure managed directly by the Directorate of PLI
D. The Public Provident Fund network administered under the Government Savings Promotion Act
Answer: A
Explanation:
The Service Discharge Benefit Scheme (SDBS) was introduced on the recommendation of the Nataraja Murti Committee and operates on the NPS-Lite platform of the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) for low-cost social security administration.

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the eligibility conditions governing enrolment under the Service Discharge Benefit Scheme:

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 1 and 2 only

D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: B
Explanation:
Under SDBS regulations, regularly engaged GDS with at least one year of satisfactory service are eligible, while those due for discharge within three years as on 01.01.2011 were ineligible. Substitutes and provisionally engaged GDS are strictly excluded from coverage.

Q4. An existing Gramin Dak Sevak who opted into SDBS upon its notification in 2011 had rendered 8 years, 7 months, and 20 days of continuous service prior to enrolment. How is the accrued severance amount calculated for addition to the subscriber's Permanent Retirement Account?

Explanation:
Under SDBS Regulation 3(a)(iv), accrued severance for pre-enrolment service is computed at ₹1,500 per completed year, with completed months calculated proportionately. Fractions of 15 days or more count as a complete month, whereas periods under 15 days are ignored.
