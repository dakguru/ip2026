Q98. What is the validity period of an Identity Card issued to a Gramin Dak Sevak, and what is the fee charged for issuing a duplicate card if lost?
A. Validity 3 years; duplicate fee ₹ 20

D. Permanent validity; duplicate fee ₹ 250
Answer: B

GDS Identity Cards are issued free initially, valid for 5 years or till date of discharge (whichever is earlier). If lost or damaged due to carelessness, a duplicate card is issued on payment of a ₹50 fee.

Q99. A regularly engaged GDS is absorbed into a regular departmental post (Multi-Tasking Staff). Per Directorate OM dated 26.11.2024, how are his GDS terminal benefits treated?

Q100. Under Rule 8 of the GDS (Conduct & Engagement) Rules, 2020, what notice period is required for termination of engagement of a GDS who has rendered less than 3 years continuous service?
A. 15 days notice or pay in lieu
B. One month notice or pay in lieu
C. Two months notice or pay in lieu
D. Three months notice or pay in lieu
Answer: B
Explanation:
Rule 8 specifies that the engagement of a GDS who has not rendered more than 3 years continuous engagement is liable to be terminated at any time by one month's notice in writing (or payment of one month TRCA+DA in lieu of notice).
