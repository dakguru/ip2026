Q1. What is the minimum and maximum deposit limit for the National Savings Certificate (VIII Issue) Scheme, 2019?
A. Minimum Rs. 500, Maximum Rs. 1,50,000
B. Minimum Rs. 1,000, Maximum No limit
C. Minimum Rs. 1,000, Maximum Rs. 1,50,000
D. Minimum Rs. 500, Maximum No limit
Answer: B
Explanation: A minimum of Rs. 1000 and any sum in multiples of Rs. 100 may be deposited in an NSC account, and there is no maximum limit for deposit.

Q2. What is the maturity period of a Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) account opened on or after July 1, 2023?
A. 115 months
B. 120 months
C. 124 months
D. 112 months
Answer: A
Explanation: The maturity period of a KVP account opened on or after the first day of July 2023 shall be 115 months, and the deposit shall double on maturity.

Q3. Consider the following statements regarding the premature closure of an NSC (VIII Issue) account:

1. It can be closed prematurely on the death of any or all account holders in a joint account.
2. It can be closed prematurely on forfeiture by a pledgee being a Gazetted Officer.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
C. Both 1 and 2
D. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: C
Explanation: Premature closure of an NSC account is permitted on the death of the account holder in a single account or any/all holders in a joint account, or on forfeiture by a pledgee being a Gazetted Officer.



Q4. Consider the following statements regarding the types of accounts under the KVP Scheme, 2019:

Q5. Consider the following statements regarding the transfer of an NSC account:

Q7. Consider the following statements regarding interest payable on premature closure of NSC:
