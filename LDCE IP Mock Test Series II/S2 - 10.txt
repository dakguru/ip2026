Q1. What is the official Enactment Date of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023?

Q4. Consider the following situation: A Magistrate requires an accused person to provide an undertaking for release without providing any surety. Under BNSS 2023, this specific undertaking is legally termed as:

Q5. Which of the following statements is/are correct regarding the definitions of offences under BNSS 2023?
