Q1. Postal Life Insurance (PLI), the oldest life insurer in India, was originally introduced on which of the following dates?
A. 1st February 1881
B. 12th October 1884
C. 1st February 1884
D. 24th March 1895
Answer: C
Explanation: Postal Life Insurance was introduced on 1st February 1884 with the approval of the Secretary of State for India, making it the oldest life insurer in the country. 12th October is celebrated as PLI Day.
