Q1. Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, what is the maximum time limit for a Public Information Officer to provide information if the request concerns the life or liberty of a person?
A. Within forty-eight hours of receipt of the request
B. Within seventy-two hours of receipt of the request
C. Within twenty-four hours of receipt of the request
D. Within ninety-six hours of receipt of the request
Answer: A
Explanation: Where the information sought concerns the life or liberty of a person, it must be provided within 48 hours of the receipt of the request as per the proviso to Section 7(1) of the Act.

Q2. What is the current term of office for the Chief Information Commissioner as per the Right to Information Rules, 2019?
A. Five years from the date of entering office
B. Three years from the date of entering office
C. Four years from the date of entering office
D. Six years from the date of entering office
Answer: B
Explanation: Under Rule 3 of the RTI (Term of Office, Salaries, Allowances and Other Terms and Conditions of Service...) Rules, 2019, the CIC holds office for a fixed term of three years from entering office.

Q3. As per Section 44(3) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which of the following modifications was made to Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act?
A. It added a new public interest override for foreign data
B. It restricted the disclosure of any third-party records
C. It deleted the internal public interest override proviso
D. It exempted all biometric information from disclosure
Answer: C
Explanation: The DPDP Act, 2023 amended Section 8(1)(j) by deleting the internal "larger public interest" override and the Parliament proviso, simplifying the clause to exempt "information which relates to personal information."

Q4. Consider the following statements regarding the third-party information procedure under Section 11 of the RTI Act:

Explanation: Section 11 of the Act strictly prescribes a 5-day limit for notice, a 10-day representation window for the third party, and a 40-day overall limit from the original request for the PIO's decision.



Q5. A person belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category applies for information involving 50 pages of photocopies. How much fee is chargeable?
