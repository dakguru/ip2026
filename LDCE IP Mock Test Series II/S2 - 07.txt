Q1. A Divisional Head proposes to keep a Branch Post Office open for six hours a day so that its counter timings match those of its Account Office. Which of the following is correct?
A. Valid, provided the Regional Head records his concurrence in the order in writing.
B. Invalid — the power to specify business hours vests solely in the Circle Head.
C. Valid — the Divisional Head is empowered to specify hours for offices under him.
D. Invalid — a Branch Post Office may be kept open for a maximum of five hours a day.
Answer: D
Explanation: Under the Post Office Regulations, 2024 a Branch Post Office may be kept open for a maximum of five hours a day, while a departmental Post Office observes six business hours. The Circle, Regional and Divisional Heads are each empowered to specify the exact business hours of offices under their jurisdiction, but that power operates inside the five-hour cap. [Post Office Regulations, 2024, in force 16.12.2024; Book of BO Rules, Rule 5.]

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the office notices, lists and maps of a Branch Office:

1. The Notice of Hours of Business (Form M-6(c)) is supplied to the Branch Office by the Sub-Divisional Inspector.
2. The Village Sorting List (Form M-52) shows, in alphabetical order, the villages served by each ABPM, whether a letter box exists there, and whether that box carries a changeable plate.
3. The Route List (Form M-53) is accompanied by a map of the beat and specifies the days fixed for departure from and return to the Post Office, and the villages where the ABPM may halt.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
A. 1 and 3 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 2 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: D
Explanation: All three are correct. M-6(c) is supplied by the SDI, not by the Head or Account Office. The changeable plate is a feature of the letter box recorded in the M-52 — it is not a separate register. [Book of BO Rules, Rules 5 and 7.]

Q3. Branch Office X exchanges mail bags directly with an RMS section working on a mail train. Branch Office Y neither exchanges with RMS nor is in direct communication with it; it merely acts as a transit and sorting office for neighbouring post offices. Who supplies the due mail and sorting list to X and to Y respectively?
A. Superintendent, RMS to X; Superintendent of Post Offices to Y
B. Superintendent of Post Offices to X; Superintendent, RMS to Y
C. The Sub-Divisional Inspector to both X and Y
D. The Head of the Circle to both X and Y
Answer: A
Explanation: A Branch Office in direct communication with the RMS is part of the RMS network, so its due mail and sorting list is supplied by the Superintendent, RMS. A Branch Office acting as a transit or sorting office sits within the postal network and is supplied by the Superintendent of Post Offices. Swapping the two authorities is the classic trap. [Book of BO Rules, Rule 8.]

Q5. A customer tenders a parcel weighing 15 kg for booking at a Branch Office, pointing out that the Post Office Regulations, 2024 permit parcels of much greater weight. What is the correct course?
A. Decline; parcels of any weight are booked only at departmental offices, never a BO.
B. Book it; 35 kg is the maximum weight permitted for a parcel under the Regulations.
C. Decline; a Branch Office may itself book a parcel only up to 10 kg in weight.
D. Book it after re-weighing; the registered-parcel maximum of 20 kg is not exceeded.
Answer: C
Explanation: The 10 kg figure has two faces — it is both the manual-carriage limit for a village postman or delivery agent and the maximum weight of a parcel that a Branch Office may itself book. The customer-side network maxima (registered parcel around 20 kg, Speed Post or Business Parcel up to 35 kg) apply at departmental offices, not at a BO. [Book of BO Rules, Rule 9 and Note thereto; Post Office Regulations, 2024.]

Q6. Which of the following correctly states the position on the supply of a cash safe to an extra-departmental Branch Office?

Explanation: It is not mandatory to supply a safe to every extra-departmental Branch Office. Q8. A Branch Office despatches mail in one direction more than once a day. What follows for its date stamps?

Q9. Which statement correctly describes the maintenance of the Book of Postmarks (Form MS-18) at a Branch Office?
A. Impressions are taken only when a stamp or seal is replaced, repaired or reset.
B. Impressions of all stamps holding date or movable type are taken daily before use.

Explanation: The daily impression, taken before the day's work begins, proves that the stamp was legible and correctly set. Q10. Consider the following statements regarding the preservation of books and forms at a Branch Office:

Q13. Which of the following correctly describes the Suggestion / Complaint Book (Form MS-94) at a Branch Post Office?
A. Entries are made by the public in a single copy and countersigned by the BPM.

Q14. A Branch Postmaster tears out a page of the Post Office Order Book (Form MS-8) in order to remove an adverse remark recorded by an inspecting officer. Which of the following is correct?
A. Permissible once the inspecting officer's remark has been fully complied with.

D. A serious offence liable to removal from service; the book has 200 numbered pages.
Answer: D
Explanation: The Order Book is the audit trail of supervision. Its 200 serially numbered pages make any missing page obvious, and the sanction of removal from service exists precisely because a dishonest official's incentive is to make an adverse remark disappear. [Book of BO Rules, Rule 42.]

Q15. A Branch Office circular is issued by the Head of the Circle in October 2022, that is, during the official year 2022-23. Applying the rule for destruction of Branch Office circulars, from what point may it be destroyed?
A. April 2026
B. March 2026
C. October 2025
D. April 2025
Answer: A
Explanation: Branch Office circulars are destroyed 3 years from the month of April following the close of the official year in which they were issued. The official year 2022-23 closes on 31.03.2023; the April following is April 2023; three years from that point is April 2026. The clock does not run from the date of issue. [Book of BO Rules, Rule 44.]

Q18. A Branch Postmaster receives a mail bag from the Account Office and finds the cord and seal bearing clear signs of tampering. What is the correct course of action?
A. Open the bag before two witnesses and record the shortage in the BO Journal.
B. Preserve the bag and label with the seal and cord intact, mark them, and report.
C. Detach the seal, inspect the contents, list the shortage and report to the SDI.
D. Forward the bag unopened to the Account Office with a note recording the suspicion.
Answer: B
Explanation: The seal and cord are the physical evidence of how and where the bag was interfered with. Detaching or opening them destroys the proof and could shift suspicion onto the receiving office. The correct sequence is preserve, then mark, then report — to both the Superintendent and the despatching office — and then record in the Error Book (MS-2). [Book of BO Rules, Rule 53.]

Q19. Which of the following correctly states the position on clearance of letter-boxes at a Branch Office?

Q20. Consider the following statements regarding the taxing of unpaid articles at a Branch Office:

Q22. On a given day a Branch Office has, for despatch, 18 unregistered articles for one particular station and 22 unregistered articles for a particular State. Which bundles must be made up?

Q36. Two officials are appointed to the same cadre on the same date. Recruitment was by a competitive examination in which some vacancies were filled from departmental candidates and some from outsiders. How is their inter se seniority fixed?

D. By the order of preference recorded by the Departmental Promotion Committee
Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 32-E fixes seniority by the date of permanent appointment to the cadre; where that date is the same, the tie-breaker follows the mode of recruitment. Q75. The minimum cash balance of a post office is fixed at Rs. 10,000. Applying Rule 139, what is the least permissible maximum cash balance?
