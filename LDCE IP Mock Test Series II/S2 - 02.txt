Q1. When did the provisions relating to the Central Consumer Protection Authority under Chapter III of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 come into force?
A. 09 August 2019
B. 20 July 2020
C. 24 July 2020
D. 17 October 2000
Answer: C

Q2. Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, which of the following is NOT explicitly included in the definition of a "complainant"?
A. A voluntary consumer association registered under any law
B. A person who buys goods for commercial resale
C. The Central Authority
D. In case of a minor consumer, their legal guardian
Answer: B
Explanation: A person who obtains goods for resale or for any commercial purpose is explicitly excluded from the definition of a "consumer", and therefore cannot be a complainant.
