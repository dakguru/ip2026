Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the Preamble to the Constitution of India:

1. The Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution.
2. It can be amended under Article 368 of the Constitution.
3. The Preamble is justiciable and enforceable in a court of law.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. Statements 1 and 2 only are correct
B. Statements 2 and 3 only are correct
C. Statements 1 and 3 only are correct
D. Statements 1, 2 and 3 are correct
Answer: A
Explanation: In the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), the Supreme Court ruled the Preamble is part of the Constitution and amendable under Article 368. However, it is non-justiciable and cannot be enforced in a court of law.

Q2. Under Article 124 of the Constitution, which of the following is a mandatory condition for the removal of a Supreme Court Judge?

B. A resolution passed by the Lok Sabha with a two-thirds majority
C. An order by the President after an address by both Houses of Parliament
D. A directive from the Union Cabinet validated by the Supreme Court
Answer: C
Explanation: Article 124(4) strictly mandates that a judge can only be removed by a Presidential order passed after an address by both Houses of Parliament supported by a special majority, on grounds of proved misbehaviour or incapacity.
