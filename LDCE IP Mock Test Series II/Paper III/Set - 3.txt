Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the Preamble of the Constitution of India:

1. It is a source of substantive power to the legislature.
2. The objectives enshrined in it are non-justiciable in a court of law.
3. It can be amended under Article 368.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: B
Explanation: The Preamble is neither a source of power to the legislature nor a prohibition upon its powers. It is non-justiciable. As established in the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), it is a part of the Constitution and can be amended under Article 368 without altering its basic structure.

Q2. Under Article 19(2) of the Constitution, reasonable restrictions can be imposed on the freedom of speech and expression on which of the following grounds?

1. Contempt of court
2. Defamation
3. Decency or morality
4. Friendly relations with foreign States
A. 1, 2 and 3 only
B. 2, 3 and 4 only
C. 1, 3 and 4 only
D. 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: D
Explanation: Article 19(2) explicitly permits the State to impose reasonable restrictions on the exercise of the freedom of speech and expression in the interests of sovereignty and integrity of India, security of the State, friendly relations with foreign States, public order, decency or morality, contempt of court, defamation, or incitement to an offence.

Q3. Which of the following statements is/are not correct concerning the Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)?
A. They are fundamental in the governance of the country
B. It shall be the duty of the State to apply them in making laws
C. They can be enforced by the High Courts through writs
D. They aim to establish social and economic democracy
Answer: C
Explanation: Under Article 37 of the Constitution, the Directive Principles of State Policy are expressly declared as non-justiciable; thus, they cannot be enforced by any court. However, they are fundamental in the governance of the country, and it is the State's duty to apply these principles in making laws.

Q4. According to Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, in which of the following situations can the formal requirement of an inquiry be dispensed with before dismissing a civil servant?

Q5. Regarding the advisory jurisdiction of the Supreme Court under Article 143, consider the following statements:

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
