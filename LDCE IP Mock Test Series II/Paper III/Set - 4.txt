Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the removal of a Supreme Court Judge under Article 124 of the Constitution:

1. A judge can be removed by the President only after an address by both Houses of Parliament in the same session.
2. The address requires a majority of total membership and two-thirds of members present and voting.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. Statement 1 is correct but Statement 2 is incorrect
B. Statement 2 is correct but Statement 1 is incorrect

D. Neither Statement 1 nor Statement 2 are fully correct
Answer: C
Explanation: Article 124(4) of the Constitution explicitly states that a Supreme Court Judge shall not be removed except by an order of the President passed after an address by each House supported by a special majority in the same session.

Q2. Under Article 311 of the Constitution, which of the following scenarios permits the dismissal of a civil servant without holding a departmental inquiry?
