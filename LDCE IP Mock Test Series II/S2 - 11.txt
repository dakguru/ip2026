Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the time limit for disposal of requests under the Right to Information Act, 2005:

1. If the request concerns the life or liberty of a person, information must be provided within forty-eight hours.
2. If an application is submitted through a CAPIO, a period of five days is added to the statutory response time.
3. Failure to decide within the stipulated time limit is legally deemed as a refusal of the request.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: D
Explanation: Section 7(1) specifies a 30-day limit, reduced to 48 hours for life/liberty. Section 5(3) adds 5 days if routed via a CAPIO. Section 7(2) deems failure to decide within time as a refusal.

Q3. An applicant requests a document containing both public information and strictly confidential commercial trade secrets. The PIO decides to sever the exempt part. Which of the following details is the PIO NOT legally bound to notify the applicant about?

Explanation: Section 10(2) of the RTI Act requires the PIO to notify the applicant of the partial access, the reasons, the decision-maker's name/designation, the fee, and review/appeal rights. Previous applicants are not disclosed.

Q96. Under the MSMED Act, 2006, within what precise time frame must a procuring entity raise an objection regarding discrepancies in materials supplied by an MSE to avoid deemed acceptance?

Q97. According to the DoPT O.M. dated 02.09.2022, how many days of Special Maternity Leave are granted to a female servant in the specific event of stillbirth or death of a child soon after birth?

Q98. A permanent government servant is granted Leave Not Due (LND) but subsequently resigns voluntarily without returning to duty. What is the financial consequence?

Q100. Under Rule 43-B, if a female servant adopts a child who is six months old, for what exact duration can leave of the kind due be granted in continuation of the 180-day Child Adoption Leave?
