Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the historical evolution of Postal Life Insurance (PLI):

1. PLI was introduced on 1st February 1884 with the approval of the Secretary of State for India, making it the oldest life insurer in the country.
2. The cover was extended to the employees of the Telegraph Department in 1888, and to female employees of the P&T Department in 1894.
3. The original upper limit of life cover was Rs. 4,000, which now stands at Rs. 50 Lakhs for all schemes combined.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: D
Explanation: All three statements are correct. PLI began on 1st February 1884; it was extended to the Telegraph Department (1888) and to female P&T employees (1894) when no other insurer covered female lives; and the original Rs. 4,000 ceiling has been progressively raised to the present Rs. 50 Lakhs combined limit. (Source: Directorate of PLI, Intro & Eligibility document, pli.indiapost.gov.in)
