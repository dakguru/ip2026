Q1. Under Rule 2 of the Central Civil Services (Implementation of National Pension System) Rules, 2021, which of the following categories of personnel is covered under these rules?
A. Civilian employees appointed substantively in the Defence Services on or after 01.01.2004
B. Persons appointed on a regular contract basis in various Union Government Ministries
C. Personnel locally recruited in foreign countries for service in Indian diplomatic missions
D. Members of the All-India Services appointed to civil posts under the Union Government
Answer: A
Explanation:
Rule 2 provides that the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021 apply to Government servants, including civilian Government servants in the Defence Services, appointed substantively on or after 01.01.2004. Contract employees, foreign local recruits, and All-India Services members are expressly excluded under Rule 2.

Q2. What is the prescribed time limit under Rule 4 of the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021 for the Head of Office to countersign and forward the Common Subscriber Registration Form (CSRF) to the Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO)?

B. Within three working days from the date of joining of the employee
C. Within five working days from the date of receipt of the application

Explanation:
Under Rule 4(2) of the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021, the Head of Office shall countersign the registration application indicating the date of receipt and forward it to the DDO within three working days of the Government servant joining service.

Q3. Under Rule 4(8) of the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021, what is the statutory outer time limit for crediting the first monthly contribution into the Individual Pension Account of a newly joined Government servant?

Explanation:
Rule 4(8) stipulates that the first contribution shall be credited to the Individual Pension Account within twenty days of the date of submission of the registration application or by the last date of the month in which the Government servant joined, whichever is later.

Q4. For the purpose of deducting monthly contributions under Rule 5 of the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021, what constitutes 'Emoluments' for an employee actively on duty?

Q7. Under Rule 7 of the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021, how is the Government contribution regulated when a subscriber is on medical leave without leave salary?

Q17. Under the Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) notified in 2025, what is the eligibility criteria and quantum for the full assured pension payout?

Answer: B
Explanation:
Under UPS notified by DFS on 24.01.2025, the full assured payout is 50% of the average basic pay drawn during the last 12 months immediately preceding superannuation, requiring a minimum of 25 years (300 months) of qualifying service.

Q18. An employee covered under the Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) retires on superannuation after completing exactly 20 years (240 months) of qualifying service. If his last 12 months' average basic pay is ₹60,000, what is his admissible monthly assured payout?
A. ₹30,000 per month
B. ₹24,000 per month
C. ₹20,000 per month
D. ₹18,000 per month
Answer: B
Explanation:
Full payout = 50% of ₹60,000 = ₹30,000. For service between 10 and 25 years, proportionate payout applies: ₹30,000 × (240 ÷ 300) = ₹30,000 × 0.8 = ₹24,000 per month.

Q19. Under the Unified Pension Scheme (UPS), what is the minimum guaranteed pension after 10 years of qualifying service, and what is the family payout percentage to the spouse on the retiree's death?

Under UPS, a minimum guaranteed payout of ₹10,000 per month is assured on superannuation after at least 10 years of qualifying service, and the family payout to the spouse is 60% of the admissible payout drawn by the subscriber before demise.

Q20. What is the formula for the separate lump sum payment payable on retirement under the Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) notified in 2025?
