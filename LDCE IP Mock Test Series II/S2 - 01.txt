Q1. When did the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002, come into force?
A. 17th January 2003
B. 1st April 2004
C. 1st July 2005
D. 31st March 2005
Answer: C
Explanation: The Prevention of Money Laundering Act (Act 15 of 2003) was enacted on 17th January 2003, but it officially came into force on 1st July 2005. The other dates reflect enactment or incorrect financial year timelines.

Q2. What is the standard punishment prescribed for the offence of money-laundering under Section 4 of the PMLA, 2002?
A. Imprisonment for 3 to 5 years and a fine
B. Rigorous imprisonment for 3 to 7 years and a fine
C. Rigorous imprisonment for 5 to 10 years and a fine
D. Imprisonment up to 7 years without any fine
Answer: B
Explanation: Section 4 prescribes rigorous imprisonment for a term not less than 3 years but extending to 7 years, along with a fine. The penalty extends to 10 years only if the offence involves narcotics or drugs.

Q3. Under the PMLA, 2002, what is the maximum period for which a property can be provisionally attached by the Director?
A. 90 days from the date of order
B. 180 days from the date of order
C. 365 days from the date of order
D. 60 days from the date of order
Answer: B
Explanation: Under Section 5 of the Act, if the Director has reason to believe proceeds of crime are likely to be concealed, he may provisionally attach the property for a period not exceeding 180 days.

Q4. Which of the following correctly describes the term of office for the Chairperson or a Member of an Adjudicating Authority under PMLA?

B. 5 years or until attaining 65 years of age

D. 4 years or until attaining 62 years of age
Answer: B
Explanation: According to Section 6 of the PMLA, the Chairperson and every Member shall hold office for a term of 5 years from the date of entering the office, or until attaining 65 years of age, whichever is earlier.

Q5. How long is a reporting entity required to maintain records of transactions after a transaction is completed under PMLA obligations?

Explanation: Section 12 of the PMLA mandates that every reporting entity must maintain a record of all transactions for a period of five years from the date of the transaction.

Q25. Which of the following is NOT an "officially valid document" for opening a Post Office account?
