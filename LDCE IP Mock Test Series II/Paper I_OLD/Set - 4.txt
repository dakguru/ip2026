Q1. Under Section 9 of the Post Office Act, 2023, which of the following is correct regarding the interception of a postal article in transmission?

B. An officer specially authorized by the Central or State Government may intercept an article on the occurrence of a public emergency.
C. The Postmaster General can unilaterally order interception of any article suspected of containing contraband without central or state authorization.

Explanation: Section 9 of the Post Office Act, 2023, empowers the Central Government, State Government, or any specially authorized officer to intercept, open, or detain any item during a public emergency or for public safety.

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the liability of the Post Office under Section 4 of the Post Office Act, 2023:
