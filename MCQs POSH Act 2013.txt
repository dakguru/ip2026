1. What is the commencement date of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013?
A) 22 April 2013
B) 09 December 2013
C) 01 July 2024
D) 01 January 2014
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Act came into force from 09 Dec. 2013, while it was enacted on 22 April 2013.

2. According to the Act, which of the following is an essential criterion to be defined as an "Aggrieved woman"?
A) She must be a regular employee of the workplace
B) She must belong to the organized sector
C) She can be of any age and whether employed or not at the workplace
D) She must be over 18 years of age
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: An 'Aggrieved woman' is defined as a woman without age limit, any place (working place, office, house, etc.), whether employed or not.

3. Under the Act, who is NOT included in the definition of a "Domestic worker"?
A) A woman employed on a daily wage basis for household work
B) A member of the family of the employer doing household work
C) A woman employed through an agency for part-time household work
D) A woman employed temporarily for household work in cash or kind
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The definition of 'Domestic worker' explicitly excludes any member of the family of the employer.

4. Which of the following best defines an "Employee" under the provisions of the Act?
A) Only a person employed on a regular basis
B) A person employed for any work on regular, temporary, ad hoc or daily wage basis, either directly or through an agent
C) Only a person working in the organized sector
D) A person possessing a written contract of employment with the principal employer
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: An employee includes a person employed at a workplace for any work on regular, temporary, ad hoc or daily wage basis, either directly or through an agent, including a contractor, with or without knowledge of the principal employer.

5. Which of the following places is defined as a "Workplace" under the Act?
A) Only Government-owned establishments
B) Only private sector organizations employing more than 50 persons
C) A house, stadium, hospital, or any place visited by the employee during the course of employment, including transportation provided by the employer
D) Only establishments registered under the Factories Act
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The definition of workplace is very broad and includes private sectors, hospitals, sports institutes, dwelling places, and any place visited by the employee arising out of employment including employer-provided transportation.

6. What is the defining characteristic of an "Un-organized sector" workplace according to the Act?
A) It lacks a formal HR department
B) It is an enterprise owned by individuals or self-employed workers with fewer than ten workers
C) It has more than ten workers but lacks trade union representation
D) It exclusively employs temporary or ad hoc workers
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: An un-organized sector workplace means an enterprise owned by individuals or self-employed workers where the number of such workers is less than ten.

7. Which of the following circumstances occurring along with an act of sexual harassment may also amount to sexual harassment under Section 3?
A) An explicit promise of preferential treatment in her employment
B) Merely demanding higher productivity targets
C) Transferring an employee based on a routine transfer policy
D) Enforcing strict attendance rules
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Section 3 explicitly mentions an implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment in her employment as a circumstance connected with an act which may amount to sexual harassment.

8. When is an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) required to be constituted at administrative units or offices?
A) Only at the headquarters of the organization
B) At all administrative units or offices located at different places or divisional/sub-divisional levels
C) Only in units with more than 100 women employees
D) In offices located only in metropolitan cities
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Where the offices or administrative units are located at different places or divisional or sub-divisional level, the Internal Committee shall be constituted at all administrative units or offices.

9. Who must be nominated as the Presiding Officer of the Internal Complaints Committee?
A) Any senior male employee with legal knowledge
B) A member from a non-governmental organization
C) A woman employed at a senior level at the workplace from amongst the employees
D) The District Officer appointed by the Government
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Presiding Officer shall be a woman employed at a senior level at the workplace from amongst the employees. If not available, she shall be nominated from other offices/units of the workplace.

10. What is the mandated gender ratio requirement for the Internal Complaints Committee?
A) At least one-third of the total members must be women
B) At least one-half of the total members must be women
C) All members must be women
D) There is no specific gender ratio mandated
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Act mandates that at least one-half of the total Members so nominated on the Internal Committee shall be women.

11. What is the maximum tenure for the Presiding Officer and members of the Internal Complaints Committee?
A) One year
B) Three years
C) Five years
D) Until superannuation
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Presiding Officer and every Member of the Internal Committee shall hold office for a period not exceeding three years from the date of their nomination.

12. In which of the following scenarios must a sexual harassment complaint be directed to the Local Complaints Committee (LCC) rather than the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)?
A) When the ICC has already started an inquiry but the complainant wants a faster resolution
B) When the complaint is against a co-worker in a large multinational company
C) When the workplace has fewer than ten workers or if the complaint is against the employer himself
D) When the complainant objects to the constitution of the ICC
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Local Complaints Committee receives complaints from establishments where the ICC has not been constituted due to having less than ten workers or if the complaint is against the employer himself.

13. Which officer is designated to receive complaints from rural or tribal areas (blocks, talukas) and forward them to the Local Complaints Committee?
A) The Presiding Officer of the ICC
B) A designated Nodal Officer
C) The Superintendent of Police
D) The Sarpanch of the village
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The District Officer shall designate one nodal officer in every block, taluka and tehsil (or ward/municipality) to receive complaints and forward the same to the Local Complaints Committee within seven days.

14. Who is responsible for nominating the Chairperson of the Local Complaints Committee?
A) The State Government
B) The Central Government
C) The District Officer
D) The National Commission for Women
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Local Complaints Committee consisting of a Chairperson and Members is nominated by the District Officer.

15. What specific background is preferred for at least one of the nominees from NGOs or associations in the Local Complaints Committee?
A) A background in human resources management
B) A background in law or legal knowledge
C) Experience in police enforcement
D) A degree in psychology
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Act prefers that at least one of the nominees from NGOs/associations should have a background in law or legal knowledge.

16. What is the statutory time limit for an aggrieved woman to file a written complaint of sexual harassment to the ICC or LCC?
A) Within 30 days of the incident
B) Within 60 days of the incident
C) Within three months from the date of the incident or date of last incident
D) Within six months from the date of the incident
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Any aggrieved woman may make a complaint within a period of three months from the date of incident, or in case of a series of incidents, within three months from the date of the last incident.

17. If an aggrieved woman is unable to make a complaint on account of her physical or mental incapacity or death, who is permitted to file the complaint?
A) Only a colleague who witnessed the incident
B) Only the Presiding Officer of the ICC
C) Her legal heir or such other prescribed person
D) The District Officer suo motu
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Where the aggrieved woman is unable to make a complaint due to physical/mental incapacity or death, her legal heir or a prescribed person may make a complaint.

18. What action must the Internal Committee or Local Committee take before initiating an inquiry, if requested by the aggrieved woman?
A) Immediately suspend the respondent
B) Take steps to settle the matter through conciliation
C) Lodge an FIR with the local police
D) Grant three months paid leave to the woman unconditionally
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Committee may, before initiating an inquiry and at the request of the aggrieved woman, take steps to settle the matter through conciliation.

19. Which of the following is strictly PROHIBITED during the conciliation process of a sexual harassment complaint?
A) Requiring a written apology
B) Allowing a family member to accompany the complainant
C) Making a monetary settlement the basis of conciliation
D) Recording the settlement in writing
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 10 clearly states that no monetary settlement shall be made as a basis of conciliation.

20. Upon the arrival of a settlement through the conciliation process, what is the next procedural step for the Committee regarding the inquiry?
A) No further inquiry shall be conducted
B) The inquiry continues simultaneously for record
C) The inquiry is suspended for 90 days and then resumed
D) The complaint is forwarded to the police for investigation
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Where a settlement is arrived through conciliation under Section 10, no further inquiry shall be conducted.

21. In cases of domestic workers or where no formal service rules exist, what action must the Local Complaints Committee (LCC) take if a prima facie case of sexual harassment exists?
A) Award financial compensation instantly
B) Refer the case to the District Magistrate for independent adjudication
C) Forward the complaint to the police within seven days for registration under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (or IPC Section 509)
D) Conduct a civil trial
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Where no rules exist or in case of a domestic worker, if a prima facie case exists, the LCC shall forward the complaint to the police within seven days for registration under Section 509 IPC (now BNS).

22. During an inquiry into a complaint, the Internal Committee possesses the same powers as a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 in respect of which of the following?
A) Awarding rigorous imprisonment
B) Confiscating private properties of the respondent
C) Summoning witnesses and examining them on oath, and requiring production of documents
D) Ordering a police arrest
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Committee has powers vested in a civil court for summoning and enforcing attendance of any person, examining them on oath, and requiring production of documents.

23. Which of the following defines the maximum statutory period within which the inquiry into a sexual harassment complaint must be completed by the Committee?
A) 30 days
B) 60 days
C) 90 days
D) 120 days
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: As per Section 11, the inquiry shall be completed within a period of ninety days (90 days).

24. During the pendency of an inquiry, on a written request by the aggrieved woman, which of the following actions can the Committee recommend to the employer?
A) To terminate the respondent's services immediately
B) To grant leave to the aggrieved woman up to a period of three months
C) To withhold the salary of the respondent during the entire inquiry
D) To make the inquiry details public for transparency
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Section 12 allows the Committee to recommend granting leave to the aggrieved woman up to a period of three months. This leave is in addition to her normal leave entitlement.

25. What is the time limit for the Internal Committee (ICC) or Local Committee (LCC) to provide the inquiry report to the employer or District Officer after completing the inquiry?
A) 7 days
B) 10 days
C) 15 days
D) 30 days
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: On completion of an inquiry, the ICC or LCC shall provide a report of its findings to the employer or District Officer within 10 days of completion.

26. After receiving the inquiry report from the Committee, within what period must the employer or the District Officer act upon the recommendations?
A) 15 days
B) 30 days
C) 60 days
D) 90 days
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The employer or District Officer shall act upon the recommendation within sixty days of receipt of the inquiry report.

27. If an Internal Committee concludes that a complaint of sexual harassment is malicious or explicitly false, what is the prerequisite before recommending punitive action against the complainant?
A) Instant termination of the complainant
B) A malicious intent must be established after an inquiry in accordance with the prescribed procedure
C) A separate complaint by the respondent must be filed in a civil court
D) The complainant must be fined Rs. 50,000 directly
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Section 14 mandates that the malicious intent on the part of the complainant shall be established after an inquiry before any action is recommended.

28. According to Section 14 of the Act, which of the following facts alone does NOT attract punitive action against the complainant?
A) Producing forged documents
B) Knowing the complaint to be utterly false beforehand
C) Mere inability to substantiate a complaint or provide adequate proof
D) Engaging a witness to give false evidence
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: A mere inability to substantiate a complaint or provide adequate proof need not attract action against the complainant for filing a false/malicious complaint.

29. Which of the following specific factors is NOT statutorily mandated to be considered while determining compensation under Section 15?
A) Medical expenses incurred by the victim
B) Income and financial status of the respondent
C) The size and gross revenue of the employer's company
D) Mental trauma, pain, suffering and emotional distress caused
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 15 specifies considering mental trauma, loss of career opportunity, medical expenses, income of the respondent, and feasibility of payment, but NOT the employer's company revenue.

30. Under Section 16 of the Act, which specific legislative tool is overridden to maintain the strict confidentiality of the identity of the aggrieved woman, respondent, and witnesses?
A) The Information Technology Act, 2000
B) The Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973
C) The Right to Information Act, 2005
D) The Indian Evidence Act, 1872
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 16 states, "Notwithstanding anything contained in the Right to Information Act, 2005, the contents of the complaint, the identity... shall not be published, communicated or made known to the public."

31. If a person entrusted to deal with sexual harassment complaints wrongly publishes or communicates the identity of the aggrieved woman, what penalty is attracted?
A) A fine of exactly Rs. 50,000 under the Act
B) Penalty in accordance with the service rules applicable to the said person, or as may be prescribed if no rules exist
C) Imprisonment up to 3 years
D) Automatic dismissal from government service without inquiry
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: As per Section 17, any person contravening Section 16 shall be liable for penalty in accordance with the provisions of their applicable service rules, or as prescribed.

32. Any person aggrieved from the recommendations of the Internal Committee, or the action taken by the employer, may prefer an appeal within what time frame?
A) Within 30 days of the incident
B) Within 60 days of the receipt of the complaint
C) Within 90 days of the recommendations
D) Within 120 days of the final action
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: According to Section 18, the appeal shall be preferred within a period of ninety days of the recommendations.

33. Which of the following is an explicit duty of the employer under the Sexual Harassment of Workplace Act, 2013?
A) Disclosing the names of all complainants in the annual organizational report
B) Providing mandatory legal defense counsel to the respondent
C) Organising workshops and awareness programmes at regular intervals for sensitising the employees
D) Paying the full compensation from the company's funds regardless of the respondent's financial capability
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 19 clearly outlines organizing workshops and awareness programs at regular intervals for sensitising the employees as a statutory duty of the employer.

34. Under the updated statutory guidelines (post-July 2024), any references to criminal offences involving sexual harassment must be routed through which legislation?
A) The Indian Penal Code (IPC)
B) The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)
C) The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005
D) The Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: As per the Final Compliance Notes, references to criminal prosecution must now be routed through the relevant provisions of the BNS, 2023 following the repeal of the IPC effective 1st July 2024.

35. The Internal Committee or Local Committee is required to prepare and submit an annual report to which authorities?
A) Only to the State Government
B) Only to the Ministry of Women and Child Development
C) To the employer and the District Officer
D) Directly to the National Commission for Women
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 21 mandates that the Internal Committee or Local Committee prepare an annual report in each calendar year and submit it to the employer and the District Officer.

36. Under Section 26, what is the maximum initial fine imposed on an employer who fails to constitute an Internal Complaints Committee?
A) Rs. 10,000
B) Rs. 25,000
C) Rs. 50,000
D) Rs. 1,00,000
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: An employer who fails to constitute an Internal Committee, or fails to act upon recommendations, is punishable with a fine which may extend to Rs. 50,000.

37. If an employer is previously convicted of failing to constitute an ICC and is subsequently convicted for the exact same offence again, what consequence can follow besides double the normal punishment?
A) Confiscation of all personal assets of the Directors
B) Cancellation of his licence or withdrawal, or non-renewal of registration required for carrying on his business
C) Life imprisonment for the employer
D) Forfeiture of all bank accounts to the State Government
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Under Section 26(2), a subsequent identical offence renders the employer liable to twice the punishment and cancellation of his licence, withdrawal, or non-renewal of registration.

38. Are offences specified under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013 considered cognizable or non-cognizable?
A) Cognizable and Bailable
B) Cognizable and Non-Bailable
C) Strictly Non-Cognizable
D) Cognizable only if approved by the District Magistrate
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 27 explicitly outlines that every offence under this Act shall be non-cognizable, meaning an arrest cannot be made without a warrant.

39. What is the lowest hierarchy of judicial court competent to take cognizance and try any offence punishable under this Act?
A) Gram Nyayalaya or Panchayat Forum
B) Executive Magistrate
C) Metropolitan Magistrate or a Judicial Magistrate of the first class
D) District Sessions Court
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Section 27 states that no court inferior to that of a Metropolitan Magistrate or a Judicial Magistrate of the first class shall try any offence punishable under this Act.

40. How many copies of the complaint should an aggrieved woman submit to the ICC or LCC according to the procedural rules?
A) One original copy
B) Three copies
C) Six copies
D) Ten copies
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: According to the 'Sexual Complaint Submission and Deciding Procedure', the complainant should submit the complaint to the ICC or LCC in six copies along with supporting documents.

41. Upon the receipt of the complaint, the Complaints Committee must send one copy to the respondent within a maximum period of how many working days?
A) 3 working days
B) 5 working days
C) 7 working days
D) 10 working days
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: On receipt of the complaint, the committee shall send one copy to the respondent within the period of seven working days.

42. How much time does the respondent have to file their reply to the complaint, along with their list of documents and witnesses, upon receiving a copy from the committee?
A) 7 days
B) 10 days
C) 15 days
D) 30 days
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The respondent shall file their reply within a period not exceeding 10 days from the date of receipt of the complaint copy from the committee.

43. Under what specific condition can the Chairperson or Presiding Officer of the Committee exercise the right to take an ex-parte decision on a complaint?
A) If the respondent submits a written denial of the charges
B) If either party requests a transfer location during the pendency
C) If the complainant or respondent fails, without sufficient cause, to attend three consecutive hearings convened by the Chairperson
D) If the District Officer demands an immediate closure without heating
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Chairperson/Presiding Officer holds the right to take an ex-parte decision if either party fails, without sufficient cause, to attend three consecutive hearings.

44. Before an ex-parte order is passed against a party for failing to attend hearings, what mandatory procedural requirement must be fulfilled?
A) A public notice must be published in a local newspaper
B) A notice in writing must be given, 15 days in advance, to the party concerned
C) Concurrence from the nearest Police Station must be acquired
D) A verbal warning via a telephone call is sufficient
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The ex-parte order may not be passed without giving a notice in writing, 15 days advance, to the party concerned.

45. Which of the following is true concerning the fee and allowances paid to a Local Committee Chairperson for carrying out functions under the Act?
A) Rs. 200/- per day
B) Rs. 250/- per day
C) Rs. 500/- per day
D) They work honorarily with no allowances
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Under the Fee and Allowances section for the Local Committee (LCC), the Chairperson receives Rs. 250/- per day, while members receive Rs. 200/- per day.

46. When must an employer indicate the number of sexual harassment cases filed and their disposal regarding statutory transparency?
A) Only when summoned by a Metropolitan Magistrate
B) Only if asked under the RTI Act by a third party
C) Employers must include this information in the annual report of his organisation, or intimate the District Officer if no such report applies
D) It must be pinned on the public notice board of every office
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: As per Section 22, the employer shall include the number of cases filed, if any, and their disposal under this Act in the annual report of his organisation.

47. What happens to the complaint process if an aggrieved woman dies while the sexual harassment inquiry is ongoing?
A) The case is immediately closed and archived
B) A fresh complaint must be initiated from zero
C) The legal heir or such other person may pursue the complaint if the initial complaint was legitimately made
D) It is transferred directly to the State's High Court
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Under Section 9(2), if the aggrieved woman is unable to make a complaint on account of her physical or mental incapacity or death, her legal heir or such other prescribed person may make or pursue the complaint.

48. A Civil Court has jurisdiction to determine matters which the Internal Committee or Local Committee is empowered to determine. True or False?
A) True, if the compensation claimed exceeds Rs. 1 lakh
B) True, civil courts possess concurrent jurisdiction over the proceedings
C) False, civil courts expressly do not have jurisdiction in respect of any matter which the ICC or LCC is empowered to determine
D) False, but they can issue injunctions to halt the ICC's proceedings
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: According to the Final Provisions, Civil courts do not have jurisdiction in respect of any matter which the Internal Committee or the Local Committee is empowered to determine.

49. Who establishes and transfers funds specifically to pay fees and allowances to the District Officer and Local Complaints Committee members?
A) The District Magistrate from general local revenue
B) The State Government via a dedicated fund, utilizing grants originating from the Central Government
C) Employers in the district proportionally
D) The National Human Rights Commission
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: According to Section 8, the Central Government makes grants to the State Government, and the State Government may set up a fund to pay the sums required for the District Officer and Local Complaints Committee.

50. Under Section 24, whose responsibility is it to develop relevant information, educational materials, and organize awareness programs to advance the understanding of the public regarding this Act?
A) Private multinational corporations
B) Trade Unions exclusively
C) Non-Governmental Organizations
D) The Appropriate Government, subject to the availability of resources
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Section 24 mandates that the Appropriate Government shall take measures to formulate training, develop educational material, and organize awareness programmes subject to financial and resource constraints.
