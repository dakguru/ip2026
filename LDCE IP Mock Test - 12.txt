"Right Process" is NOT one of them.

Q20. (RTI Act & Rules) [Difficulty: Moderate] Which of the following is NOT explicitly included in the definition of "Information" under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, 2005? A. E-mails B. Logbooks C. Q21. (RTI Act & Rules) [Difficulty: Scenario] A CPIO receives an RTI request involving third-party confidential information.
