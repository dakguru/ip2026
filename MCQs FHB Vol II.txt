1. What is the primary function of the Postal Financial Handbook Volume II?
A) Handling Post Office Savings Bank Rules
B) Providing instructions for Post Office Accounts and banking procedures
C) Providing regulations on foreign mail distribution
D) Rules for recruitment of postal employees
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Postal Financial Handbook Volume II is primarily concerned with instructions and regulations relating to Post Office Accounts, handling of cash, and accounting procedures.

2. A sub post office receives its regular cash remittances from which office?
A) Circle Office
B) Head Post Office
C) Branch Post Office
D) Divisional Office
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A Sub Post Office operates under a Head Post Office, which supplies cash resources and remittances needed for daily transactions.

3. Under the FHB rules, the daily balance of cash at a Sub-Post Office should not exceed what limit?
A) The authorized maximum limit fixed by the Superintendent of Post Offices
B) Rs. 50,000 always
C) The authorized maximum limit fixed by the Postmaster General
D) Rs. 1 Lakh
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Remittances and cash retention at SOs are governed by the maximum limits fixed by the Divisional Head (Superintendent of Post Offices).

4. Which register is used to record the daily transactions of cash in a Head Post Office?
A) HO Cash Book
B) BO Journal
C) SO Slip
D) Daily Account
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The HO Cash Book is the primary accounting record maintained at the Head Office for all cash receipts and payments.

5. When a remittance is sent from a Head Office to a Sub Office, what essential document must accompany it for reconciliation?
A) Remittance advice / SO Slip
B) Daily Account
C) HO Summary
D) BO Slip
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The SO Slip contains details of the remittance and instructions from the HO, acting as both an advice and acknowledgment record.

6. Under FHB Vol II, which authority is responsible for periodically verifying the physical cash balance at a Head Post Office?
A) The Head Postmaster
B) The Postmaster General
C) The Divisional Superintendent
D) The Senior Superintendent of Railway Mail Service
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The Head Postmaster is directly responsible for verifying the physical cash balance in the HO treasury daily.

7. How are discrepancies in the cash balance of a Head Post Office treated?
A) Ignored if less than Rs. 100
B) Kept pending in a suspense account until recovered or written off
C) Automatically written off by the Postmaster
D) Adjusted from the postmaster's salary immediately without inquiry
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Detailed accounting procedure in FHB requires that discrepancies like shortages are placed in Unclassified Payments or a Suspense Account pending investigation and recovery/orders.

8. According to FHB rules, what is the meaning of "Treasury"?
A) Any local commercial bank
B) The government treasury or a bank conducting government treasury business
C) The internal vault of the Post Office
D) The central reserves of India Post
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: "Treasury" in the context of government financial rules implies a district or sub-treasury, or a branch of the RBI/SBI conducting treasury business for the government.

9. Who acts as the principal accounting officer for the entire Postal Circle?
A) Chief Postmaster General
B) General Manager (Finance) / Director of Accounts (Postal)
C) Divisional Head
D) Treasury Officer
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The GM (Finance) or Director of Accounts (Postal) is the principal accounts officer who audits and compiles the accounts of the Circle.

10. What action must a Postmaster take if a Sub Office sends a Remittance without proper authorization?
A) Return the remittance immediately
B) Refuse to accept the cash
C) Accept the cash, account for it, and report the irregularity
D) Keep it in a personal locker
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Government cash must never be refused or kept unaccounted for. It must be accepted into the accounts and the irregularity reported for disciplinary action.

11. According to the Financial Handbook, which of the following is considered 'Cash'?
A) Promissory Notes
B) Currency notes, coins, and RBI drafts
C) Unused postage stamps
D) Both A and B
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Cash includes legal tender (currency notes and coins) as well as drafts drawn on the RBI or Treasury, but not unused postage stamps, which are accounted as stock.

12. The "Daily Account" is a document generated by which office?
A) Head Office for the Circle Office
B) Sub Office for the Head Office
C) Branch Office for the Circle Office
D) HO for the Director of Accounts
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Daily Account is an essential document prepared by the Sub Post Office and sent to its Head Office showing all its daily transactions.

13. The responsibility for the safe custody of cash in a Sub Post Office lies with whom?
A) Sub Postmaster (SPM)
B) Postal Assistant
C) Head Postmaster
D) Postman
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The Sub Postmaster (SPM) is personally responsible for the safe custody of cash and valuables at the Sub Post Office.

14. When excess cash is found in the cash balance at the end of the day, how should it be accounted for?
A) Distributed among staff
B) Kept separately for future shortages
C) Credited to Unclassified Receipts (UCR)
D) Returned to the public next day
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Any unexplained excess cash found must be credited to Unclassified Receipts (UCR) pending investigation.

15. What is the prescribed method to remit excess cash from a Sub Office to a Head Office?
A) Sending through a local courier
B) Sending it via ordinary mail
C) Remittance through a Special Carrier, Dak Runner, or Bank Remittance as authorized
D) Handing it over to a stranger
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Cash remittances between offices must follow strict security protocols, utilizing authorized carriers, railway mail service, or bank remittances.

16. What is the Head Office Summary?
A) A monthly report of employee attendance
B) A daily consolidation of accounts from all its Sub Offices and its own transactions
C) An annual financial audit report
D) A summary of complaints received
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Head Office Summary is the grand consolidation of the HO's own transactions plus the Daily Accounts received from all SOs under its jurisdiction.

17. Under FHB Vol II, what is an "ACG-17" form primarily used for?
A) A receipt for money paid by the public
B) A standard voucher/receipt for payments made by the post office to payees
C) A register of registered articles
D) A form for opening an SB account
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: ACG-17 is the standard receipt form (voucher) used to obtain the signature of the payee for miscellaneous payments made by the PO.

18. If a remittance sent to a Sub-Office is lost in transit, where is the loss initially booked?
A) UCR
B) UCP (Unclassified Payments)
C) Directly to the SPM's personal account
D) Ignored till found
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The amount is booked under Unclassified Payments (UCP) indicating a pending loss until it is recovered or written off by a competent authority.

19. A Sub Office is divided into which of the following categories for accounting purposes?
A) Selection Grade and Time Scale
B) Delivery and Non-Delivery
C) Treasury / Bank SOs and Non-Bank SOs
D) All of the above
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: From a financial remittance point of view, SOs are primarily distinguished between those that draw cash directly from a Bank/Treasury and those purely dependent on HO remnants.

20. How frequently should the Head Postmaster check the SO Slips and Daily Accounts?
A) Weekly
B) Bi-weekly
C) Daily
D) Monthly
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Head Postmaster must scrutinize the Daily Accounts and SO Slips daily to ensure prompt accounting and detect irregularities.

21. What constitutes the 'Cash Balance' of a post office?
A) Office furniture and computers
B) Currency notes, coins, and authorized cash equivalents
C) Unpaid postal orders and stamp stock
D) Salary register
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Cash balance strictly includes currency, coins, and specific authorized cash equivalents. Stamps and postal orders have separate stock accounts.

22. During inspection, the Inspector of Posts observes a cash shortage. What is the immediate action?
A) Suspend the Postmaster instantly without asking
B) Require the Postmaster to make good the shortage immediately, failing which book it under UCP and report to higher authorities
C) Write it off if it is below Rs. 1000
D) Adjust it against the next day's collection
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The inspecting officer must order the official to make good the loss immediately. If not, it is reported and booked under UCP.

23. When accounting for Indian Postal Orders (IPOs) sold, they are treated as:
A) Unclassified Receipts
B) Revenue of the state government
C) Remittances
D) Specific IPO receipt head
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: IPO sales are accounted for under a specific, dedicated accounting head for Indian Postal Orders.

24. The 'Schedule of Receipts and Payments' is prepared by the Head Office for submission to:
A) The Postmaster General
B) The Director of Accounts (Postal) / GM Finance
C) The Ministry of Finance
D) The State Treasury
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The HOs prepare and submit schedules of receipts and payments periodically to the Director of Accounts (Postal) for auditing and compilation.

25. What is the procedure for dealing with mutilated currency notes received from the public?
A) The Post Office must exchange them without question
B) Post Offices are not legally bound to accept mutilated notes; they should be directed to the RBI or designated banks
C) Post office sends them to the local police
D) They are accepted and kept forever
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: FHB guidelines stipulate that POs should not accept mutilated currency, and the public must be advised to exchange them at banks/RBI per RBI guidelines.

26. Which rule book specifically dictates the format and maintenance of the Head Office Cash Book?
A) Postal Manual Vol VI
B) Financial Handbook Volume II
C) POSB Manual
D) Book of Information
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: FHB Volume II contains detailed instructions and forms for the accounting procedures, including the HO Cash Book.

27. Who determines the minimum and maximum cash balance limits for a Head Post Office?
A) Chief Postmaster General
B) Superintendent of Post Offices
C) Postmaster General / Head of Circle
D) Director General of Posts
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The maximum/minimum cash balances for Head Offices are usually fixed by the Head of the Circle (PMG/CPMG).

28. If a Branch Office (BO) requires cash, how is it requested?
A) By sending an email to the PMG
B) Through the BO Daily Account or a special requisition to its Account Office (SO or HO)
C) By borrowing from a local bank
D) By stopping all withdrawals
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A BO requests cash from its Account Office (which can be an SO or HO) through notes on the BO daily account or a separate requisition.

29. What is a "Cash Bag"?
A) A bag used by postmen to carry letters
B) A leather/canvas bag securely sealed and locked, used for remitting cash between post offices
C) The personal bag of the postmaster
D) A bag for storing old stamps
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A Cash Bag is the specialized, secure container used for authorized remittances of cash between offices.

30. In accounting terms, drawing money from the bank into the Post Office Treasury is known as:
A) Bank Remittance
B) Drawings from Bank
C) Unclassified Receipt
D) Treasury Loan
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: "Drawings from Bank" refers to the specific transaction of pulling cash from a designated Bank to fund the Post Office Treasury.

31. If a Postmaster discovers fake currency in the cash balance, what should be done?
A) Spend it immediately
B) Throw it away
C) Impound it, do not return it to the tenderer, and follow RBI guidelines for reporting counterfeit notes
D) Cut it and give it back
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Fake currency must be impounded immediately, not returned, and reported to the police/nodal bank as per RBI directives.

32. The stamp balance of a Head Post Office is verified by the Head Postmaster:
A) Once a year
B) Once a month
C) Daily
D) Only during inspections
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Just like the cash balance, the stock of stamps in the HO treasury must be tallied and verified by the Head Postmaster daily.

33. Which accounting concept prevents the Post Office from keeping government receipts outside the regular accounts?
A) Unity of command
B) Centralization
C) Consolidated Fund principles (No keeping money out of account)
D) Cash accounting parity
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: A fundamental rule of government finance is that all money received on behalf of the government must be brought into account immediately.

34. The term 'Remittance to Bank' means:
A) Paying bank loan EMIs
B) Depositing surplus post office cash into the designated Bank
C) Transferring employee salaries
D) Exchanging old notes
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: It signifies depositing post office excess cash into the authorized bank (SBI/RBI) to clear the PO's cash balances.

35. How are the daily accounts of Branch Offices (BOs) integrated into the Head Office accounts?
A) Directly sent to the Director of Accounts
B) Consolidated into the BO Summary at the Account Office (SO or HO), and then incorporated into the main accounts
C) Ignored until the end of the month
D) Merged with the Postmaster's personal account
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The BO Daily Accounts are first consolidated at their Account Office (SO/HO) in a BO Summary, which then flows into the daily accounting stream of the HO.

36. An expenditure incurred for the immediate repair of a broken window at the PO is booked under:
A) Capital Expenditure
B) Office Contingencies / Contingent Expenditure
C) Unclassified Payments
D) Salaries
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Recurring, minor operational or repair expenses are generally booked under Office Contingency allowances.

37. For claiming contingent expenditure, what is strictly required?
A) Verbal approval
B) A proper voucher (ACG-17) or vendor receipt correctly sanctioned by the competent authority
C) Only an entry in the cash book
D) Approval from the public
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Valid payment requires an authorized voucher or receipt proving the expenditure was made and sanctioned appropriately.

38. The register used for recording all unpaid money orders at a Head Office is called:
A) Register of Unpaid MOs
B) Register of VPPs
C) SO Summary
D) BO Slip
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Unpaid or undelivered MOs must be tracked securely in the Register of Unpaid MOs until they are repaid to the remitter or forfeited.

39. What does the "Nil" balance in a Sub Post Office signify?
A) The office has closed permanently
B) The office has remitted all its cash to the HO or Bank and has zero cash on hand at the day's close
C) The postmaster is on leave
D) There is a cash shortage
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A "Nil" cash balance simply means exactly zero cash is retained overnight, having remitted surplus and completely exhausted remaining cash.

40. Who conducts the audit of the Post Office accounts compiled by the Director of Accounts (Postal)?
A) The State Government Auditor
B) Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG) through the Director General of Audit (P&T)
C) The Inspector of Posts
D) The Reserve Bank of India
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The statutory external audit of the Department of Posts' accounts is conducted by the CAG of India via the DGA(P&T).

41. According to the FHB, what happens to revenue stamps affixed on a voucher that is being submitted as proof of payment?
A) They must be removed and reused
B) They must be defaced/cancelled so they cannot be reused
C) They are ignored
D) They are preserved in a museum
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Any revenue stamp affixed on a receipt must be immediately defaced or cancelled to prevent its fraudulent reuse.

42. How are the accounts of PLI (Postal Life Insurance) premium collections at POs routed?
A) Directly sent to LIC
B) Brought into the PO Cash book and accounted for under the PLI specific scheduling heads, then forwarded to DAP/PLI Directorate
C) Kept in a separate bank account by the PM
D) Sent to the State Government
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: PLI cash collections flow into the PO cash balance, but are scheduled strictly under PLI accounting heads.

43. Which form is used for preparing the Sub Office Daily Account?
A) ACG-22
B) ACG-17
C) ACG-3
D) ACG-11
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Form ACG-22 (or structurally similar local variants as per FHB rules) is standard for rendering daily accounts.

44. Can a Postmaster use Post Office cash to cash a personal cheque for a customer?
A) Yes, if the customer is known
B) Yes, up to Rs. 5000
C) No, cashing of private cheques from PO funds is strictly prohibited
D) Yes, if the customer pays a fee
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The FHB explicitly forbids using government cash balances to encash private cheques.

45. The 'Error Book' in a post office is used for:
A) Writing complaints against public
B) Noting down irregularities, accounting errors, and instructions given by inspecting officers
C) Recording leave of staff
D) Writing draft letters
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Error Book is an official record maintained for noting all procedural errors, shortages, and corrective directions from inspections.

46. When a Head Office receives a remittance from the Bank, what entry is made in the Cash Book?
A) Receipts side: Drawings from Bank
B) Payments side: Remittance to Bank
C) Receipts side: UCR
D) Payments side: Cash transferred
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Cash coming into the HO from the bank is entered on the receipt side as 'Drawings from Bank'.

47. What is an 'Objection Statement'?
A) A complaint by a customer
B) A statement issued by the Audit or DAP pointing out irregularities, missing vouchers, or wrong classifications in the submitted accounts
C) A police FIR
D) A grievance application by staff
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The DAP/Audit issues Objection Statements for irregularities found in the HO accounts, which the Head Postmaster must reply to and settle.

48. A Sub Post Office is authorized to retain cash up to its maximum authorized balance. What happens if the liabilities on a certain day exceed this balance?
A) It must refuse to make payments
B) It must ask customers to come back tomorrow
C) It must request a special remittance from its Head Office or Bank in advance
D) It can borrow from the nearby market
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The SPM must anticipate liabilities and requisition extra cash from the Account Office or Bank to ensure public payments are not dishonored.

49. Payment of a Time-Barred Money Order requires sanction from:
A) The Postman
B) The Sub Postmaster
C) The competent higher authority (Divisional Head / Audit) depending on the expiry period
D) No sanction is required, just pay
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Void or time-barred MOs cannot be paid directly without revalidation or sanction from the competent authority.

50. Under FHB Vol II, what is the meaning of 'Scale Register'?
A) A register denoting weights and measures scales used in PO
B) A register maintained in the Head Office to record the sanctioned establishments (staff, scales of pay) for drawing salary bills
C) A register of postal distances
D) A register for ranking employees
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Scale Register records the authorized establishment strength and pay scales of the office, crucial for passing salary bills.
