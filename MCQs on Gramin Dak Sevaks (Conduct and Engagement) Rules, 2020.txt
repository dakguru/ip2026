### **Section 3: Termination & Discharge (Rule 8)**

**Q18. Which of the following statements regarding the "Termination of Engagement" under Rule 8 is INCORRECT?**
A. It applies to Sevaks with less than three years of continuous engagement.
B. Notice period required is one month.
C. It acts as a disqualification for future employment.
D. TRCA plus DA can be paid in lieu of notice.
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 8 Termination of Engagement.


* **Logic:** Termination under Rule 8 is a discharge simpliciter for those with less than 3 years of service. It is not a penalty like "Dismissal" and does not inherently disqualify one from future employment.

**Q19. The "Sevaks" are entitled to which allowance during the notice period of termination under Rule 8?**
A. Basic Pay + DA
B. Basic TRCA + Dearness Allowance
C. Only Basic TRCA
D. Subsistence Allowance
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 8 Termination.


* **Logic:** The Sevak is entitled to claim a sum equivalent to the amount of Basic Time Related Continuity Allowance plus Dearness Allowance.

### **Section 4: Penalties (Rule 9)**

**Q20. Which Rule of the GDS (Conduct and Engagement) Rules, 2020 deals with the "Nature of Penalties"?**
A. Rule 8
B. Rule 9
C. Rule 10
D. Rule 12
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9 Nature of Penalties.


* **Logic:** Rule 9 outlines the classification of Minor and Major penalties applicable to Sevaks.

**Q21. Which of the following constitutes a "Major Penalty" under Rule 9?**
A. Censure
B. Withholding of annual increase in TRCA
C. Recovery from TRCA
D. Removal from engagement
**Correct Answer: D**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9 (Major Penalties).


* **Logic:** Censure, withholding increments, and recovery are Minor Penalties. Removal from engagement is listed specifically as a Major Penalty (Item 8).

**Q22. Which penalty under Rule 9 is described as "Reduction to a lower stage in the TRCA slab"?**
A. Penalty No. 4
B. Penalty No. 5
C. Penalty No. 6
D. Penalty No. 7
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9 Major Penalties.


* **Logic:** Clause (6) of Rule 9 defines the Major Penalty of reduction to a lower stage in the TRCA slab for a period exceeding three years.

**Q23. If a GDS is dismissed from engagement (Rule 9, Penalty 9), what is the consequence regarding future engagement?**
A. No disqualification
B. Disqualification for 3 years
C. Ordinarily a disqualification for future engagement
D. Disqualification only within the same Circle
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9, Clause 9.


* **Logic:** Dismissal is distinct from Removal; Dismissal ordinarily implies disqualification for future engagement.

**Q24. Which of the following is NOT a Minor Penalty under Rule 9?**
A. Censure
B. Compulsory Discharge
C. Recovery from TRCA
D. Withholding of annual increase in TRCA
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9 Penalties.


* **Logic:** Compulsory Discharge (Clause 7) is listed under Major Penalties.

**Q25. "Reduction to a lower stage in the TRCA slab" as a Major Penalty shall not exceed a specified period of:**
A. One year
B. Two years
C. Three years
D. It must exceed three years
**Correct Answer: D**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9 Major Penalties.


* **Logic:** The definition of this specific Major Penalty (Clause 6) is for a "specified period exceeding three years."

**Q26. Under Rule 9, "Debarring of a Sevak from appearing in recruitment exam for MTS" is valid for a period not exceeding:**
A. 1 year
B. 2 years
C. 3 years
D. 5 years
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 9 Minor Penalties.


* **Logic:** The penalty of debarring from recruitment exams is limited to a period "not exceeding 3 years."

### **Section 5: Disciplinary Authorities & Procedure (Rule 10, 10-A to G)**

**Q27. Which of the following is designated as the "Engaging Authority" for a Branch Post Master (BPM)?**
A. Inspector of Posts (IP)
B. Assistant Superintendent of Posts (ASP)
C. Divisional Head (Gr. "A" JTS/STS / Gr. "B")
D. Director of Postal Services
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Competent Authority Schedule.


* **Logic:** The schedule clearly identifies the Divisional Head as the Engaging Authority for the post of BPM.

**Q28. Who is the Appellate Authority for an Assistant Branch Post Master (ABPM) regarding penalties imposed under Rule 9?**
A. Sub-Divisional Head
B. Divisional Head (Gr. "A" JTS/STS/ Gr. "B")
C. Director of Postal Services
D. Postmaster General
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Competent Authority Schedule.


* **Logic:** For ABPM, the Disciplinary Authority is the Sub-Divisional Head, and the next superior authority, the Divisional Head, acts as the Appellate Authority.

**Q29. In the case of an ABPM, who serves as the Disciplinary Authority for both Minor and Major Penalties?**
A. Inspector of Posts only
B. Senior Postmaster
C. Sub-Divisional Head (ASP/IP)
D. Divisional Head
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Competent Authority Schedule.


* **Logic:** The Sub-Divisional Head (ASP or IP) is the Disciplinary Authority empowered to impose both Minor and Major penalties (1 to 9 of Rule 9) on an ABPM.

**Q30. Which Rule governs the "Procedure for imposing Major Penalty"?**
A. Rule 10-A
B. Rule 10-B
C. Rule 10-D
D. Rule 11
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 10-B.


* **Logic:** Rule 10-B specifically outlines the procedure required before imposing any Major Penalty.

**Q31. Departmental proceedings instituted after the discharge of a Sevak (Rule 10-G) require the sanction of:**
A. The Head of Circle
B. The Director General
C. The President
D. The Engaging Authority
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 10-G.


* **Logic:** Proceedings not instituted while the GDS was in engagement cannot be instituted after discharge without the sanction of the President.

**Q32. According to Rule 10-D, if a GDS accepts the charge unconditionally, is an inquiry necessary for a Major Penalty?**
A. Yes, always mandatory.
B. Yes, for record-keeping.
C. No, there would be no need for an inquiry.
D. Only if the President directs.

**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 10-D.


* **Logic:** The rule states: "In case the charge is accepted by him/her unconditionally, there would obviously be no need for an inquiry for imposing major penalty."

**Q33. Proceedings under Rule 10-G (Disciplinary proceedings after discharge) shall not be in respect of any event which took place more than _____ before such institution.**
A. 1 year
B. 2 years
C. 3 years
D. 4 years
**Correct Answer: D**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 10-G.


* **Logic:** There is a limitation: proceedings cannot be instituted for events that took place more than four years prior.

**Q34. For a Dak Sevak, who is the Engaging Authority?**
A. Inspector of Posts
B. Sr. Postmaster (Gr. B) / ASP / ASRM / IP / IRM
C. Divisional Head

* **Logic:** The schedule lists Senior Postmaster (Gr B), ASP, ASRM, IP, or IRM as the Engaging Authority for Dak Sevaks.

**Q35. Rule 11 states that the provisions of Rule 10 (Procedure) do NOT apply when:**

### **Section 6: Put Off Duty (Rule 12)**

**Q36. If a GDS is put off duty, what percentage of TRCA is payable as ex-gratia compensation for the first 90 days?**
A. 50%
B. 25%
C. 75%
D. No compensation is paid
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 12 (Put off Duty).


* **Logic:** A Sevak is entitled to ex-gratia payment equal to 25% of their TRCA plus admissible DA for the initial period.

**Q37. Under Rule 12, who is competent to put a Sevak off duty in cases involving fraud or embezzlement if they are not the Engaging Authority?**
A. Any Postal Assistant
B. The Head Postmaster
C. IP or ASP of the Sub-Division
D. The District Collector
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 12 Put off Duty.


* **Logic:** The IP or ASP of the Sub-Division may put a Sevak off duty in fraud cases under immediate intimation to the Engaging Authority.

**Q38. If the period of put off duty exceeds 90 days, the compensation may be increased by a suitable amount not exceeding:**
A. 25%
B. 30%
C. 50%
D. 75%
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 12 Put off Duty.


* **Logic:** If the delay is not attributable to the Sevak, the authority can increase the ex-gratia payment by up to 50% of the amount admissible during the first 90 days.

**Q39. An order made by an ASP (who is not the Engaging Authority) to put a Sevak off duty ceases to be effective after how many days if not confirmed by the Engaging Authority?**
A. 10 days
B. 15 days
C. 30 days
D. 45 days
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 12 Put off Duty.


* **Logic:** Such an interim order by a subordinate authority expires after 15 days unless confirmed or cancelled.

**Q40. If a GDS is absconding and subsequently put off duty, are they entitled to ex-gratia payment?**
A. Yes, 25%
B. Yes, 50%
C. No
D. Yes, if the Divisional Head approves
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 12 Put off Duty.


* **Logic:** The proviso explicitly states that a Sevak who has been absconding or remains absent unauthorizedly shall not be entitled to any compensation.

### **Section 7: Appeals, Revision & Review (Rule 13-20)**

**Q41. What is the time limit for submitting an appeal under Rule 14?**
A. 45 days
B. 60 days
C. Three months
D. Six months
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 14 Period of limitation for Appeal.


* **Logic:** No appeal shall be entertained unless submitted within three months from the date the appellant receives the order.

**Q42. Under "Review" (Rule 20), who has the power to review an order passed under these rules?**
A. The Head of Circle
B. The President
C. The Director General
D. The Revision Authority
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 20 Review.


* **Logic:** The Rule clearly vests the power of Review in The President.

**Q43. Which authority acts as the Revision Authority for a BPM?**
A. Divisional Head
B. Director of Postal Services (DPS)
C. Postmaster General (PMG) / CPMG
D. President
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Competent Authority Schedule.


* **Logic:** For a BPM, while the DPS is the Appellate Authority, the Revision Authority is the PMG or CPMG.

**Q44. What is the limitation period for a Revision petition to be reopened by the appellate authority on its own motion?**
A. 3 months
B. 6 months
C. 1 year
D. 2 years
**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 19 Revisions.


* **Logic:** No case shall be reopened under this rule after the expiry of six months from the date of the order to be revised (with exceptions for Govt/Head of Circle/PMG).

**Q45. Which authority is the "Review Authority" for all categories of GDS (BPM/ABPM/Dak Sevak)?**
A. Chief Postmaster General
B. Secretary (Posts)
C. President
D. Member (Personnel)
**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Competent Authority Schedule.


* **Logic:** The schedule uniformly lists "President" as the Review Authority for all GDS posts.

**Q46. The Appellate Authority must consider which of the following when reviewing an appeal?**
A. Whether the penalty is excessive, adequate, or inadequate
B. Whether the GDS has political connections
C. Whether the GDS is close to retirement
D. Whether the GDS has paid the appeal fee
**Correct Answer: A**
**Explanation:**

* 
**Rule/Concept:** Rule 18 Consideration of Appeal.


* **Logic:** The Appellate Authority looks at procedure compliance, justification of findings, and the proportionality of the penalty (excessive/adequate/inadequate).
