PRESERVATION PERIOD OF RECORDS  
Understanding the exact preservation periods of departmental records is a critical 
component of daily postal operations and constitutes highly testable material for 
departmental competitive examinations. Proper record management ensures regulatory 
compliance, seamless audits, and adherence to established legal frameworks. 
These structured notes provide a comprehensive, chronological breakdown of the 
retention schedules governing various Postal records/registers/documents.  
Key areas covered in this material include: 
• Savings Bank (SB) Records:  Account opening forms, KYC documents, silent 
account ledgers, and claim cases. 
• Mail & Sorting Branch:  Franking machine ledgers, mail lists, and bag account 
reports. 
• Accounts & Sub Accounts: Cash books, pay bills, GPF statements, and expenditure 
records. 
• Miscellaneous: Disciplinary proceedings, service books, and standing orders. 
The compilation progresses from records requiring immediate destruction to those 
preserved for extended durations and those retained permanently, enabling systematic 
understanding and quick revision. 
Mastering these preservation schedules establishes a strong foundation for 
administrative accuracy, audit preparedness, and departmental examination success.
