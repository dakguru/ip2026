### [CONSOLIDATED ANSWER KEY & EXPLANATIONS]

**Q1: B**
*Concept:* In the Core Banking Solution (CBS) terminology used by India Post, all operational units (HOs, SOs) are referred to as Service Outlets (SOLs).
*Reference:* Chapter 1, Rule 1.

**Q2: B**
*Concept:* The standard tenure for SB/SC Counter Assistants in HOs and large SOs is fixed at 3 years to prevent monopoly and fraud.
*Reference:* Chapter 1, Rule 2(9).

**Q3: B**
*Concept:* Form SB-4(a) is the designated Stock Register for maintaining records of Passbooks (SB-5 and SB-5A) in Head and Sub Offices.
*Reference:* Chapter 2, Rule 4(6).

**Q4: B**
*Concept:* Form SB-103 (Pay-in-Slip) is the standard voucher used for depositing cash or cheques into Savings Bank accounts.
*Reference:* Chapter 2, Rule 5.

**Q5: A**
*Concept:* SB-26 is the Preliminary Receipt issued by Branch Postmasters for initial deposits when opening new accounts, acknowledging receipt of money and documents before the passbook is generated.
*Reference:* Chapter 2, Rule 7.

**Q6: B**
*Concept:* Form SB-28 is the formal receipt given to a depositor when their passbook is retained by the Post Office for any official purpose (like interest entry or audit).
*Reference:* Chapter 2, Rule 8.

**Q7: D**
*Concept:* A letter from a Gazetted Officer is only valid if accompanied by another OVD containing the address. Standalone letters are no longer primary OVDs under strict KYC/AML norms.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 11 (Note 1 & 2).

**Q8: B**
*Concept:* The minimum balance for a standard Post Office Savings Account is Rs. 500. Non-maintenance attracts a fee.
*Regulatory Update:* This limit was revised from Rs. 50 to Rs. 500 w.e.f. 11.12.2020.

**Q9: B**
*Concept:* Nomination has been made mandatory for all National Savings Schemes accounts opened on or after 18.12.2019 to facilitate smooth claim settlements.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 12(1).

**Q10: B**
*Concept:* A "Joint B" account is an "Either or Survivor" account, meaning any of the account holders can operate it independently. "Joint A" requires all holders to operate jointly.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 13(2).

**Q11: B**
*Concept:* A minor who has attained the age of 10 years can open and operate an account in their own name. Below 10, it must be operated by a guardian.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 14(1).

**Q12: B**
*Concept:* An account is classified as "Silent" if no deposit or withdrawal transaction takes place for three continuous financial years.
*Reference:* Chapter 4, Rule 62.

**Q13: B**
*Concept:* The fee for transferring an account is Rs. 100 plus applicable taxes (GST).
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 21 & Fee Schedule.

**Q14: C**
*Concept:* In the case of a deceased claim, interest is payable on the balance up to the date of payment/closure of the account.
*Reference:* Chapter 19, Rule 172.

**Q15: B**
*Concept:* For an illiterate depositor, the thumb impression must be attested by a witness known to both the depositor and the Post Office. The BPM can witness but an independent witness is preferred/required in specific contexts.
*Reference:* Chapter 4, Rule 57(1)(b).

**Q16: B**
*Concept:* As per RBI guidelines adopted by POSB, the validity of a cheque is 3 months from the date of issue.
*Reference:* Chapter 4, Rule 57(2).

**Q17: D**
*Concept:* A depositor can nominate up to four individuals in a single account.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 12(1).

**Q18: B**
*Concept:* IES (Inquire on Signatures) is the Finacle menu command used to view and verify the specimen signature and photograph of the depositor.
*Reference:* Chapter 4, Rule 48.

**Q19: B**
*Concept:* Section 194N mandates TDS deduction @ 2% on cash withdrawals exceeding Rs. 20 Lakh in a year for persons who have not filed Income Tax Returns for the previous three years.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 46.

**Q20: A**
*Concept:* SB-10(b) is the application form used by a depositor to request the transfer of their account from one SOL to another.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 22.

**Q21: C**
*Concept:* Conversion of a Single account to a Joint account (or vice versa) is not permitted under the new rules effective 18.12.2019. To add a name, the account must be closed and a new one opened.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 38.

**Q22: C**
*Concept:* A POSB cheque cannot be issued for an amount less than Rs. 50.
*Reference:* Chapter 4, Rule 57(2)(a)(iii).

**Q23: A**
*Concept:* The Long Book Detailed Report (BRN) lists all transactions of the day and must be verified against vouchers by the staff.
*Reference:* Chapter 3, Rule 33.

**Q24: C**
*Concept:* The fee for issuing a duplicate passbook is Rs. 50 + GST.
*Reference:* Chapter 2, Rule 25.

**Q25: D**
*Concept:* Under PMLA rules, any cash transaction (deposit or withdrawal) exceeding Rs. 10 Lakh must be reported in the Cash Transaction Report (CTR).
*Reference:* Appendix I (KYC/AML Norms).

**Q26: C**
*Concept:* The minimum monthly installment for an RD account is Rs. 100 or multiples of Rs. 10.
*Reference:* Chapter 6.

**Q27: C**
*Concept:* An RD account can be closed prematurely only after 3 years (36 months) from the date of opening.
*Reference:* Chapter 6, Rule 94.

**Q28: B**
*Concept:* A loan facility is available on RD accounts after the account has been in operation for 1 year and 12 installments have been paid.
*Reference:* Chapter 6, Rule 89.

**Q29: C**
*Concept:* The minimum deposit for opening a Time Deposit (TD) account is Rs. 1,000.
*Reference:* Chapter 7.

**Q30: C**
*Concept:* While TD interest is compounded quarterly, it is payable annually.
*Reference:* Chapter 7, Rule 98.

**Q31: B**

*Concept:* This reflects the "Dynamic Currency Protocol" update.

**Q32: C**

*Reference:* Chapter 8, Rule 105(5).

**Q33: D**

**Q34: C**

**Q35: B**

*Reference:* Chapter 10.

**Q36: B**
*Concept:* A loan from a PPF account can be taken from the 3rd financial year up to the end of the 6th financial year.
*Reference:* Chapter 10.

**Q37: C**

**Q38: C**

**Q40: C**
