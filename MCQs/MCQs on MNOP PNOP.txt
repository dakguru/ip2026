**Q13: C** – Decentralization to Taluk level is **not** a component; MNOP focuses on consolidation (NSHs/ICHs). **Q14: C** – There are **96 L1** hubs for First Class Unregistered mail. **Q15: B** – The Delivery Norm for Speed Post between Metro Cities is **1-3 days**. **Q16: B** – The norm for 2-wheeler beats in a congested area is **5 Minutes/KM**. **Q17: C** – At least **95%** of accountable articles must be processed through IMA. **Q18: B** – The network consists of **79 NPH/Level-1 (L1) and 110 PH/Level-2 (L2)** hubs. **Q19: B** – The parcel bag label is **Yellow** for Air connectivity (Speed Post Parcel). **Q20: B** – NDCs are set up where parcel density exceeds **1 Parcel per Sq K.M.** **Q21: B** – The fixed dimensions are **45cm x 35cm x 56cm**. 
**Q22: C** – The fixed incentive is Rs. 300/- per smartphone per month. **Q23: C** – **Blue Color** is used to mark different **processing areas**. **Q24: B** – Small Category Parcel Hubs have a throughput of **121 to 250 parcels per hour**. **Q25: C** – A Roller Container can hold up to **200 parcels** (20 bags x 10 parcels).
