Q1. Under which File No. is the Branch Post Office Inspection Questionnaire issued by the Inspection Division, Department of Posts?
   A. 16-02/2017-Inspn.
   B. 16-03/2019-Inspn.
   C. 17-31/2016-GDS
   D. 24-3/2012/PO
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: The Branch Post Office Inspection Questionnaire is issued by the Inspection Division of the Directorate under File No. 16-03/2019-Inspn., which is the governing file for the current questionnaire.

Q2. The revised Branch Office Inspection Questionnaire forming the current base document was issued vide Letter No. 16-03/2019-Inspn. dated which date?
   A. 09.08.2019
   B. 24.09.2020
   C. 15.04.2021
   D. 07.02.2022
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: The revised BO Inspection Questionnaire (current base document, 20 pages, Sections A to K) was issued vide Letter No. 16-03/2019-Inspn. dated 09.08.2019.

Q3. The current base Branch Office Inspection Questionnaire comprises how many pages and is organised into which range of sections?
   A. 18 pages, Sections A to J
   B. 25 pages, Sections A to L
   C. 20 pages, Sections A to K
   D. 20 pages, Sections A to H
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: The revised questionnaire issued vide Letter No. 16-03/2019-Inspn. dated 09.08.2019 is the current base document running to 20 pages and organised into Sections A to K.

Q4. Consider the following statements regarding revisions to Q. No. 36 (Passbook Verification) of the BO Inspection Questionnaire: 1. The original frequency was one passbook per month. 2. The revision dated 15.04.2021 changed the collection frequency to one passbook for every two months. 3. The revision dated 07.02.2022 added Note 2 regarding repeated passbook verification. Which of the above is/are correct?
   A. 2 and 3 only
   B. 1 and 2 only
   C. 1 and 3 only
   D. 1, 2 and 3
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: The latest consolidated position is that Q.36 was revised on 15.04.2021 (frequency changed to 1 passbook per 2 months) and on 07.02.2022 (Note 2 added). Statement 1 is incorrect because the original 2019 requirement was 5 passbooks per scheme, not one per month.

Q5. Under the latest position of Q. No. 36 (w.e.f. 07.02.2022), how many passbooks are to be collected and verified for each scheme since the last inspection at a Branch Office?
   A. 5 passbooks per scheme
   B. 12 passbooks per scheme
   C. 6 passbooks per scheme
   D. 10 passbooks per scheme
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Following the revision to 1 passbook for every two months, the current requirement under Q.36 is 6 passbooks for each scheme since the last inspection (one for each of the six bi-monthly periods).

Q6. As per the technical norms for a Branch Post Office, the prescribed working hours of the BO should be:
   A. Minimum 3 hours and maximum 4 hours
   B. Minimum 5 hours and maximum 6 hours
   C. Minimum 4 hours and maximum 6 hours
   D. Minimum 4 hours and maximum 5 hours
   Correct Answer: D
   Explanation: While verifying technical information of the BO, the inspecting officer must check that the BO functions as per the norm of a minimum of 4 hours and a maximum of 5 hours.

Q7. In Section A (Administration) of the BO Inspection Questionnaire, the prescribed frequency of Mail Overseer visits to a Branch Office is:
   A. Once per month
   B. Once per fortnight
   C. Once per quarter
   D. Twice per quarter
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Q.7 requires the inspecting officer to check the effectiveness of Mail Overseer visits, which are prescribed once per quarter, and whether the MO has verified accounts and noted the results in the order book.

Q8. To obtain the Facility ID and Profit/Cost Centre of a Branch Office in SAP, which T-code is used by inputting the office type as 'BO' and the receiver PINCODE?
   A. ZFID
   B. MB52
   C. FAGLL03
   D. HACLI
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: For the Facility ID and Profit/Cost Centre ID of the BO, the inspecting officer uses T-code ZFID in SAP, entering the office type as 'BO' and the receiver PINCODE, then clicking Execute.

Q9. Regarding records at a Branch Office, which of the following is to be preserved as a permanent record and must NOT be weeded out?
   A. Error Book
   B. Village Sorting List
   C. BO Order Books
   D. Route List and Beat Map
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: While weeding out old records, the inspecting officer must ensure that BO Order Books are preserved as permanent records and not weeded out, even though other records may be disposed of.

Q10. When operating the DARPAN device, from which power source must the Main Computing Device (MCD) always be powered?
   A. From the battery cradles
   B. Directly from the Solar UPS
   C. Directly from the Electricity Supply
   D. From an external generator
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Q.15 requires that the DARPAN MCD is always powered from the batteries (using both battery cradles alternately) and is NOT directly connected to the Solar UPS or Electricity Supply.

Q11. In the context of network checks at a Branch Office, the abbreviation 'CSQ' noted for each Business Client Application stands for:
   A. Cellular Service Quotient
   B. Check Signal Strength Quality
   C. Connectivity Status Quality
   D. Customer Service Queue
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: Q.16.4 requires the inspecting officer to note the CSQ (Check Signal Strength Quality) value, which reflects the network connectivity availability inside the BO for each Business Client Application.

Q12. What type of login access do Inspecting Persons (IPs) have on the DARPAN device for inspection purposes?
   A. Full administrative login
   B. BPM-level login
   C. Read-only login
   D. Substitute login with Aadhaar
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Q.16.3 notes that IPs have a 'Read-only' login; access for inspection is obtained via 'Edit RICT Admin details' in the RICT Admin Management Menu.

Q13. For verifying VP/COD articles against which VPMOs have been booked since the Date of Last Inspection, which application reference is prescribed in Section C?
   A. FAGLL03 – GL Head 4200030002
   B. MB51 – GL Head 4200030002
   C. ZFID – GL Head 4200030001
   D. HACLI – GL Head 4200030002
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Q.20 prescribes the use of FAGLL03 with GL Head 4200030002 to check whether VPMOs have been booked against the VP articles delivered since the Date of Last Inspection (DLI).

Q14. Working hours of a Branch Office in respect of business, receipt and despatch of mails are required to be fixed as per which order?
   A. OM No. 17-31/2016-GDS dated 25.06.2018
   B. OM No. 16-03/2019-Inspn. dated 09.08.2019
   C. DG Letter No. 24-3/2012/PO dated 01.10.2018
   D. DG Letter No. 6-18/2010-PE-II dated 07.05.2010
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Q.22 requires that working hours of the BO be fixed as per OM No. 17-31/2016-GDS dated 25.06.2018, keeping in view mode of transport, distance from the Account Office and workload.

Q15. During inspection of the Branch Office, the wallet balance is verified against the physical cash in hand. Which application/path is prescribed to ascertain the wallet balance?
   A. Delivery App → Inventory Report
   B. DEMO Booking App → Wallet Balance
   C. MB52 in SAP with Plant ID
   D. Postinfo mobile app → Balance
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: Q.25 prescribes asking the BPM to login to the DEMO Booking App, click the navigation icon (three vertical lines) and then Wallet Balance; alternatively, a DTR of the previous day may be generated from SAP.

Q16. Under Section D (Finance and Accounting), the balances of the BO account are required to be checked for a randomly selected day at what interval since the last inspection?
   A. Weekly
   B. Monthly
   C. Bi-monthly
   D. Quarterly
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Q.27 requires checking the balances of the BO account for one day selected at random, bi-monthly, since the last inspection, by comparison with the balances noted by the Account Office on the BO slips.

Q17. Remittance norms for a Branch Office (relating to retention and exchange of cash) are governed by which Directorate Letter?
   A. Letter No. 24-3/2012/PO dated 01.10.2018
   B. Letter No. 17-31/2016-GDS dated 25.06.2018
   C. Letter No. 6-18/2010-PE-II dated 07.05.2010
   D. Letter No. 16-02/2017-Inspn. dated 24.09.2020
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Q.28 requires the inspecting officer to verify whether norms for remittances have been followed as mentioned in Directorate Letter No. 24-3/2012/PO dated 01.10.2018 while examining cash retention at the BO.

Q18. While checking SB-26 receipts at a Branch Office, the inspecting officer must ensure that the PR Book (SB-26 receipt books) are maintained in:
   A. Duplicate
   B. Triplicate
   C. Quadruplicate
   D. Single copy
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: While checking all SB-26 issued since the last inspection, the officer must ensure that PR Books (i.e., SB-26 receipt books) are maintained in triplicate, with original receipts pasted along with office copies.

Q19. After the close of which date of every financial year must the BPM collect all SB and SSA passbooks (irrespective of presentation for transaction) and send them to the Account Office for entry of interest, as per Rule 49(6) of the POSB (CBS) Manual?
   A. 31st December
   B. 31st January
   C. 31st March
   D. 30th June
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: As per Rule 49(6) of the POSB (CBS) Manual referred to in Q.38, after 31st March of every financial year the BPM must collect all SB and SSA passbooks and send them to the Account Office for entry of interest, issuing SB-28 receipts to depositors.

Q20. Consider the following regarding Section E (Savings Bank) checks in the BO Inspection Questionnaire: 1. Collection of all TD passbooks since DLI stands deleted in the 2020 revision. 2. Five passbooks of RD discontinued accounts are to be collected and verified. 3. SB-28 depositor's receipts are issued for reasons other than interest posting. Which of the above is/are correct?
   A. 1 and 2 only
   B. 2 and 3 only
   C. 1 and 3 only
   D. 1, 2 and 3
   Correct Answer: D
   Explanation: All three are correct: Q.41(i) (collection of all TD passbooks since DLI) was deleted in the 2020 revision, Q.41(ii) requires collecting five RD discontinued account passbooks, and Q.37(i) deals with SB-28 receipts issued for reasons other than interest posting.

Q21. The Supplementary Questions on Financial Services require the GDS BO to maintain a manual cheque register following the procedure prescribed in which SB Orders?
   A. SB Order 22/2020 and SB Order 27/2020
   B. SB Order 18/2020 and SB Order 23/2020
   C. SB Order 22/2020 and SB Order 25/2021
   D. SB Order 27/2020 and SB Order 36/2021
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Supplementary Q.1 (added vide Letter No. 16-02/2017-Inspn. dated 24.09.2020) requires the BO to maintain a manual cheque register as per SB Order 22/2020 dated 18.06.2020 and SB Order 27/2020 dated 23.07.2020.

Q22. Under Section F (PLI/RPLI), what is the minimum number of Premium Receipt Books (PRBs) to be collected for two days of a month to compare credit entries?
   A. Three
   B. Two
   C. Ten
   D. Five
   Correct Answer: D
   Explanation: Q.49.11 requires collecting a few PRBs, minimum five, for two days of a month, and comparing the credit entries with the Day Collection Report and Cash Book Report to ensure correct accounting.

Q23. For checking PLI/RPLI Day End and Summary Reports, the inspection covers 10 days since the last inspection. How are these 10 days to be distributed across the four quarters?
   A. 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 days
   B. 2 + 3 + 3 + 2 days
   C. 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 days
   D. 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 days
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Q.49.10 specifies that the 10 days are to be checked taking 3 days in the first quarter, 3 days in the second quarter, and 2 days each in the third and fourth quarters.

Q24. A distinguishing operational feature of India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) transactions at a Branch Office, as noted in Section J, is that they are:
   A. Recorded through physical transaction vouchers retained at the BO
   B. Entered in a manual log of transactions maintained by the end-user
   C. Completely online with biometric authentication and no physical signatures or passbooks
   D. Authenticated only through the DARPAN booking application
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Section J notes that IPPB operations are completely online with biometric authentication without physical signatures, so there are no transaction vouchers, passbooks or transaction logs at the end-user/post office level; the inspecting officer accesses the IPPB system log in real time.

Q25. In the inspection scoring (Section K), the maximum weightage of 40 marks out of 100 is allotted to which parameter?
   A. Business Development and attitude of BPM
   B. Service Standards (delivery, booking, MO, SB, Insurance)
   C. IPPB (micro-ATM/Accounts Processing/Accounts Servicing)
   D. Use of RICT Application (CBS/CIS/Delivery App/Booking App)
   Correct Answer: D
   Explanation: Q.59 awards a maximum of 40 marks to the Use of RICT Application (CBS/CIS/Delivery App/Booking App), the highest single weightage, reflecting the Department's emphasis on technology adoption. Business Development and Service Standards carry 20 each, IPPB 10, and Records and Hardware 5 each.

Q26. Under the Sub Office Inspection Questionnaire framework, the Supplementary Questions for Sub Post Offices (S1–S18) were introduced vide which Directorate communication?
   A. File No. 16-02/2017-Inspn. dated 24.09.2020

   C. File No. 29-1/85-Inspn. dated 13.05.1986
   D. File No. 27-1/77-Inspn. dated 01.12.1977
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: The 18 Supplementary Questions for Sub Post Offices were added vide Directorate File No. 16-02/2017-Inspn. dated 24.09.2020 and came into force with immediate effect from that date.

Q27. As per the prescribed inspection time schedule, the number of days allowed for the Annual Inspection of an HSG I/HSG II Sub Post Office is:
   A. 2 days
   B. 3 days
   C. 4 days
   D. 8 days
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Sub Post Offices in HSG I and HSG II are allowed 4 days for Annual Inspection. LSG offices get 3 days, Time Scale offices 2 days, and single-handed non-delivery SPOs / Branch Offices 1 day.

Q28. According to the prescribed quarterly time schedule for inspections, the maximum proportion of inspections is to be completed in which quarter?
   A. 2nd Quarter (April–June)
   B. 1st Quarter (January–March)
   C. 3rd Quarter (July–September)
   D. 4th Quarter (October–December)
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: The prescribed distribution is 15% (Q1), 35% (Q2), 30% (Q3) and 20% (Q4); the 2nd Quarter (April–June) carries the highest share at 35%.

Q29. Where the duration of an inspection is 2 days and below 8 days, the Inspection Report (IR) must be issued within how many days from commencement?
   A. 15 days
   B. 30 days
   C. 45 days
   D. 60 days
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: Per Directorate Order No. 17-01/2018-Inspn. dt. 05.10.2018, the IR issue deadline is 15 days for a 1-day inspection, 30 days for 2 days and below 8 days, and 45 days for inspections of 8 days and above.

Q30. The complete cycle of an inspection, including compliance, should be completed within what period from the date of commencement?
   A. 3 months
   B. 4 months
   C. 4½ months
   D. 6 months
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: The questionnaire mandates that the full inspection cycle, including compliance, be completed within 4½ months from the date of commencement.

Q31. For an inspection conducted by an Inspector of Posts (IP), the Inspection Report is to be reviewed by the next higher authority within how many days?
   A. 30 days
   B. 45 days
   C. 60 days
   D. 90 days
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: An IP's inspection report is reviewed by the next higher authority (ASP(Dn)/ASP(HQ)/Dy. Suptd./Divisional Head) within 60 days.

Q32. After review intimation, the compliance period prescribed for offices other than HO/GPO is:
   A. 15 days
   B. 30 days
   C. 45 days
   D. 60 days
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: The compliance period is 45 days for HO/GPO and 30 days for all other offices, reckoned from the date of review intimation.

Q33. Inspections mandated for more than one day are required to be:
   A. Completed in one spell and not staggered over days
   B. Split equally across two convenient spells
   C. Conducted only during the first quarter of the year
   D. Staggered so as not to disrupt counter operations
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: The questionnaire specifically directs that inspections of more than one day be completed in ONE SPELL and not staggered over days.

Q34. Records relating to the Half Yearly Enumeration of Unregistered Postal Articles are conducted in which two months each year, with records maintained for the latest how many years?
   A. August and February; latest 3 years
   B. June and December; latest 3 years
   C. August and February; latest 5 years
   D. April and October; latest 2 years
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: Q.15(d) requires that records for the Half Yearly Enumeration of Unregistered Postal Articles (held in August and February each year) be maintained for the latest 3 years.

Q35. Under the International Money Transfer Service (IMTS), the maximum number of transactions that may be paid to a single beneficiary in a calendar year is:
   A. 12
   B. 30
   C. 50
   D. Unlimited subject to KYC
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: As per RBI guidelines applied in IMTS checks, a maximum of 30 transactions per beneficiary is permitted in a calendar year.

Q36. In IMTS, an amount exceeding Rs. 50,000 paid to a beneficiary must be disbursed:
   A. Only in cash after enhanced KYC
   B. By cheque or by direct credit to the SB account
   C. Only through an India Post Payments Bank account
   D. By demand draft drawn on the Head Office
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: For IMTS, amounts exceeding Rs. 50,000 are required to be paid by cheque or by direct credit to the beneficiary's SB account, not in cash.

Q37. For the India Post Payments Bank (IPPB), the inspecting officer is required to randomly check accounts where the end-of-day balance exceeds:
   A. Rs. 25,000
   B. Rs. 50,000
   C. Rs. 1 lakh
   D. Rs. 2 lakh
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: An IPPB account balance should not exceed Rs. 1 lakh at the end of the day; accounts breaching this limit must be randomly checked during inspection [Q.46(C)(n)].

Q38. After GL Integration, the inspecting officer verifies that the POS Cash Balance in CSI is zero using which transaction code?
   A. ZFSI_ERROR
   B. FAGLL03
   C. HACLINQ
   D. zfi_receipt
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: Tcode FAGLL03 (General Ledger Line Items Report) is used to confirm that the POS Cash Balance is 0 in CSI from the last DLI; ZFSI_ERROR is the report for entries not posted automatically.

Q39. During inspection, the office account bearing SOL ID + 0382 (used for new account opening and subsequent deposits) must show what balance at End of Day?
   A. A credit balance equal to the day's deposits
   B. Always a debit (Dr.) balance
   C. Zero
   D. A balance not exceeding Rs. 1 lakh
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: Per Supplementary Question S3, the office account SOL ID + 0382 used for new account opening and subsequent deposits must be ZERO at the time of EoD.

Q40. The ATM Cash Account, identified by SOL ID + 0006, should always reflect which balance, and is checked through which Finacle menu?
   A. Debit (Dr.) balance; HACLINQ menu
   B. Credit (Cr.) balance; CCMM menu
   C. Zero balance; HAFI menu
   D. Debit (Dr.) balance; CDEDUP menu
   Correct Answer: A
   Explanation: The ATM cash account (SOL ID + 0006) is treated as cash of the SOL and should always be in Debit (Dr.) balance; it is verified through the HACLINQ menu [Q.60(b)].

Q41. During inspection of a CBS Post Office, the inspecting officer should verify at least how many RD loan accounts opened since the last inspection?
   A. 3
   B. 5
   C. 10
   D. All RD loan accounts irrespective of number
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: Q.52(c), added in 2022, requires verification of at least five RD loan accounts opened since the last inspection; if fewer than five exist, all are to be verified.

Q42. Procedures for transfer of accounts, issue of duplicate passbooks, and revival of silent accounts where the office lacks two supervisors are governed by which reference?

Q43. Consider the following statements regarding savings-bank thresholds in the SO inspection: 1. In iMO, Customer Due Diligence is required if the amount exceeds Rs. 50,000 in a month. 2. For high-value PLI/RPLI policies above Rs. 20 lakhs, all financial underwriting entries must be checked. 3. Q48. For the Bill Mail Service, the minimum number of articles required to be posted at one time is:

Q49. While verifying cash and stamp balances, the inspecting officer generates the daily account and the DOP/POS cash balance report. Which transaction codes are used, and what should the POS cash balance be at end of day?

Q50. Complaints pending against the India Post Call Centre are reviewed during grievance inspection. The toll-free number of the India Post Call Centre is:
