── Sub-Section 1.1: TRCA Wage Scales and Fixation · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q1–Q5 ──

Q1. What is the revised minimum Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA) for a Branch Postmaster (BPM) working for 5 Hours (Level 2)?
A. Rs. 10,000/-
B. Rs. 12,000/-
C. Rs. 14,500/-
D. Rs. 16,000/-
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The revised minimum TRCA for a BPM for 5 Hours/Level 2 is Rs. 14,500/-.



Q2. Under the revised TRCA matrix, Wage Scale-2 corresponds to which of the following categories of Gramin Dak Sevaks?
A. ABPM/Dak Sevak (Level-1) only
B. ABPM/Dak Sevak (Level-2) and BPM (Level-1)
C. BPM (Level-2) only
D. ABPM/Dak Sevak (Level-1) and BPM (Level-1)
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Wage Scale-2 corresponds to ABPM/Dak Sevak (Level-2) and BPM (Level-1), both of which share a minimum TRCA of Rs. 12,000/-.



Q3. Which of the following statements regarding the annual increase in TRCA is/are correct?

1. The annual increase of TRCA is fixed at 3%.
2. A GDS is entitled to an annual increase on both 1st July and 1st January of every year.
3. For GDS engaged on or after 01.07.2018, initial fixation is done on the first stage of Level 1 of the respective category.
A. 1 only
B. 1 and 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The annual increase of TRCA is 3%.


* Initial fixation for GDS engaged on or after 01.07.2018 is done on the first stage of Level 1 of the respective category.


* There are two dates of annual increase (1st July and 1st January), but a GDS is entitled to only one annual increase on either of these dates depending on the date of engagement.



Q4. What is the prescribed multiplication factor used for the fixation formula of the revised TRCA?
A. 2.50
B. 2.57
C. 2.65
D. 2.81
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The fixation formula for the revised TRCA involves multiplying by a factor of 2.57.



Q5. The revised TRCA matrix for all Gramin Dak Sevaks, along with the revised rates of other allowances, came into effect from which date?
A. 01.01.2016
B. 01.01.2018
C. 01.07.2018
D. 01.10.2018
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The revised TRCA as per the matrix and the revised rates of other allowances took effect from 01.07.2018.

── Sub-Section 2.1: Allowances Matrix · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q9–Q14 ──

Q9. What is the Office Maintenance Allowance (OMA) payable to a Branch Postmaster for maintaining standard accommodation?
A. Rs. 250/- per month
B. Rs. 500/- per month
C. Rs. 750/- per month
D. Rs. 1000/- per month
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The Office Maintenance Allowance for standard accommodation is Rs. 500/- per month.


* For non-standard accommodation, the rate is Rs. 250/- per month.



Q10. Which of the following allowances is payable on an incident basis and is NOT structured as a fixed monthly allowance?
A. Fixed Stationary Charge
B. Cycle Maintenance Allowance
C. Cash Conveyance Allowance
D. Risk & Hardship Allowance
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Cash Conveyance Allowance is not a fixed allowance and is payable on an incident basis.



Q11. A Gramin Dak Sevak conveys cash of Rs. 1,50,000. What is the allowable Cash Conveyance Allowance for this incident, excluding actual public bus fare?
A. Rs. 30/-
B. Rs. 50/-
C. Rs. 115/-
D. Rs. 180/-
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* For cash conveyance above 1 Lakh, the allowance is Rs. 50/- plus actual conveyance charges subject to the maximum fare of a public bus.


* For amounts up to 1 Lakh, the rate is Rs. 30/- plus actual conveyance.



Q12. An Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) is assigned to perform the duties of a BPM. What is the Combined Duty Allowance payable per day for this work?
A. Rs. 45/- per day
B. Rs. 75/- per day
C. Rs. 90/- per day
D. Rs. 115/- per day
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* An ABPM performing BPM work is entitled to a Combined Duty Allowance of Rs. 75/- per day, subject to a maximum of Rs. 1950/- per month.



Q13. Consider the following statements regarding the Combined Duty Allowance (CDA):

* An ABPM/GDS doing additional work of another ABPM/GDS receives Rs. 45/- per day (max Rs. 1170/month).


* A BPM performing Delivery or Mail Conveyance receives Rs. 45/- per day (max Rs. 1170/month).



Q14. What is the fixed monthly rate for the Cycle Maintenance Allowance granted to Gramin Dak Sevaks?
A. Rs. 115/-
B. Rs. 180/-

── Sub-Section 3.1 & 3.2: CEFA Conditions, Amounts, and Limits · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q17–Q22 ──

Q17. What is the fixed annual amount of reimbursement under the Children Education Facilitation Allowance (CEFA) per eligible child for a Gramin Dak Sevak?
A. Rs. 4,000/-
B. Rs. 5,000/-
C. Rs. 6,000/-
D. Rs. 7,000/-
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The amount of reimbursement of CEFA is Rs. 6,000/- per annum (fixed) per child.


* This amount is fixed irrespective of the actual expenses incurred by the GDS.



Q18. What are the respective upper age limits for claiming CEFA for a Divyaang child and a normal child?
A. 20 years for Divyaang children; 18 years for other children
B. 22 years for Divyaang children; 20 years or till passing 12th class (whichever is earlier) for other children
C. 25 years for Divyaang children; 21 years for other children
D. No upper age limit for Divyaang children; 20 years for other children
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The upper age limit for Divyaang children has been set at 22 years.


* For other children, the age limit will be 20 years or till the time of passing 12th class whichever is earlier.


* There shall be no minimum age.



Q19. A Gramin Dak Sevak claims CEFA for a child who subsequently fails in the 9th standard. For the next academic year, the child is admitted to the 9th standard in a *different* school. What is the status of the CEFA reimbursement?
A. The allowance will continue to be paid at 50% of the normal rate.
B. The allowance will be reimbursed as usual because failure does not stop the benefit.
C. The allowance shall not be reimbursable for that year.
D. The allowance will be held in abeyance until the child passes the 9th standard.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The reimbursement has no nexus with the performance of the child; however, if the child fails and is admitted in the same class in *another* school, CEFA shall not be reimbursable.



Q20. Under which of the following conditions can CEFA be claimed for a diploma/certificate course at a Polytechnic or ITI?
A. For the full three years of the diploma course, provided it is taken after the 10th standard.
B. For the initial two years of the course, if pursued after the 10th standard and no CEFA was granted for the 11th and 12th standards.
C. CEFA is strictly limited to classes up to the 12th standard and cannot be claimed for ITI courses.
D. For the final year of the diploma course only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* CEFA is admissible for the initial two years of a diploma/certificate course from Polytechnic/ITI/Engineering College if the child pursues the course after passing 10th standard and the GDS has not been granted CEFA for studies in 11th and 12th standards.



Q21. During which of the following duty statuses is a Gramin Dak Sevak NOT eligible to accrue/receive the Children Education Facilitation Allowance?
A. While on paid leave
B. While under put off duty
C. During any period treated as 'non counted for duty'
D. While actively performing regular duty
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* CEFA is admissible to a GDS while he/she is on duty, under put off duty, or on leave, provided that during any period which is treated as 'non counted for duty', the GDS shall not be eligible for the allowance.



Q22. Consider the following statements regarding the Children Education Facilitation Allowance (CEFA):

1. CEFA is allowed for children studying through "Correspondence or Distance Learning".
2. The allowance strictly covers studies from class nursery to the twelfth standard.
3. Reimbursement can only be claimed for the two eldest surviving children, with no exceptions whatsoever.
Which of the above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

* CEFA is applicable for children from class nursery to twelfth.


* It is allowed for children studying through correspondence or distance learning.


* While it is generally for the two eldest surviving children, exceptions exist for twin/multiple births from a second childbirth and for children born out of the failure of a sterilization operation.

── Sub-Section 4.1 & 4.2: Emergency Leave and Paid Leave · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q23–Q26 ──

Q23. What is the maximum number of days of Emergency Leave that can be granted to a GDS in a completed calendar year?
A. 2 days
B. 5 days
C. 10 days
D. 20 days
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Emergency leave will be granted for a maximum of 5 days in a completed calendar year of the engagement period.



Q24. A Branch Postmaster (BPM) requires Emergency Leave. Who is the competent authority to sanction this leave prior to the BPM proceeding on leave?
A. Sub Divisional Head
B. Inspector Posts
C. Divisional Head
D. Regional PMG
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Prior sanction for Emergency Leave for a BPM is required from the Divisional Head.


* For an ABPM/GDS, the Sub Division Head/Sr. PM/Unit Head is the sanctioning authority.



Q25. An ABPM stays for 3 continuous days on emergency leave at one time. How will this period be treated under the GDS leave rules?
A. The third day will be treated as Leave Without Allowance (LWA).
B. All 3 days will be debited against his/her paid leave account due.
C. The third day will be treated as unauthorized absence unconditionally.
D. It will be sanctioned as a special case by the Sub Divisional Head.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* When a GDS stays beyond two days of emergency leave at a time, the whole period shall be debited against his/her paid leave account due.


* In case he/she is not having paid leave, the excess period is treated as unauthorized absence and the GDS shall not be entitled to any TRCA.



Q26. Which of the following statements regarding the calculation and combination of Emergency Leave is correct?
A. Emergency leave can be seamlessly combined with paid leave.
B. Half-day emergency leave may be granted in urgent situations.
C. Sundays and Postal holidays falling during the period of emergency leave are not counted as part of the leave.
D. Unused emergency leave can be carried forward or encashed in the next calendar year.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Sundays and Postal holidays falling during the period of emergency leave are not counted as part of emergency leave.


* Emergency leave cannot be carried forward, encashed, combined with paid leave, or granted for a half day.

── Sub-Section 5.1: Eligibility and Transfer Conditions · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q31–Q36 ──

Q31. What is the minimum continuous engagement period required for a Gramin Dak Sevak to become eligible to seek a transfer under the Limited Transfer Facility (LTF)?
A. One year from the date of regular engagement
B. Two years from the date of regular engagement
C. Three years from the date of regular engagement
D. Five years from the date of regular engagement
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The minimum engagement condition for transfer of a GDS under LTF is two years of continuous engagement from the date of regular engagement.



Q32. Under the Mutual Exchange facility, what is the minimum period of continuous engagement required for all GDS from the date of regular engagement?
A. Six months
B. One year
C. Two years
D. Three years
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The Mutual Exchange facility can be provided to all GDS on completion of 1 (one) year from the date of regular engagement.



Q33. What is the maximum number of chances provided to a female GDS to avail the Limited Transfer Facility during her entire career?
A. One chance
B. Two chances
C. Three chances
D. Unlimited chances
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* A female GDS will get three chances under the Limited Transfer Facility during her entire career.


* A male GDS is provided two chances during his entire career.



Q34. If a Gramin Dak Sevak applies for a transfer but withdraws the application within the available window period, how is this action treated regarding their transfer chances?
A. It is counted as one chance availed under the facility.
B. It is counted as half a chance availed.
C. The chance shall not be counted as a chance availed under the facility.
D. The GDS is permanently barred from applying for LTF again.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* If an application for transfer is withdrawn by the GDS within the window period available, the chance shall not be counted as a chance availed under the Limited Transfer Facility.


* Conversely, refusal to accept the issued Limited Transfer Order shall be counted as one chance availed.



Q35. A GDS completes their first transfer and immediately applies for a second transfer under the LTF. What is the minimum required 'cooling off' period between two successive transfers?
A. Six months of continuous engagement
B. One year of continuous engagement
C. Two years of continuous engagement
D. Three years of continuous engagement
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* There is a mandatory 'cooling off' period of one year of continuous engagement required between two successive transfers.



Q36. Consider the following statements regarding the conditions of transfer for a GDS:

1. Past engagement period is counted for assessing eligibility for departmental examinations.
2. A GDS retains the right to claim a return to their previous recruitment Division if they are dissatisfied with the new posting.
3. Transfer requests from GDS against whom a police case is pending will not be entertained.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 1 and 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Past engagement period is counted for assessing eligibility for departmental examinations and annual increments.


* Transfer requests of GDS who are under put off duty, or against whom disciplinary action, a police case, or a court case is pending will not be entertained.


* A GDS will have no claim to go back to the previous engagement/recruitment Unit/Division under any circumstances.



── Sub-Section 5.2: Competent Authorities and Seniority · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q37–Q41 ──

Q37. Who is the Competent Authority to approve a transfer request from a GDS seeking to transfer outside their Division but within the same Region?
A. Divisional Head
B. Regional Head (PMG)
C. Circle Head (CPMG)
D. Director General Postal Services
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* For a transfer requested outside the Division but within the same Region, the Competent Authority is the Regional Head (PMG).



Q38. A GDS applies for a transfer to a Division located completely outside their current Circle. Who is the Competent Authority to approve this request?
A. The Regional Head (PMG) of the destination region
B. The Circle Head (CPMG) of the parent circle only
C. The Circle Heads of both the parent and destination Circles
D. The Divisional Heads of both divisions
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* For a transfer outside the Circle, the request must be approved by the Circle Head of both the Circles.



Q39. When a GDS is transferred at their own request to a new Sub Division (different from their recruitment Sub Division), how is their seniority determined in the new unit?
A. They carry forward their past seniority and rank above all newly recruited GDS.
B. They will rank junior in the seniority list of the new unit to all GDS of that unit existing on the date the transfer is ordered.
C. Their seniority is determined by a proportional formula based on past service years.
D. They will rank senior to all GDS in the new unit if their TRCA slab is higher.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* When a GDS is transferred at his own request, he/she will rank junior in the seniority list of the new unit to all the GDS of that unit who exist in the seniority list on the date the transfer is ordered.


* The only exception is in case of a transfer within the same engagement/recruitment Sub Division/Unit/Division.



Q40. What is the exception to the rule that a transferred GDS ranks junior to all existing GDS in the new unit?
A. If the GDS is transferred outside the Circle, they retain seniority.
B. If the transfer is ordered on administrative grounds.
C. If the transfer occurs strictly within the same engagement/recruitment Sub Division/Unit/Division.
D. If the GDS has more than 15 years of continuous service.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* A transferred GDS ranks junior in the new unit, *except* in the case of transfer within the same engagement/recruitment Sub Division/Unit/Division.



Q41. If two or more GDS are transferred to the same Sub Division/Division in the same transfer cycle, how is their inter-se seniority decided?
A. Based on the distance of their transfer
B. Based on their TRCA level
C. Based on their date of joining in the Department
D. Based on their date of birth
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The inter-se seniority of two or more GDS transferred to the same Sub Division/Division in the same cycle is decided on the basis of their date of joining in the Department (i.e., the one joining earlier ranks senior).

── Sub-Section 5.5: Transfer Committees and Process · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q47–Q48 ──

Q47. A committee is formed to consider the transfer of a GDS within a Division. Which of the following correctly describes the composition of this committee?

* The committee for transfer within the Division comprises the Head of Division, ASP (HQ)/Office Superintendent of Divisional office, and the concerned Sub Divisional heads of both the Sub Division where the GDS is working and where he/she is being transferred.



Q48. Even when a Transfer and Placement Committee recommends a transfer of a GDS *within a Division or subdivision*, the Divisional Head is prohibited from issuing the transfer order until they receive prior approval from which authority?
