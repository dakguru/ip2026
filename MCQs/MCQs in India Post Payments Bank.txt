### ANSWER KEY & EXPLANATIONS

**Q1. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** IPPB was launched as a pilot project on 30 January 2017 in **Ranchi (Jharkhand) and Raipur (Chhattisgarh)**.
* **Elimination:** Varanasi, Patna, Hyderabad, etc., are incorrect distractors not mentioned as pilot cities.

**Q2. Correct Answer: C**

* **Concept:** IPPB was set up under the Department of Posts with **100% equity owned by the Government of India**.

**Q3. Correct Answer: A**

* **Concept:** **Fincluvation** is the specific initiative by DoP & IPPB to invite fintech startups to co-create financial inclusion solutions.

**Q4. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** For the Premium Aarogya Savings Account, the annual renewal subscription fee for existing customers is **₹99 + GST**. (New account opening is ₹149 + GST).

**Q5. Correct Answer: A**

* **Concept:** The interest rate slab is **2.00%** for balances up to ₹1 Lakh and **2.25%** for balances above ₹1 Lakh and up to ₹2 Lakh.

**Q6. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** The tagline printed on the document is **"(Aapka Bank, Aapke Dwaar)"**.

**Q7. Correct Answer: C**

* **Concept:** For customers, the maximum capping of SMS charges per quarter is **₹100 + GST**. (For merchants, it is ₹50 + GST effective Oct 1, 2024).

**Q8. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** The Basic Savings Account offers **4 free withdrawals** per month. Post the free limit, charges of 0.50% (Min ₹25) apply.

**Q9. Correct Answer: D**

* **Concept:** All three statements are correct. DigiSmart is app-based, opens in a negative lien of ₹149+GST, and has **No Monthly Average Balance** requirement.

**Q10. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** For new UPI users, there is a cooling period limit where a maximum of ₹5,000 worth of transactions can be done in the **first 24 hours**.

**Q11. Correct Answer: C**

* **Concept:** Once a Premium (SBPRM) account is downgraded, it is eligible for an upgrade only after a **180-day cooling period**.

**Q12. Correct Answer: C**

* **Concept:** IPPB offers International Money Transfer services in partnership with **Ria Money Transfer**.

**Q13. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** For a Regular Savings Account, the Virtual Debit Card issuance charge is **₹25 (inclusive of taxes)**.

**Q14. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** Doorstep Banking charges are **Flat ₹20 + GST** per visit per customer serviced beyond 1 km from the Post Office.

**Q15. Correct Answer: B**

* **Concept:** The cumulative transaction limit for sweep-in from a linked POSA is **₹50,000** for the first 3 days post successful linkage.

**Q16. Correct Answer: A**
