Q24. Which of the following statements regarding the Post Office Act, 2023 is correct?

A. It received Presidential assent on 18th June 2024.
B. It was notified after Presidential assent on 24th December 2023.
C. It came into force on 17th June 2024.
D. It repealed the Indian Post Office Act, 1854.

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
• The Post Office Act, 2023 (Act 43 of 2023) was notified on 24th December 2023 after Presidential assent. It came into force on 18th June 2024 (notified vide S.O. 2352(E) dated 17th June 2024), repealing the Indian Post Office Act, 1898.
