── Sub-Section 1.1 & 1.2: Background, Legislative Journey & Crucial Dates · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q1–Q5 ──

Q1. The separate Schedule of Financial Powers for the Department of Posts is currently delegated under which of the following statutory provisions?
A. Rule 12(3) of the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 1978
B. Rule 12(2) of the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 2024
C. Rule 13 of the General Financial Rules, 2017
D. Rule 12(2) of the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 1978
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The current delegation of financial powers for the Department of Posts is exercised under Rule 12(2) of the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules (DFPR), 2024.


* The revised schedules under this rule were notified on 28.03.2025.



Q2. Consider the following events regarding the delegation of financial powers in the Department of Posts:

1. Bifurcation of P&T into DoP and DoT.
2. Department of Posts brought under DFPR, 1978.
3. Commencement of DFPR, 2024.
4. Notification of the revised Schedules II-A, II-B, II-C, and III.
Which of the following is the correct chronological sequence of the above events?
A. 1 - 2 - 3 - 4
B. 2 - 1 - 3 - 4
C. 1 - 3 - 2 - 4
D. 2 - 3 - 1 - 4
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

* The bifurcation of P&T occurred first in January 1985.


* The DoP was subsequently brought under DFPR, 1978 on 01.04.1988.


* The DFPR 2024 came into force on 01.04.2024.


* Finally, the revised DoP Schedules were notified on 28.03.2025.



Q3. The revised Schedules of Financial Powers for the Department of Posts, notified on 28.03.2025 under DFPR 2024, officially came into effect on:
A. 01.04.2024
B. 22.05.2025
C. 01.04.2025
D. 09.06.2026
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* While the DFPR 2024 itself came into force on 01.04.2024, the revised schedules specific to DoP were notified on 28.03.2025 and became effective on 01.04.2025.



Q4. Who is the approving authority for the delegation of financial powers to various officers in the Department of Posts under Rule 12(2) of DFPR, 2024?
A. Minister of Communications in consultation with the Secretary (Posts)
B. Secretary (Posts) in consultation with the Additional Secretary & Financial Advisor (AS&FA)
C. Director General Postal Services in consultation with the Member (Finance)
D. Ministry of Finance (Department of Expenditure) directly
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The approving authority for the current delegation under Rule 12(2) of DFPR, 2024 is the Secretary (Posts) in consultation with the AS&FA.



Q5. Which of the following Addendums introduced the most recent revisions to the DoP Schedule of Financial Powers, taking effect in June 2026?
A. Addendum I
B. Addendum II
C. Addendum III
D. Addendum IV
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Addendum III is the latest revision, dated 09.06.2026, which incorporated fresh changes into the base OM and earlier addendums.



── Sub-Section 1.3: Four Schedules under DFPR 2024 · 🟡 HIGH · MCQs Q6–Q7 ──

Q6. Under the revised DFPR 2024 structure for the Department of Posts, the financial powers delegated to the Heads of Postal Divisions and Heads of RMS Divisions are contained in:
A. Schedule II-A
B. Schedule II-B
C. Schedule II-C
D. Schedule III
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

* Schedule III covers the financial powers delegated to Heads of Postal Divisions, RMS Divisions, Chief PM (GPO), Supdt. PSDs, and Sr. PM.


* Schedule II-A is for HOC & HOD.



Q7. Which of the following officers exercises financial powers strictly under Schedule II-C of the DFPR 2024 for the Department of Posts?
A. Director, RAKNPA
B. General Manager (Finance)
C. Chief Postmaster General (CPMG)
D. Director (Postal Services) having Independent Charge
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Schedule II-C is designated for the Sr. DDG (PAF), General Manager (Finance), and Directors of Accounts (Postal).


* Director RAKNPA and CPMG fall under Schedule II-A.


* Directors (Postal Services) with Independent Charge fall under Schedule II-B.

── Sub-Section 2.1 & 2.2: Definition of HOD and Equivalent Powers · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q8–Q10 ──

Q8. As per Addendum III to the Schedule of Financial Powers, which of the following designations was newly added to the official definition of "Head of Department" (HOD)?
A. Director (Foreign Post)
B. General Manager (CEPT)
C. Director Postal Services (HQ)
D. Chief General Manager (PLI)
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Addendum III explicitly added "Director Postal Services (HQ)" to the Head of Department definition with effect from 09.06.2026.


* The other listed officers were already included in the base definition of HOD.



Q9. Which of the following officers is NOT included in the definition of "Head of Department" (HOD) for the purpose of exercising powers under Schedule II-A?
A. Director, Rafi Ahmed Kidwai National Postal Academy (RAKNPA)
B. General Manager (CEPT)
C. Chief Engineer
D. Director of Accounts (Postal) heading a PAO
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

* A Director-level officer heading a Postal Accounts Office (PAO) is treated as a Head of Office and exercises specific powers delegated under Schedule II-C to establish uniformity.


* Director (RAKNPA), GM (CEPT), and Chief Engineers are explicitly classified as HODs under Schedule II-A.



Q10. The In-charge of the Foreign Post Office exercises delegated financial powers equivalent to which of the following authorities?
A. Schedule II-A (HOD) exclusively
B. Schedule III (Divisional Head) exclusively
C. Equivalent to HOD, Independent Director, or Divisional Head, depending on the specific case
D. Equivalent to the Head of Circle (HOC) only
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Note 3 of the definitions specifies that the In-charge of Foreign Post will exercise the delegated financial powers equivalent to HoD (Schedule II-A), Independent Director (Schedule II-B), or Divisional Head (Schedule III), as the case may be.



── Sub-Section 2.3: Items Exclusive to HOC & Special Delegatees · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q11–Q13 ──

Q11. Within Schedule II-A, certain financial powers are restricted and can be exercised ONLY by the Head of the Postal Circle (HOC). Which of the following powers is strictly reserved for the HOC and cannot be exercised by an ordinary Head of Department (HOD)?
A. Sanctioning ad-hoc payment of arrears of pay and allowances
B. Write-off of irrecoverable losses of stores or public money
C. Minor civil and electrical works in government buildings
D. Contingent expenditure (recurring) for office maintenance
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* According to Note 4, write-offs of revenue, stores, stock, and condemnation of motor vehicles (Items 34(i)–(v)) are powers exercised only by the Head of the Postal Circle (HOC).


* Ad-hoc payments of arrears and minor civil works have defined limits for both HOC and HOD.



Q12. Consider the following statements regarding the financial powers of the Director (RAKNPA) and Chief General Managers (PD/BD/PLI):

1. They can exercise the power to grant cash awards to Gramin Dak Sevaks (GDS) up to Rs. 25,000.
2. They are authorized to exercise specific HOC-only powers regarding the procurement of computers under schemes and laptops for eligible officers.
3. They are authorized to write off irrecoverable losses of public money.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Statement 2 is correct: Note 5 explicitly states that financial powers delegated to HOC ONLY under specific items, including 27(iii), 27(iv), and 27(v) (which cover ICT computers under schemes and laptops), shall ALSO be exercised by Director (RAKNPA) and CGM (PD/BD/PLI).


* Statement 1 is incorrect: Cash awards to GDS (Item 4(ii)) is an HOC-only power that is NOT extended to Director RAKNPA or CGMs.


* Statement 3 is incorrect: Write-offs (Item 34) are strictly HOC-only and not extended to these officers under Note 5.



Q13. Under the updated Schedule II-A (Addendum III), the power to procure a NEW Operational Vehicle purely as a replacement against a condemned operational vehicle is delegated exclusively to:
A. The Head of Circle (HOC) only
B. The Head of Circle (HOC) and the Director (RAKNPA) only
C. Any Head of Department (HOD)
D. The Head of Circle (HOC) and all Chief General Managers (CGMs)
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Addendum III clarified that the power for procurement of a new operational vehicle (Object Head 51, Item 25(i)) is delegated exclusively to the "HOC & Director (RAKNPA) only".

── Sub-Section 3.1: Five Restrictive Conditions · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q14–Q17 ──

Q14. An officer competent to sanction works up to Rs. 2 Lakhs wishes to approve a project estimated at Rs. 3.5 Lakhs. To avoid seeking the sanction of a higher authority, the officer divides the project into two distinct estimates of Rs. 1.75 Lakhs each. Under the Delegation of Financial Powers, this action is:
A. Permissible, provided both parts are executed within the same financial year.
B. Permissible, provided the IFA concurs with the splitting of the work.
C. Strictly prohibited, as no work requiring the sanction of higher authorities may be sanctioned by splitting it into distinct parts.
D. Strictly prohibited, unless the work is classified as a minor civil repair in a rented building.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The first restrictive condition explicitly states that no work requiring the sanction of higher authorities may be sanctioned by an officer by splitting it into two or more distinct parts.



Q15. When a financial contract extends over a multi-year period, how is the value determined for the purpose of applying the delegated financial limit of the sanctioning authority?
A. The average annual value of the contract is taken as the applying limit.
B. The total value over the entire currency of the contract shall be taken as the value for applying the limit.
C. Only the value of the first financial year is considered for applying the limit.
D. The value is calculated strictly on a month-to-month expenditure basis.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* The second restrictive condition mandates that if a contract extends over a period, the total value over its entire currency shall be taken as the value for applying the delegated limit.



Q16. Under Rule 12(3) of the DFPR 2024, where Gazetted Officers are authorised to exercise delegated financial powers, who retains the ultimate responsibility for the correctness, regularity, and propriety of their decisions?
A. The Head of Circle (HOC)
B. The Integrated Financial Advisor (IFA)
C. The Head of Department (HOD)
D. The Director General Postal Services (DGPS)
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* The fourth restrictive condition specifies that under Rule 12(3) DFPR 2024, where Gazetted Officers are authorised, the HoD remains responsible for the correctness, regularity, and propriety of their decisions.



Q17. Consider the following statements regarding the restrictive conditions governing the exercise of financial powers:

1. Limits apply not only to individual estimates but also to projects comprising one or more constituent estimates.
2. The total cost of the project, including all constituent estimates, determines the competence of the sanctioning authority.
3. All conditions and remarks attached to each item in the schedule are merely advisory and can be overridden by the HOC in emergent cases.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 1 and 2 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Statements 1 and 2 are correct as per the third restrictive condition, which states limits apply to projects comprising constituent estimates, and the total cost determines competence.


* Statement 3 is incorrect because the fifth condition states that all conditions/instructions/remarks attached to each item MUST be complied with.



── Sub-Section 3.2: IFA Consultation Rule for Divisional Heads · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q18–Q19 ──

Q18. A Head of a Postal Division (Divisional Head) wishes to incur an expenditure under Schedule III. The relevant government manual explicitly mandates IFA consultation for this specific type of expenditure. What is the correct procedural action for the Divisional Head?
A. The Divisional Head must consult the Divisional Accounts Officer.
B. The Divisional Head must refer the matter to the concerned HOD / HOC for IFA consultation.
C. The Divisional Head can waive the IFA consultation if the amount is below Rs. 2 Lakhs.
D. The Divisional Head must directly approach the Nodal Ministry for approval.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Para 6 states that for Divisional Heads, where rules or manuals explicitly mandate IFA consultation, the matter must be referred to the concerned HOD / HOC.


* Divisional Heads do not have direct IFA consultation channels for Schedule III items.



Q19. Before any financial case is forwarded to the AS&FA for the approval of the Secretary (Posts) or the Hon'ble Minister of Communications, it must first be:
A. Administratively cleared by the HOD in consultation with the Divisional IFA.
B. Administratively cleared by the HOC in consultation with the Circle IFA.
C. Cleared by the Director of Accounts (Postal) independently.
D. Vetted by the General Manager (CEPT).
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Para 7 mandates that all cases requiring approval of Secretary (Posts) / Hon'ble MoC shall first be cleared administratively by the HOC in consultation with Circle IFA, then forwarded via the concerned Member / DGPS.

── Sub-Section 4.1: OH 01 — Salaries & Ad-hoc Arrears · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q20–Q22 ──

Q20. As per the latest amendments (Addendum III), what is the maximum limit up to which a Head of Department (HOD) can sanction the ad-hoc payment of arrears of pay and allowances where connected records are destroyed?
A. Rs. 25,000/- per case
B. Rs. 50,000/- per case
C. Rs. 1,00,000/- per case
D. Rs. 5,00,000/- per case
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Object Head 01, Sl. No. 1(ii), the HOD may sanction ad-hoc payment of arrears not exceeding Rs. 50,000/- in each case.


* Rs. 1 Lakh is the limit for the HOC, while the overall headline extent is up to Rs. 5,00,000/- for older/investigative claims by HOC.



Q21. Which authority is competent to authorise the investigation of claims for arrears of pay and allowances that are MORE THAN SIX YEARS OLD, provided the connected records are not available in the Circle Accounts Office?
A. Head of Department (HOD) only
B. Head of Circle (HOC) up to the delegated financial powers
C. Director General Postal Services (DGPS) only
D. Senior Deputy Director General (PAF) only
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Object Head 01, Sl. No. 1(ii), Heads of Circles (HOC) are competent to authorise the investigation of claims for arrears of pay & allowances which are more than six years old, up to the delegated powers.



Q22. A competent authority wishes to sanction an honorarium to a government servant under Object Head 01. Beyond what financial limit in each case is consultation with the Integrated Financial Advisor (IFA) strictly required?
A. Rs. 1,000/-
B. Rs. 2,000/-
C. Rs. 2,500/-
D. Rs. 5,000/-
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 1(iv) Honorarium & Rewards to Government Servants, IFA consultation is required beyond Rs. 2,500/- in each case.

── Sub-Section 5.1: OH 09 & 13 — Training & Office Expenses · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q29–Q36 ──

Q29. A Head of Circle (HOC) wishes to sanction expenses for the domestic training of postal staff. What is the maximum extent of financial power delegated to the HOC in each case for domestic training expenses under Object Head 09?
A. Rs. 1 Lakh
B. Rs. 2 Lakhs
C. Rs. 5 Lakhs
D. Full Powers
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 7(i) Training Expenses, the power for domestic training is Rs. 2 Lakh in each case.



Q30. Under Object Head 13, what is the maximum per-annum ceiling prescribed for a Head of Department (HOD) for sanctioning NON-RECURRING contingent expenditure, as updated by Addendum III?
A. Rs. 3 Lakhs in each case, cap of Rs. 30 Lakhs per annum
B. Rs. 5 Lakhs in each case, cap of Rs. 50 Lakhs per annum
C. Rs. 1 Lakh in each case, cap of Rs. 10 Lakhs per annum
D. Rs. 10 Lakhs in each case, cap of Rs. 40 Lakhs per annum
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

* Under Sl. 10(ii), the limit for HOD for Non-Recurring contingent expenditure is Rs. 3 Lakh in each case, not exceeding Rs. 30 Lakhs per annum.


* The Rs. 5L/50L limit applies to the HOC.



Q31. Which of the following authorities has been specifically granted a power of up to Rs. 10 Lakhs in each case, not exceeding Rs. 40 Lakhs per annum, for the petty local purchase of articles of stationery?

B. Chief General Manager (PLI)
C. Secretary (Postal Services Board)
D. Director (RAKNPA)
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Addendum III added a specific row for Secretary (PSB) under Sl. 10(iv) Petty local purchases of stationery, granting powers up to Rs. 10 Lakhs in each case, not exceeding Rs. 40 Lakhs per annum.



Q32. For an item of office furniture to be classified as revenue expenditure and booked under Object Head 13 (Office Expenses) rather than Capital (Object Head 74), it must satisfy which of the following threshold conditions?
A. Cost must not exceed Rs. 50,000 OR 5 years of useful life.

Explanation:

* The rules under Object Head 13 specify that office equipment and furniture & fixtures NOT EXCEEDING the threshold of Rs. 1 Lakh OR 3 years of useful life, either of the two, are booked under revenue (OH-13).



Q33. The Chief Postmaster General of the Karnataka Postal Circle wishes to outsource services for Postal operations. According to Addendum II, what is the maximum financial limit per annum available to this specific HOC for such outsourcing?

* For the remaining HOCs, the limit is Rs. 10 Crores, and for an HOD, it is Rs. 5 Crores.

Q35. What is the maximum limit for HOC and HOD for sanctioning commission charges for the auction of stores on any occasion, as per the latest Addendum III?

Q36. For the payment of funeral expenses incidental to the death of a departmental employee on duty at an outstation, what is the maximum amount an HOC can sanction without requiring IFA consultation?

── Sub-Section 6.1: Capital Heads (OH 51, 52, 71, 72, 74) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q41–Q46 ──

Q41. A Head of Circle (HOC) requires the procurement of a new inspection vehicle. Under Object Head 51, which of the following is an essential prerequisite for exercising full financial powers for this purchase?
A. It must be procured strictly for a newly established regional office.
B. It must be a replacement against a formally condemned inspection vehicle.
C. It must have at least 40% Make-in-India (MII) components.
D. It requires prior approval of the Ministry of Finance in all cases.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Object Head 51, Sl. No. 25(ii), the HOC has full powers for the procurement of a NEW Inspection Vehicle ONLY for REPLACEMENT against a condemned inspection vehicle.



Q42. What is the maximum cost ceiling (exclusive of taxes) for the procurement of a laptop/notebook for an eligible officer on functional requirement by the HOC, provided the Make-in-India (MII) component of the device is more than 40%?
A. Rs. 1,00,000/-
B. Rs. 1,30,000/-
C. Rs. 1,50,000/-
D. Rs. 2,00,000/-
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 27(iv), the standard cost of a laptop is Rs. 1,00,000/- plus taxes. However, for devices where the Make-in-India (MII) component is >40%, the ceiling is increased to Rs. 1,30,000/- plus taxes.



Q43. As per Addendum III, consider the following statements regarding the procurement of Laptops specifically for "Office Use Only" (not for individual officers):

1. The Head of Circle (HOC) can sanction up to Rs. 50 Lakhs per annum.
2. The Head of Department (HOD) can sanction up to Rs. 25 Lakhs per annum.
3. The necessity and urgency of the functional requirement must be recorded by the sanctioning authority.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. All of the above
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 27(v), Addendum III raised the HOC limit to Rs. 50 Lakh per annum and added an HOD limit of Rs. 25 Lakh per annum.


* The rules also mandate that the urgency/necessity of the official requirement must be recorded by the HOC/HOD.



Q44. What is the maximum limit up to which a Head of Department (HOD) can sanction project estimates for the construction or reconstruction of Postal Buildings, as introduced by Addendum III?
A. Rs. 2 Crores
B. Rs. 5 Crores
C. Rs. 10 Crores
D. Rs. 20 Crores
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Addendum III explicitly introduced an HOD limit of up to Rs. 10 Crores for the construction/reconstruction of Postal Buildings or Staff Quarters under Sl. No. 28(i).


* The limit for HOC is Rs. 20 Crores.



Q45. For carrying out additions or alterations to an existing Heritage Building, which authority possesses the financial power to sanction the expenditure, and up to what limit?
A. HOD up to Rs. 5 Crores in each case
B. HOC up to Rs. 10 Crores in each case
C. HOC up to Rs. 20 Crores in each case
D. Both HOC and HOD up to Rs. 20 Crores in each case
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 28(ii) regarding Addition/Alteration to non-residential buildings, Addendum III specifies that in the case of heritage buildings, the power is "up to Rs. 20 Crores to HOC only," excluding overhead charges.



Q46. Which of the following conditions correctly applies to the sanctioning of project estimates for residential buildings by a Head of Circle?
A. The permissible variation in the plinth area due to structural considerations is up to 10%.
B. The limits laid down for electrical/sanitary works carried out departmentally are inclusive of the share of establishment and store-keeping charges.
C. The scale of accommodation must not exceed that prescribed for different classes of staff, allowing for a variation of up to 5% in plinth area.
D. The powers can be used to acquire land for any project estimate not previously sanctioned.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 28(i), restrictive condition (iv)(b) states that variation up to 5% is allowed in the plinth area due to structural considerations.


* Condition (i) strictly prohibits applying these powers to the acquisition of land.


* Note 2 specifies that the limit is EXCLUSIVE of the share of establishment and store-keeping charges.

Q47. Under the delegated powers for Write-offs (Object Head 64), what is the maximum limit per case up to which an HOC can write off the irrecoverable loss of stores or public money?
A. Rs. 5,000/-
B. Rs. 25,000/-
C. Rs. 50,000/-
D. Rs. 1,00,000/-
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 34(ii), the HOC can write off irrecoverable losses of Stores / Public Money up to Rs. 50,000/- in each case.


* The Rs. 5,000/- limit applies to the loss of Revenue (Sl. 34(i)).



Q48. A Head of Circle (HOC) intends to condemn a fleet of mail motor vehicles. Which of the following statements represents the correct exercise of this power?
A. The HOC has full powers for mature condemnation based purely on the distance run, without IFA consultation.
B. The HOC has full powers to scrap vehicles that have reached 15 years of age, but exclusively through a Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility (RVSF).
C. The HOC can condemn any vehicle exceeding 10 years of age directly through local MSTC auctions.
D. The HOC requires prior approval of the Directorate for condemning any vehicle exceeding 15 years.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 34(v), the HOC is delegated full powers to scrap vehicles which have reached 15 years of age through Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility (RVSF) only, established per MoRTH guidelines.


* IFA consultation is required for write-offs and condemnations.



Q49. As per Addendum III, what is the maximum financial power per contract delegated to a Head of Department (HOD) for the conveyance of mails by Road, including RTN contracts?
A. Rs. 1 Crore

D. Rs. 20 Crores
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 35(iii), Addendum III introduced an HOD limit of up to Rs. 1 Crore per contract for the conveyance of mails by Road/RTN.


* The limit for HOC is Rs. 20 Crores per contract.



Q50. In a situation of extreme emergency where the normal tender procedure is likely to cause administrative delay, an HOC wishes to sanction the conveyance of mails by private carriers without calling for tenders. What is the maximum permissible limit and period for this action?
A. Up to Rs. 2.5 Lakhs per month, for a period not exceeding 3 months

D. Up to Rs. 10 Lakhs per route, without any specific time limit
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 35(iv) Conveyance by Other Means, Addendum III amended the limit to Rs. 5 Lakh per month in each case for a period NOT EXCEEDING 3 MONTHS without calling tenders in emergency cases.



Q51. Consider the following scenarios regarding refunds of postage charges sanctioned by a competent authority:

1. Refund for unused but spoilt postage stamps is subject to a 6¼% reduction and is permitted only on complete sheets.
2. Refund for unused but spoilt postal stationery is subject to a 20% reduction and is permitted only on a complete ream.
3. Refund for franking machine impressions made in error is subject to a 5% deduction and the claim must be made within 6 months.
Which of the scenarios mentioned above is/are strictly correct according to the rules?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 1 and 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. All of the above
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

* Scenario 1 is correct: Spoilt postage stamps face a 6¼% reduction on complete sheets.


* Scenario 2 is correct: Spoilt postal stationery faces a 20% reduction on complete reams.


* Scenario 3 is incorrect: While the 5% deduction for franking machine errors is correct, the claim must be made within 3 MONTHS, not 6 months.



Q52. Under Addendum III, what is the maximum expenditure limit in each case that a Head of Circle (HOC) and a Head of Department (HOD) can sanction for purchases or contracts based on a Negotiated or Single Tender?
A. HOC: Rs. 10 Lakhs; HOD: Rs. 5 Lakhs
B. HOC: Rs. 20 Lakhs; HOD: Rs. 10 Lakhs
C. HOC: Rs. 50 Lakhs; HOD: Rs. 10 Lakhs
D. HOC: Rs. 1 Crore; HOD: Rs. 50 Lakhs
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 37, Addendum III delegates the power for negotiated/single tender purchases up to Rs. 50 Lakh in each case for the HOC, and explicitly adds an HOD limit of up to Rs. 10 Lakh in each case.

Q53. A Head of a Postal Division wishes to exercise financial powers under Schedule III for an item where the government guidelines explicitly mandate consultation with an Integrated Financial Advisor (IFA). According to Para 6 of the Delegation Order, what action must the Divisional Head take?
A. Directly consult the Circle IFA before issuing the sanction.
B. Request an ad-hoc IFA from the local Postal Accounts Office.
C. Sanction the expenditure without IFA consultation if it is below Rs. 2 Lakhs.
D. Refer the matter to the concerned HOD or HOC for IFA consultation.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

* Para 6 dictates that where government rules explicitly mandate IFA consultation, Divisional Heads must refer the matter to the concerned HOD / HOC.


* Divisional Heads do not have direct IFA consultation channels for Schedule III items.



Q54. Under the newly inserted Object Head 06 (Medical Treatment) via Addendum III, what is the maximum financial power delegated to a Divisional Head for the reimbursement of medical bills?
A. Up to Rs. 50,000/- in each case
B. Up to Rs. 1,00,000/- in each case
C. Up to Rs. 2,00,000/- in each case
D. Up to Rs. 10,000,000/- (10 Lakhs) in each case
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Object Head 06, Sl. No. 3(a) in Schedule III, the Divisional Head is delegated powers up to Rs. 2 Lakh in each case for medical reimbursements.


* The Rs. 10 Lakh limit belongs to the HOC/HOD in Schedule II-A.



── Sub-Section 7.2: OH 13 (Office Expenses) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q55–Q58 ──

Q55. What is the limit up to which a Divisional Head can sanction non-recurring contingent charges (where no specific limit is prescribed elsewhere), according to the latest Addendum III?
A. Up to Rs. 10,000/- in each case, not exceeding Rs. 1 Lakh per annum
B. Up to Rs. 25,000/- in each case, not exceeding Rs. 3 Lakhs per annum
C. Up to Rs. 50,000/- in each case, not exceeding Rs. 5 Lakhs per annum
D. Up to Rs. 1 Lakh in each case, not exceeding Rs. 10 Lakhs per annum
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under Sl. No. 8(i) Contingent Charges (Non-Recurring), Addendum III raised the Divisional Head's power from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 25,000/- in each case, not exceeding Rs. 3 Lakh per annum.



Q56. A Head of RMS Division is organising a Customer Meet. Based on Addendum III, which of the following best represents the maximum limits allowable for this event?
A. Total meet cost: Rs. 25,000/-; Working Lunch: Rs. 500/- per head; Annual ceiling: Rs. 1 Lakh.
B. Total meet cost: Rs. 10,000/-; Tea/Snacks: Rs. 200/- per head; Annual ceiling: Rs. 50,000/-.
C. Total meet cost: Rs. 25,000/-; Hiring cost: Rs. 10,000/-; Annual ceiling: Rs. 2 Lakhs.

* Under Sl. 8(v) Holding of Customer Meets, Addendum III amended the limit to Rs. 25,000/- for each meet, added a "Working Lunch" limit of Rs. 500/head, and set an annual cap of Rs. 1 Lakh per annum.


* Tea & snacks remain at Rs. 200/head, hiring at Rs. 5,000.



Q57. A Senior Postmaster wishes to urgently purchase stock and stationery items that are not ordinarily available in the local market. What is the annual limit available to the Senior Postmaster under Schedule III, and what specific condition must be fulfilled?

Q58. What is the maximum extent of financial power available to a Divisional Head for the purchase of Office Equipment (which does not include computers and is within the useful-life threshold) as updated by Addendum III?

Q59. A Divisional Head oversees a Post Office functioning out of a rented building. The landlord refuses to carry out necessary minor civil alterations for a sanctioned scheme. What is the maximum limit the Divisional Head can sanction for these alterations in this rented building, ensuring the cost will be recovered from future rent?
A. Rs. 10,000/- in each case
B. Rs. 30,000/- in each case
C. Rs. 50,000/- in each case
D. Rs. 1,00,000/- in each case
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. 12(ii) Minor Civil & Electrical Works for Rented Buildings, Addendum III set the limit at Rs. 30,000/- in each case for non-scheme, and up to Rs. 50,000/- in each case for scheme.


* Since the alterations are for a sanctioned scheme, the limit is Rs. 50,000/-.



Q60. Which of the following statements correctly details the powers of a Divisional Head regarding Object Head 29 (Repair and Maintenance) and Object Head 19 (Digital Equipment), as updated by Addenda II and III?
A. Repair & Maintenance is limited to Rs. 25,000/- per case; Digital Equipment is limited to Rs. 20,000/- per case.
B. Repair & Maintenance is limited to Rs. 10,000/- per case; Digital Equipment is limited to Rs. 50,000/- per case.
C. Both Repair & Maintenance and Digital Equipment are limited to Rs. 25,000/- per case.
D. The Divisional Head has Full Powers for Repair & Maintenance but zero powers for Digital Equipment.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

* Under Sl. 18 (Object Head 29), Addendum III raised the repair and maintenance limit from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 25,000/- in each case.


* Under Sl. 15 (Object Head 19, introduced by Addendum II), procurement of digital equipment is up to Rs. 20,000/- in each case.



Q61. Under Schedule III, what is the extent of financial power granted to a Divisional Head for Police Escort Charges required for the carriage of cash, a provision inserted by Addendum II?

C. Full Powers
D. No powers delegated; strictly an HOC item
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

* Under Sl. 8(xi), Police Escort Charges for Carriage of Cash, introduced by Addendum II, the Divisional Head is granted Full Powers.



Q62. Addendum III explicitly introduced new refund powers for the In-charge of the Foreign Post Office. Which of the following is NOT covered under the specific Full Powers (Sl. No. 19) granted to the Head of Foreign Post Office?
A. Refund of air surcharge in case of surcharged air-mail articles subjected to delay due to being sent by surface route.
B. Refund of earnest money received from tenderers whose tenders were rejected.
C. Refund of postage on articles which have been maliciously sent unpaid for the purpose of annoying the addressee.
D. Compensation of loss, damage, or non-delivery as specified by administrative instructions.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

* Under the specific new category "Financial Powers of Heads of Foreign Post Office" (Sl. 19 Refunds), the clauses explicitly listed are (a) refund of air surcharge, (b) maliciously sent unpaid, and (c) compensation for loss/damage/non-delivery.


* Refund of earnest money is a general Divisional Head power (Sl. 14(a)), not one of the specific three clauses listed under the new Foreign Post Office Sl. 19.
