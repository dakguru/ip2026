── Sub-Section 1.1: Rule 143 (Definition of Goods) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q1–Q3 ──
Q1. Under Rule 143 of the General Financial Rules, 2017, which of the following is strictly EXCLUDED from the definition of 'Goods'? A. Intangible products like software and technology transfer B. Books, publications, and periodicals for a library C. Transportation, insurance, and installation services associated with equipment D. Patents and other intellectual properties acquired for Government use Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 143 explicitly defines 'goods' to include all tangible and intangible products, including software, patents, and associated services like transportation and installation, but excludes books, publications, periodicals, etc., meant for a library. 
Q2. Which of the following statements correctly interprets the inclusion of "services" within the definition of 'Goods' under Rule 143? A. Only consulting services are treated as goods. B. No services of any kind can be classified as goods. C. 'Goods' covers works and services such as transportation, insurance, installation, commissioning, training, and maintenance. D. Outsourced manpower services are universally classified as goods. Correct Answer: C Explanation: • According to the definition under Rule 143, the term 'Goods' covers works and services that are incidental to the procurement, explicitly listing transportation, insurance, installation, commissioning, training, and maintenance. 
Q3. Consider the following statements regarding the term 'Goods' as per GFR 2017:
1.	It includes intangible products like software and licenses.
2.	It excludes patents and intellectual properties.
3.	It excludes books and periodicals purchased for a library. Which of the statements given above is/are correct? A. 1 only B. 1 and 3 only C. 2 and 3 only D. 1, 2, and 3 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Statement 1 is correct as software and licenses are included. Statement 2 is incorrect because patents and intellectual properties are explicitly included in the definition. Statement 3 is correct as library books and periodicals are excluded. 
── Sub-Section 1.2: Rule 144 & 149 (GeM & Fundamental Principles) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q4–Q8 ──
Q4. As per the fundamental principles of public buying (Rule 144), within how many days of Budget approval must Ministries/Departments project their Annual Procurement Plan of goods and services on the GeM portal? A. 15 days B. 30 days C. 45 days D. 60 days Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 149(iv) mandates that Ministries/Departments shall project their Annual Procurement Plan on the GeM portal within 30 days of Budget approval. 
Q5. Based on the verified amendments to Rule 149, up to what monetary limit can a Ministry/Department procure goods through ANY of the available suppliers on GeM, provided they meet the requisite quality and delivery period? A. Rs. 25,000/- B. Rs. 50,000/- C. Rs. 5,00,000/- D. Rs. 10,00,000/- Correct Answer: B Explanation: • As per the DoE O.M. F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024, the ceiling for procuring from any available supplier on GeM has been raised to Rs. 50,000/-. 
Q6. An office needs to procure standard desktop computers worth Rs. 6,00,000/- through GeM. Under Rule 149(ii), which of the following represents the correct procurement procedure? A. Direct purchase from any single supplier of choice. B. Purchase from the GeM Seller having the lowest price amongst available sellers of at least three different manufacturers. C. Mandatorily obtaining bids using the online bidding or reverse auction tool provided on GeM. D. Constitution of a Local Purchase Committee to finalize the vendor offline before placing the order on GeM. Correct Answer: B Explanation: • For procurements above Rs. 50,000/- and up to Rs. 10,00,000/- (as amended), the procurement must be done through the GeM Seller having the lowest price amongst available sellers of at least three different manufacturers. 
Q7. What is the ceiling limit for the direct procurement of automobiles under sub-rule (i) of Rule 149 through GeM? A. Rs. 50,000/- B. Rs. 10,00,000/- C. Rs. 30 Lakhs D. There is no ceiling limit Correct Answer: D Explanation: • The note under Rule 149(i) explicitly states that in the case of automobiles, procurement under this sub-rule is permitted without any ceiling limit. 
Q8. Consider the following statements regarding procurement through GeM:
1.	Online bidding or reverse auction is mandatory for procurements above Rs. 10,00,000/-.
2.	Business Analytics (BA) tools on GeM cannot be used for procurements below Rs. 10,00,000/-.
3.	A demand for goods shall not be divided into small quantities to avoid procurement through GeM bidding. Which of the above statements is/are correct? A. 1 only B. 1 and 3 only C. 2 and 3 only D. 1, 2, and 3 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Statement 1 is correct as per the amended Slab 3 threshold. Statement 2 is incorrect because Rule 149(ii) states BA tools can be used even for procurements less than Rs. 10,00,000/- if decided by the competent authority. Statement 3 is explicitly correct per Rule 149(vi) to prevent piecemeal purchases. 
── Sub-Section 1.3: Rule 150-152 (Registration & Debarment) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q9–Q12 ──
Q9. When a Ministry/Department registers suppliers for goods and services not available on GeM under Rule 150, for what fixed period are these suppliers typically registered before requiring renewal? A. 1 to 2 years B. 1 to 3 years C. 3 to 5 years D. 5 years Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 150(3) states that the supplier(s) will be registered for a fixed period between 1 to 3 years, after which they must apply afresh for renewal. 
Q10. What is the maximum period of debarment for a bidder convicted of an offence under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, for causing loss of life or property? A. One year B. Two years C. Three years D. Five years Correct Answer: C Explanation: • Under Rule 151(1), a bidder convicted under the Prevention of Corruption Act or IPC/BNS for causing loss of life/property can be debarred for a period not exceeding three years. 
Q11. If a procuring entity determines that a bidder has breached the "code of integrity", what is the maximum period for which the bidder can be debarred under Rule 151(2)? A. One year B. Two years C. Three years D. Five years Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 151(2) specifies that a procuring entity may debar a bidder for a period not exceeding two years if it determines that the bidder has breached the code of integrity. 
Q12. Which of the following statements correctly differentiates between 'Registration of Suppliers' and 'Enlistment of Indian Agents' as per GFR 2017? A. They are identical processes managed by the Khadi Village Industries Commission. B. Enlistment of Indian Agents is compulsory for those quoting on behalf of foreign principals and is not equivalent to the registration of suppliers under Rule 150. C. Registration is done by the Ministry of Finance, whereas Enlistment is done by individual Head of Departments. D. Both Registration and Enlistment carry a maximum fixed validity of 2 years. Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 152 clarifies that it is compulsory for Indian agents desiring to quote directly on behalf of foreign principals to get enlisted with GeM, but such enlistment is explicitly not equivalent to the registration of suppliers mentioned under Rule 150. 
── Sub-Section 1.4: Rule 153-155 (Reserved Items & Direct/Committee Purchases) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q13–Q16 ──
Q13. According to the Purchase Preference Policy under Rule 153, what minimum percentage of textiles required by the Central Government MUST be procured from amongst items of handloom origin? A. 10% B. 15% C. 20% D. 25% Correct Answer: C Explanation: • Rule 153 mandates the procurement of at least 20% from amongst items of handloom origin for exclusive purchase from KVIC, handloom clusters, etc.. 
Q14. Following the July 2024 amendments to Rule 154, what is the maximum value up to which purchase of goods on each occasion may be made WITHOUT inviting quotations or bids? A. Rs. 25,000/- B. Rs. 50,000/- C. Rs. 1,00,000/- D. Rs. 2,50,000/- Correct Answer: B Explanation: • The threshold for purchase without quotation was substituted and verified against DoE O.M. dated 10.07.2024, raising the limit from Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 50,000/-. 
Q15. Under Rule 155, purchase of goods not available on the GeM portal can be made on the recommendations of a duly constituted Local Purchase Committee. What is the amended financial slab and the composition requirement for this committee? A. Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 2,50,000; consisting of two members B. Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 2,50,000; consisting of three members C. Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 5,00,000; consisting of three members D. Rs. 1,00,000 to Rs. 10,00,000; consisting of five members Correct Answer: C Explanation: • As per the DoE O.M. dated 10.07.2024 amendment, the Local Purchase Committee can recommend purchases costing above Rs. 50,000/- and up to Rs. 5,00,000/-. Rule 155 also specifies the committee must consist of three members of an appropriate level. 
Q16. Consider the following statements regarding direct purchases:
1.	A demand for goods should not be divided into small quantities to make piecemeal purchases to avoid higher authority sanctions.
2.	The Local Purchase Committee must contain a minimum of five members.
3.	All items of hand-spun and hand-woven textiles (khadi goods) are reserved for exclusive purchase from KVIC. Which of the above is/are correct? A. 1 and 2 only B. 1 and 3 only C. 2 and 3 only D. 1, 2, and 3 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Statement 1 is correct per Rule 157. Statement 2 is incorrect as Rule 155 mandates a three-member committee. Statement 3 is correct per Rule 153. 
── Sub-Section 1.5: Rule 161-162 (Advertised & Limited Tender Enquiries) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q17–Q20 ──
Q17. Based on the 2024 verified amendments, what is the estimated value threshold beyond which an invitation to tenders by advertisement (Advertised Tender Enquiry) should ordinarily be used? A. Rs. 25 lakhs B. Rs. 50 lakhs C. Rs. 1 crore D. Rs. 200 crores Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Per the DoE O.M. dated 10.07.2024, the threshold for Advertised Tender Enquiry under Rule 161(i) was raised to Rs. 50 lakhs and above. 
Q18. In an Advertised Tender Enquiry, what is the minimum time that should ordinarily be allowed for the submission of bids from the date of publication of the tender notice for domestic bidders? A. Two weeks B. Three weeks C. Four weeks D. Six weeks Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 161(ii) specifies that ordinarily, the minimum time allowed for the submission of bids should be three weeks from the date of publication. 
Q19. Under what monetary limit is the Limited Tender Enquiry method primarily adopted, and what is the minimum required number of supplier firms that should be approached? A. Up to Rs. 25 lakhs; more than two firms B. Up to Rs. 50 lakhs; more than three firms C. Up to Rs. 1 crore; more than three firms D. Up to Rs. 50 lakhs; exactly three firms Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Following the July 2024 amendment, Limited Tender Enquiry (Rule 162) is adopted for estimated values up to Rs. 50 lakhs, and the rule specifies the number of supplier firms approached should be "more than three". 
Q20. What is the monetary ceiling up to which a Global Tender Enquiry (GTE) shall NOT be invited, subject to exemptions granted by the competent authority? A. Rs. 50 lakhs B. Rs. 10 crores C. Rs. 100 crores D. Rs. 200 crores Correct Answer: D Explanation: • Rule 161(iv)(b) states that a Global Tender Enquiry shall not be invited for procurement of value up to Rs. 200 crore. 
── Sub-Section 1.6: Rule 170-171 (Bid & Performance Securities) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q21–Q24 ──
Q21. Which of the following categories of bidders is NOT automatically exempt from submitting a Bid Security (Earnest Money) under Rule 170? A. Bidders registered with GeM B. Bidders having a turnover of Rs. 100 crore in the preceding financial year C. Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) D. Bidders having a Startup certificate Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Under Rule 170, exemptions are provided to MSEs, GeM registered bidders, bidders having Vendor Assessment, Startups, bidders with BIS certification, and bidders having a turnover of more than Rs. 500 crore (not Rs. 100 crore). 
Q22. What is the standard prescribed range for the amount of Bid Security (Earnest Money) as a percentage of the estimated value of the goods to be procured? A. 1% to 3% B. 2% to 5% C. 3% to 10% D. 5% to 10% Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 170 states that the amount of bid security should ordinarily range between 2% to 5% of the estimated value of the goods. 
Q23. According to the Finmin O.M. dated 01.01.2024 amending Rule 171(i), what is the revised range for Performance Security? A. 2% to 5% of the value of the contract B. 3% to 5% of the value of the contract C. 3% to 10% of the value of the contract D. 5% to 10% of the value of the contract Correct Answer: B Explanation: • The amendment verified against Finmin O.M. dated 01.01.2024 substituted the old range with a requirement that Performance Security should be for an amount of 3% to 5% of the value of the contract. 
Q24. Consider the validity periods for securities under GFR 2017:
1.	Bid security should normally remain valid for a period of 45 days beyond the final bid validity period.
2.	Performance Security should remain valid for a period of 30 days beyond the date of completion of all contractual obligations.
3.	Bid securities of unsuccessful bidders must be returned latest by the 30th day after the award of the contract. Which of the above statements is/are correct? A. 1 and 2 only B. 1 and 3 only C. 2 and 3 only D. 1, 2, and 3 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Statement 1 is correct per Rule 170(i). Statement 2 is incorrect because Rule 171(ii) dictates Performance Security must remain valid for sixty (60) days, not 30 days. Statement 3 is correct per Rule 170(ii). 
── Sub-Section 1.7: Rule 172-176 (Advances & Transparency) · 🟡 HIGH · MCQs Q25–Q26 ──
Q25. Under Rule 172 regarding advance payment to suppliers, what is the maximum permissible advance limit allowed for a private firm and a State/Central Government agency, respectively? A. 20% to private firms; 30% to Government agencies B. 30% to private firms; 40% to Government agencies C. 40% to private firms; 50% to Government agencies D. 50% to private firms; 100% to Government agencies Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 172(1) stipulates that advance payments should not exceed 30% of the contract value for private firms and 40% for a State/Central Government agency or PSU. 
Q26. Which of the following clauses MUST be included in the Bidding Documents to ensure transparency and eliminate arbitrariness as per Rule 173(1)? A. "Bids from Startups shall be given a 10% price preference automatically." B. "If a firm quotes NIL charges/consideration, the bid shall be treated as unresponsive and will not be considered." C. "The buyer reserves the right to negotiate the final price with the lowest three bidders simultaneously." D. "Electrical appliances below 3-star BEE rating are subject to a 5% penalty." Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 173(1) explicitly states that bidding documents should include a clause that "if a firm quotes NIL charges/consideration, the bid shall be treated as unresponsive and will not be considered".

Q27. As per Rule 177, the term 'Consulting Service' includes professional, intellectual, training, and advisory services. However, which of the following is strictly EXCLUDED from this definition? A. Services involving satellite imagery mapping B. Outsourcing of building facilities management C. Direct engagement of a retired Government servant D. Advisory services rendered by an Association of consultancy firms Correct Answer: C Explanation: • The definition of Consulting Service under Rule 177 explicitly states that it does not include the direct engagement of a retired Government servant. 
Q28. What is the amended monetary threshold for consulting services up to which the preparation of a long list of potential consultants may be done merely on the basis of formal or informal enquiries, without publishing an 'Expression of Interest' on the CPPP? A. Up to Rs. 10 lakhs B. Up to Rs. 25 lakhs C. Up to Rs. 50 lakhs D. Up to Rs. 1 crore Correct Answer: C Explanation: • Verified against the DoE O.M. dated 10.07.2024, Rule 183(i) permits informal/formal enquiries for estimating potential sources when the estimated cost is up to Rs. 50 lakhs. 
Q29. For a consultancy assignment with an estimated cost of Rs. 75 lakhs, what is the mandatory requirement for the identification of likely sources under Rule 183(ii)? A. Direct nomination of a single source consultant B. Only informal enquiries from the Chamber of Commerce C. Publication of an enquiry seeking 'Expression of Interest' from consultants on the Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP) D. Mandatory execution through the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) using the OPEX model Correct Answer: C Explanation: • Rule 183(ii) requires that where the estimated cost is above Rs. 50 lakhs (amended limit), an enquiry seeking 'Expression of Interest' must be published on the CPPP at www.eprocure.gov.in. 
Q30. Under Rule 184 regarding the shortlisting of consultants following the receipt of 'Expression of Interest', what is the minimum prescribed number of short-listed consultants? A. Two B. Three C. Four D. Five Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 184 clearly dictates that the number of short-listed consultants should not be less than three. 
── Sub-Section 2.2: Rule 192 (Quality and Cost Based Selection) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q31–Q34 ──
Q31. In the Quality and Cost Based Selection (QCBS) method for procuring consultancy services (Rule 192), what is the maximum permissible weightage that can be assigned to technical (non-financial) parameters? A. 50 percent B. 60 percent C. 70 percent D. 80 percent Correct Answer: D Explanation: • Rule 192(ii) strictly prohibits the weightage of the technical (i.e., non-financial) parameters from exceeding 80 percent in any case. 
Q32. In a QCBS evaluation using a 70:30 (Technical:Financial) ratio, a bidder receives a 'Very Good' technical grade (score = 80). The lowest financial bid in the pool is Rs. 1,00,000, and this bidder's quoted financial bid is Rs. 1,25,000. What is the bidder's final Combined Technical & Financial Score (CTFS)? A. 56.00 B. 80.00 C. 83.33 D. 88.00 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Technical Score = 80. Applying 70% weight = 80 × (70/100) = 56. Financial Score = (LFB / F) × 100 = (1,00,000 / 1,25,000) × 100 = 80. Applying 30% financial weight = 80 × (30/100) = 24. Combined CTFS = 56 + 24 = 80. 
Q33. Under the Stage 1 QCBS Grading System illustration provided in the GFR rules, what range of technical marks corresponds to an 'Excellent' grade yielding a score of 90? A. 91-100 B. 81-90 C. 71-80 D. 61-70 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • According to the QCBS Grading System table under Rule 192, an 'Excellent' grade, which grants 90 marks, maps to the mark range of 81-90. (Outstanding is 91-100). 
Q34. Consider the following statements regarding evaluation methods for consulting proposals:
1.	Under QCBS, the proposal with the highest weighted combined score (quality and cost) shall be selected.
2.	Under the Least Cost System (LCS), technical score carries a mandatory 20% weightage in the final evaluation.
3.	Single Source Selection (Nomination) is permissible if only one consultant has the requisite expertise. Which of the above is/are correct? A. 1 only B. 1 and 3 only C. 2 and 3 only D. 1, 2, and 3 Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Statement 1 is correct per Rule 192(i). Statement 2 is incorrect because Rule 193 defines the Least Cost System (LCS) as having "no weightage for technical score in the final evaluation". Statement 3 is correct per Rule 194(iii).

── Sub-Section 4.2: Rule 163-166 (Bidding Systems & Single Tenders) · 🔴 CRITICAL · MCQs Q42–Q45 ──
Q42. When procuring high-value plant or machinery of a complex and technical nature using the 'Two bid system' (Rule 163), what is the correct procedural sequence for opening the bids? A. Both technical and financial bids are opened simultaneously by the purchasing Ministry. B. Financial bids are opened first to ensure budget compliance, followed by technical bids. C. Technical bids are opened first; financial bids of only those technically acceptable offers are opened later. D. The financial bid is opened first, and if it exceeds the estimated cost, the technical bid is returned unopened. Correct Answer: C Explanation: • Under the Two bid system, Rule 163(1)(c) dictates that technical bids are opened and evaluated first. At the second stage, financial bids of only the technically acceptable offers are opened after intimating the date and time to the bidders. 
Q43. Which of the following best differentiates 'Two-Stage Bidding' (Rule 164) from the 'Two bid system' (Rule 163)? A. Two-Stage Bidding is used only for non-consulting services, whereas the Two bid system is for consulting services. B. In Two-Stage Bidding, the first stage involves submitting technical aspects without a bid price, leading to a revised set of terms for the final financial bid in the second stage. C. The Two bid system allows manual submission of documents, whereas Two-Stage Bidding is strictly via e-procurement. D. Two-Stage Bidding requires the approval of the Ministry of Finance, whereas the Two bid system does not. Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 164 describes Two-Stage Bidding as inviting technical aspects without a bid price in the first stage. After evaluating these, bids for prices are invited in the second stage based on a revised set of terms and conditions, unlike the Two bid system where both technical and financial bids are submitted simultaneously in separate envelopes. 
Q44. What is the standard operating procedure regarding 'Late Bids' (bids received after the specified date and time) under Rule 165 for an advertised or limited tender enquiry? A. They may be considered if accompanied by a late fee. B. They should not be considered under any circumstances. C. They may be considered with the specific approval of the Financial Adviser. D. They can be accepted if the delay is proven to be caused by postal transit. Correct Answer: B Explanation: • Rule 165 strictly mandates that in the case of an advertised or limited tender enquiry, late bids should not be considered. 
Q45. Under Rule 166, which of the following is NOT a valid recognized condition for resorting to a Single Tender Enquiry? A. It is in the knowledge of the user department that only a particular firm manufactures the goods. B. The required goods are needed in an emergency and must be purchased from a particular source. C. The Local Purchase Committee determines that inviting multiple bids is too time-consuming. D. The required item is to be purchased only from a selected firm. Correct Answer: C Explanation: • Rule 166 outlines only three specific exceptional circumstances for a Single Tender Enquiry: knowledge of a sole manufacturer, emergency requirements from a particular source, and the necessity to purchase only from a selected firm.
