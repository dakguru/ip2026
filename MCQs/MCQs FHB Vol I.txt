1. Under which Article of the Constitution are all moneys received by or deposited with any Officer, other than Revenues or public money, required to be paid into the Public Account?
A) Article 280
B) Article 284
C) Article 266
D) Article 268
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Under Article 284 of the Constitution, all moneys received by or deposited with any Officer, other than Revenues or public money raised or received by Government, shall be paid into the Public Account.

2. Which of the following defines a "Bank" concerning the general rules of the Financial Handbook Volume-I?
A) Only the State Bank of India
B) Any schedule commercial bank authorized by the Ministry of Finance
C) Any office or branch of the Banking Department of the Reserve Bank of India, and any branch of SBI acting as its agent
D) The Post Office Savings Bank exclusively
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The term 'Bank' means any office or branch of the Banking Department of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), any branch of the State Bank of India (SBI) acting as the agent of the RBI, and any other agency appointed by the RBI.

3. Who holds the authority to declare any Gazetted Officer as the Head of an Office in offices where there is more than one Gazetted Officer?

B) The Head of the Circle
C) The Head of Department
D) The Senior Superintendent of Post Offices
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Under Rule 3, Heads of Departments are authorized to declare any of the Gazetted Officers as the Head of an Office for the purpose of the rules in this Handbook.

4. What is the prescribed timeline for preferring claims by a Government servant against the Government (other than TA claims) without requiring an authority from the Circle Accounts Officer?
A) Within 30 days of becoming due
B) Within 6 months of becoming due
