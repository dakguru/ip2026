Q1. In respect of the 5 Years Recurring Deposit (RD) scheme, what is the prescribed compounding frequency for interest calculations?
A. Annually
B. Half-yearly
C. Quarterly
D. Monthly
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Under the revised interest rates for Small Savings Schemes, the interest for a 5 Years Recurring Deposit is compounded on a quarterly basis.

Q2. The maturity value of a Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) account opened on or after 01.01.2026 will double in exactly —
A. 113 months
B. 115 months
C. 118 months
D. 120 months
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The doubling period for Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) investments is statutorily prescribed as 115 months for the specified period.

Q3. With the establishment of the Centralized Processing & Reconciliation Centre (CPRC) at Chennai, which of the following Nodal Offices' POSB operations are being discontinued in a phased manner?
A. Mumbai GPO and PAO Chennai
B. Bengaluru GPO, PAO Delhi, and Sansad Marg HO
C. Kolkata GPO and Sansad Marg HO
D. Chennai GPO and PAO Delhi
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Nodal Office activities for POSB Operations previously carried out by Bengaluru GPO, PAO Delhi, and Sansad Marg HO stand discontinued as they are migrated entirely to CPRC, Chennai.

Q4. Which of the following transactions is NOT centralized and accounted for at the CPRC, Chennai?
A. Internet Banking & Mobile Banking Transactions
B. Outward Debits through NEFT & RTGS at Post Offices
C. ATM transactions including ATM charges and related GST
D. APY/PMJJBY/PMSBY auto debit transactions
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: CPRC Chennai accounts for inward credits through NEFT & RTGS and outward debits through NEFT & RTGS strictly (other than at Post Offices), meaning transactions initiated physically at the Post Office counter are not accounted there.

Q5. Consider the following statements regarding the accounting of POSB transactions at CPRC, Chennai:

1. TDS deductions under Section 194N and 194A of the Income Tax Act are accounted at CPRC.

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: All three statements are correct. CPRC Chennai is designated to account for TDS deductions, HIARM interest corrections, and PLI-POSB transactions to centralize the reconciliation process.

Q7. Under Section 194-A of the Income Tax Act, the TDS threshold limit for Senior Citizens (individuals resident in India aged 60 years or more) for the financial year 2025-26 has been revised to —
A. Rs. 40,000

C. Rs. 75,000
D. Rs. 1,00,000

Explanation: The threshold limit for Senior Citizens under Sec 194-A is enhanced from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 1 Lakh starting from the financial year 2025-26.

Q8. For payees other than Senior Citizens, what is the threshold limit for TDS deduction under Section 194-A of the IT Act from the financial year 2025-26?
A. Rs. 40,000
B. Rs. 50,000
C. Rs. 60,000
D. Rs. 1,00,000
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: For "Others" (non-senior citizens), the Sec 194-A threshold limit has been increased from Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 50,000 from the 2025-26 financial year.

Q9. In accordance with Section 194-H of the Income Tax Act, the applicable rate of TDS deducted at source from the commission paid to POSB agents with effect from 1st October 2024 is —
A. 1%
B. 2%
C. 5%
D. 10%
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The rate of tax deducted at source on commission paid to POSB agents was revised downward to 2% (from the previous 5%) effective October 1, 2024.

Q25. For an irregular opening of two NSS-87 accounts PRIOR to the DG Post's Order dated 02.04.1990, the second account will earn interest at —
A. The prevailing POSA rate
B. The prevailing scheme rate
C. The prevailing POSA rate plus 200 bps (2%)
D. Zero percent interest
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: For two NSS-87 accounts opened prior to 02.04.1990, the first account gets the scheme rate, while the second account earns the prevailing POSA rate plus 200 bps (2%) on the outstanding balance, subject to ceiling limits.

Q26. If a depositor holds two NSS-87 accounts opened AFTER 02.04.1990, what is the correct interest treatment for the second account?
A. Prevailing POSA rate plus 200 bps
B. Prevailing POSA rate on the outstanding balance
C. Prevailing scheme rate
D. No interest; principal refunded
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: For irregular multiple accounts opened after 02.04.1990, the penal regularization mandates that the second account will only earn the prevailing POSA rate without the 200 bps premium.

Q27. In the case of an individual irregularly holding more than two NSS-87 accounts, what action is prescribed for the third or any subsequent accounts?
A. They will earn the POSA rate.
B. They will earn POSA rate minus 2%.
C. No interest shall be paid and the principal amount shall be refunded.
D. The balance shall be merged with the primary account.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: For a third account or more irregular NSS-87 accounts, the rules explicitly dictate that no interest shall be paid and only the principal amount is refunded to the investor.

Q28. When a Public Provident Fund (PPF) account is irregularly opened under the name of a minor, what rate of interest is applicable to the account until the individual attains 18 years of age?
A. POSA interest rate
B. PPF scheme interest rate
C. Zero percent interest
D. POSA rate plus 1%
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Irregular PPF accounts opened for a minor earn only the POSA rate of interest until the individual attains majority (18 years), after which the applicable PPF interest rate is paid.

Q29. An individual operates two PPF accounts. As per regularization guidelines, the balance in the second account is merged with the primary account. What happens to any excess balance in the second account that breaches the applicable investment ceiling?
A. It earns the POSA rate of interest.
B. It is carried forward to the next financial year's limit.
C. It is refunded with a zero percent rate of interest.
D. It is transferred to the Senior Citizen Welfare Fund.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Upon merging, if the combined funds exceed the primary account's applicable investment ceiling for the year, the excess balance from the second account is refunded with a zero percent rate of interest.

Q30. Under what condition does an active NRI’s PPF account (where Form H did not specifically ask for residency status) start earning a zero percentage rate of interest?
A. From the exact date the citizen became an NRI.
B. On or after 01st October 2024.
C. On or after the maturity of the initial 15-year block.
D. Upon the total balance exceeding Rs. 15 Lakh.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: For Indian citizens who became NRIs during the currency of the account (where Form H lacked residency questions), the POSA rate of interest is given until 30th September 2024, after which it drops to a zero percentage rate of interest.

Q31. If more than two Sukanya Samriddhi Accounts (SSA) are opened in a family in violation of Para 3 of the Scheme guidelines, the irregular accounts shall be —
A. Merged with the primary SSA account
B. Regularized with POSA interest rate
C. Allowed to continue without further deposits
D. Closed by treating them as opened in contravention to scheme guidelines
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Irregular SSAs exceeding the family limit (beyond two, barring valid twin exceptions) cannot be merged or regularized; they must be closed outright as contraventions of scheme guidelines.

Q32. Consider the following statements regarding the regularization of a Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA) irregularly opened under the guardianship of grandparents (who are not legal guardians):

The account is not closed or downgraded to POSA rate simply due to the original grandparent guardianship error.

2. An MIS account closed before the expiry of one year requires a 2% deduction from the principal.
3. In the event of a valid premature closure of a PPF account (after 5 years), the interest is recalculated at a rate lower by 1% than the rate originally credited.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 1 and 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1, 2, and 3
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is incorrect because an MIS account cannot be prematurely closed at all before the expiry of one year from the date of opening; the 2% deduction applies only for closures between 1 and 3 years.



Q109. The closure of an SSA account before its standard 21-year maturity is permitted for the intended marriage of the account holder. This closure is allowed strictly within which of the following time windows?
A. Not before 6 months from the intended marriage, or after 6 months from the marriage.

C. Anytime within the financial year the marriage takes place.
D. Exactly on the date the account holder turns 18 years of age.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: For the reason of intended marriage, closure is permitted provided it is not requested before 1 month from the date of the intended marriage or after 3 months from the date of marriage.

Q110. To facilitate an SSA premature closure due to intended marriage, the account holder must furnish a declaration signed on non-judicial stamp paper attested by a notary. What critical fact must this declaration establish?

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The declaration, supported by proof of age, must explicitly confirm that the account holder will not be less than eighteen years of age on the date of the intended marriage.

Q111. Under the National Savings (Monthly Income Account) Scheme, 2019, if a joint account is opened and operated by three adults, how is each individual holder's share calculated to determine the maximum deposit ceiling?
A. The share is taken as one-half of the total balance.
B. The share is calculated dynamically based on initial contributions.
C. The share is taken as one-third of the total balance.
D. The entire balance is counted fully against each holder's individual limit.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: For the purpose of calculating maximum deposit limits in an MIS account, the account holder's share in the balance of a joint account is split equally as one-half or one-third, depending on whether it is held by two or three adults.

Q112. Under the Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS), 2019, the spouse of a deceased government employee who died in harness is allowed to open an account irrespective of age, subject to the condition that the deceased employee had attained what minimum age at the time of death?
A. 45 years
B. 50 years
C. 55 years
D. 60 years
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The spouse of a government employee who died in harness can open an SCSS account regardless of their own age, provided the deceased employee had attained the age of 50 years and met the other specified criteria.

Q113. Where both spouses have opened separate independent accounts under the Senior Citizen Savings Scheme and one of the spouses dies during the currency of the accounts, what is the prescribed treatment for the deceased holder's account?
A. The surviving spouse can automatically merge the balance into their own account.
B. The account can be continued by the surviving spouse as a sole nominee on the same terms.
C. The account cannot be continued by the surviving spouse and must be closed.
D. The account automatically converts into a standard Post Office Savings Account.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: If both spouses maintain separate independent accounts under SCSS and one dies, the account standing in the name of the deceased holder cannot be continued by the surviving spouse and must be closed.

Q114. If a Public Provident Fund (PPF) account holder fails to deposit the minimum subscription of Rs. 500 in a financial year, the account is treated as discontinued. What interest treatment applies to the balance of such a discontinued account if it remains unrevived?
A. The balance stops earning interest immediately from the date of default.
B. The balance earns interest at the standard Post Office Savings Account (POSA) rate.
C. The balance continues to earn interest at the rate applicable to the PPF Scheme from time to time.
D. The balance earns interest at the scheme rate minus a 2% penalty.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Statutory PPF rules state that the balance in a discontinued account that is not revived by the holder before its maturity will continue to earn interest at the standard rate applicable to the PPF Scheme from time to time.

Q115. A depositor operates a PPF account that has been treated as discontinued due to a subscription default. Which of the following operational options is legally open to the account holder?
A. The holder can apply for a partial withdrawal up to 25% of the balance.
B. The holder can open a new regular PPF account under their name in a different branch.
C. The holder can revive the account during its maturity period by paying a fee of Rs. 50 along with the arrears of minimum deposit for each year of default.
D. The holder can avail of a loan facility up to 50% of the standing balance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: A discontinued PPF account can be revived during its maturity period by paying a default fee of Rs. 50 along with the Rs. 500 minimum deposit arrears per year. While discontinued, the holder is strictly barred from opening a new account, taking loans, or taking partial withdrawals.

Q116. Under the Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019, the total amount deposited into an account during a financial year, inclusive of any arrears paid to regularize years of default, must adhere to which structural limit?
A. The total must not exceed Rs. 1,50,000, excluding the default fee.
B. The total must not exceed Rs. 1,50,000, including the default fee.
C. The total can go up to Rs. 3,00,000 if clearing past defaults.
D. There is no maximum ceiling if deposits are made to clear long-standing defaults.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The total deposit made in a single year can include regularizations for past default years, but the aggregate amount must remain within the annual ceiling of Rs. 1,50,000, explicitly excluding the default fee itself.

Q117. If a deposit exceeding Rs. 1,50,000 is accepted into a Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA) in a single financial year due to an accounting error, how is the excess amount treated?
A. The excess amount is regularized and earns interest at the standard POSA rate.
B. The excess amount is carried forward to the next financial year's limit automatically.
C. The excess amount is not eligible for any interest and must be returned immediately to the depositor.
D. The excess amount is forfeited and transferred to the Senior Citizen Welfare Fund.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Any deposit in excess of Rs. 1,50,000 in an SSA in a single financial year accepted due to an accounting error is completely ineligible for interest and must be returned immediately to the depositor.

Q118. Until what specific age milestone must a Sukanya Samriddhi Account be operated exclusively by the guardian on behalf of the minor girl child?
A. Until the girl child attains 10 years of age.
B. Until the girl child passes the 10th standard.
C. Until the girl child attains 15 years of age.
D. Until the girl child attains 18 years of age.

Explanation: An SSA account must be operated by the guardian until the account holder (girl child) attains the age of eighteen years, after which she can operate the account herself by submitting the necessary documents.

Q119. A guardian wishes to regularize a Sukanya Samriddhi Account that has fallen under default. What is the prescribed penalty and deposit requirement for regularization?
A. A penalty of Rs. 20 per year of default plus a deposit of Rs. 500 per year.

C. A penalty of Rs. 100 flat plus a standard deposit of Rs. 1,000.
D. No penalty is levied if the child is still under 10 years of age.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Regularization of a defaulted SSA account requires a penalty payment of fifty rupees (Rs. 50) for each year of default along with the minimum annual deposit requirement of Rs. 250 for each defaulted year.

Q120. Under the National Savings Certificate (VIII Issue) Scheme, 2019, how are Joint A-Type Accounts and Joint B-Type Accounts functionally differentiated at the time of maturity payout?
A. Joint A is payable to the first survivor only; Joint B is payable to all holders jointly.
B. Joint A is payable to up to two adults; Joint B is payable to up to three adults.
C. Joint A is payable to all holders jointly; Joint B is payable to any of the depositors or survivors.
D. Joint A requires a nominee signature; Joint B bypasses nomination rules.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: For both NSC and KVP schemes, a Joint A-Type account is payable to all adults jointly, whereas a Joint B-Type account is payable to any of the depositors or the survivors.

Q121. Under the Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) Scheme, 2019, if an account is prematurely closed after the expiry of two years and six months from its opening, what is the structure of the payout?
A. Only the principal amount is paid without interest.
B. The principal amount along with simple interest calculated at the POSA rate is paid.
C. The principal amount inclusive of a predefined interest amount fixed for that specific holding period is paid.
D. The principal amount minus a 1% management fee is paid.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: KVP rules state that if an account is closed any time after the expiry of two years and six months from the date of opening, the amount payable is inclusive of interest defined for that block period in the scheme schedules.

Q122. Under what condition can a Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) or National Savings Certificate (NSC) account be transferred from one individual to another individual before maturity?
A. Upon the request of the account holder to any family member after 1 year.
B. On the order of a court or on pledging the account to a Gazetted Officer.
C. Automatically when the holder changes their city of residence.
D. Only if the transferee pays a premium fee equal to 5% of the certificate value.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Certificate transfers from one individual to another are strictly limited to cases involving the death of the holder, court orders, or when the account is officially pledged to an authorized entity like a Gazetted Officer.

Q123. In the event of the death of a Post Office Savings Account (POSA) holder, interest on the accumulated deposits in the account is legally paid up to which point?
A. Till the exact date of death of the account holder.
B. Till the end of the financial year in which the death occurred.
C. Till the end of the month preceding the month in which the account is finally closed.
D. No interest is paid post the date of death.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: In the event of a POSA holder's death, the interest on the account deposits is paid only till the end of the month preceding the month in which the account is closed.

Q124. What statutory protection does the Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019 afford to the credit balance of an account holder against legal liabilities?
A. Up to 50% of the balance can be attached by a court order.
B. The balance is completely immune and shall not be liable to attachment under any order or decree of any court in respect of any debt or liability.
C. The balance is protected only if it is less than Rs. 1,50,000.
D. The protection applies only to accounts opened on behalf of minors.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The PPF scheme provides legal protection stating that the amount standing to the credit of an account holder shall not be liable to attachment under any order or decree of any court in respect of any debt or liability incurred by the holder.

Q125. If a PPF loan is availed by an account holder who subsequently passes away before fully repaying the principal and interest, how is the outstanding amount recovered?
A. The amount is recovered directly from the legal heir's personal savings account.
B. The outstanding loan and due interest are adjusted from the balance at the time of the final closure of the account.
C. The outstanding loan interest is waived, and only the principal is recovered.
D. The account is frozen and balances are permanently transferred to the government.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: In case of the death of the account holder, the nominee or legal heir is liable to clear the interest on the loan, and such outstanding due interest and principal are adjusted at the time of final account closure.
