Q5. In the context of Post Office bags, which of the following is NOT categorized as a "due bag"?
A. Branch office bag
B. Account bag from a Sub Office to a Head Office
C. Cash bag
D. Account bag from a Head Office to a Sub Office
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
•	An account bag is used between a sub-office and its Head Office and is a due bag. 
•	A branch office bag is used between a branch office and its account office and is also a due bag. 
•	A cash bag is used to enclose remittances of cash between Post Offices and is explicitly defined as not a due bag.
