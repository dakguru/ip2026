SCHEDULE OF FINANCIAL POWERS — MCQ BANK
PART 1: Foundations, General Conditions & Object Heads 01–49 (Schedule II-A)
(LDCE / PS Group B Standard)
Q1. With effect from which date was a separate Schedule of Financial Powers drawn up exclusively for the Department of Posts?

A. 1st April, 1988
B. 1st January, 1985
C. 30th July, 2021
D. 1st April, 2024

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:

Concept:
After the bifurcation of the Posts & Telegraphs Department into Department of Posts and Department of Telecommunications, a separate Schedule of Financial Powers for officers of the Department of Posts was drawn up and followed with effect from January 1985.

Elimination:
A (1st April, 1988) — this is when the DoP was brought under the purview of the erstwhile DFPR 1978, not when the separate schedule was created. C (30th July, 2021) — this is the date of the last revision of financial powers prior to the current revision. D (1st April, 2024) — this is when the new DFPR 2024 came into effect.

Q2. The Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 1978 (DFPR 1978) has been replaced by DFPR 2024. From which date did DFPR 2024 come into effect?

A. 30th July, 2021
B. 1st January, 2025
C. 1st April, 2024
D. 1st April, 2025

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
The Ministry of Finance replaced DFPR 1978 with DFPR 2024, which came into effect on 1st April, 2024. This necessitated review and revision of the existing Schedules of Financial Powers of the Department of Posts.

Elimination:
A — date of the last revision under the old framework. B — incorrect; not associated with any such event. D — this is the date when the revised Schedules (II-A, II-B, II-C and III) of DoP came into force, not when DFPR 2024 itself came into effect.

Q3. The revised Schedules of Financial Powers delegated by the Secretary (Posts) under Rule 12(2) of DFPR 2024 came into force on which date?

A. 1st April, 2024
B. 30th July, 2021
C. 1st April, 2025
D. 1st January, 2025

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
The revised delegated powers enclosed in Schedules II-A, II-B, II-C, and III came into force from April 01, 2025, replacing the financial powers contained in the previously existing schedules.

Elimination:
A — DFPR 2024 effective date, not the DoP schedule revision date. B — date of the last revision (OM No. 6-1/2021-Fin.Coord). D — incorrect; no such milestone on 1st January, 2025.

Q4. Under which Rule of DFPR 2024 has the Secretary (Posts), in consultation with AS&FA, delegated financial powers to HOC, HOD, Independent Directors, and Divisional Heads?

A. Rule 10(2)
B. Rule 12(3)
C. Rule 12(2)
D. Rule 15(1)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
The Secretary (Posts), in consultation with the AS&FA, delegated financial powers under Rule 12(2) of the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 2024 to the extent indicated in the respective schedules.

Elimination:
B (Rule 12(3)) — this rule deals with the responsibility of the Head of Department for correctness, regularity and propriety of decisions taken by authorized Gazetted Officers, not the basis for delegation. A and D — do not correspond to relevant DFPR provisions in this context.

Q5. Which of the following statements about general conditions for exercise of financial powers is CORRECT?

A. A work requiring sanction of a higher authority may be split into parts to fit within delegated limits.
B. For a contract extending over a period of time, the value of the first instalment is considered for the purpose of limits.
C. The total value of the entire currency of a contract shall be taken as the value for applying the sanction limit.
D. A competent authority to sanction a project is determined on the basis of the largest constituent estimate.

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
If a contract extends over a period of time, the total value over the entire period of its currency shall be taken as the value for the purpose of applying the sanction limit — preventing evasion through time-splitting.

Elimination:
A — expressly prohibited; no work requiring sanction of higher authority may be sanctioned by splitting into two or more parts. B — only the first instalment value is not the measure; total contract value applies. D — competent authority is determined on the basis of total cost of all constituent estimates, not just the largest one.

Q6. As per the general conditions for exercise of delegated financial powers, which of the following is true regarding the Head of Department's responsibility?

A. Once delegation is made under Rule 12(2), the HOD has no further responsibility.
B. The HOD shall continue to be responsible for correctness, regularity and propriety of decisions taken by authorized Gazetted Officers.
C. The IFA bears full responsibility for all financial decisions taken by authorized Gazetted Officers.
D. The AS&FA assumes responsibility for all decisions taken under DFPR 2024 delegation.

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:

Concept:
As per Rule 12(3) of DFPR 2024, while authorizing financial powers, the Head of the Department shall continue to be responsible for the correctness, regularity, and propriety of the decisions taken by the Gazetted Officers so authorized. Delegation does not dilute the HOD's accountability.

Elimination:
A — incorrect; delegation does not absolve HOD of responsibility. C — IFA consults and concurs but does not bear primary responsibility for line decisions. D — AS&FA plays a consultative role, not direct responsibility for individual decisions.

Q7. For cases approved by Secretary (Posts) or the Hon'ble Minister-in-charge, which authority must first clear the matter administratively?

A. Divisional Head in consultation with Divisional IFA
B. Head of Circle in consultation with Circle IFA
C. Sr. DDG (PAF) in consultation with AS&FA
D. Head of Department directly to AS&FA

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:

Concept:
All cases to be approved by Secretary (Posts) or the Hon'ble Minister-in-charge must first be cleared administratively by the concerned Head of Circle in consultation with the Circle IFA, and then forwarded to the concerned Member/DGPS for onward submission to AS&FA for financial concurrence/advice.

Elimination:
A — Divisional Heads clear cases within their own delegated powers; they do not handle cases destined for Secretary/Minister. C — Sr. DDG (PAF) has a separate schedule (II-C); this is not the prescribed channel. D — direct forwarding without Circle clearance is not the procedure.

Q8. For the purpose of Schedule II, who among the following is included in the definition of "Head of Department"?

A. Postmaster of a Head Post Office
B. Superintendent of Post Offices
C. Chief Post Master General (CPMG)
D. Director of Postal Services (DPS)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
For the purpose of Schedule II, "Head of Department" includes: Secretary PSB, Chief Post Masters General (CPMGs), Chief General Managers, Director RAKNPA, Post Masters General (PMGs), Sr. DDG (PAF), General Managers (PAF), GM (CEPT), Chief Engineers, and Director (Foreign Post).

Elimination:
A — Postmaster of a Head Post Office is not a Head of Department. B — Superintendents of Post Offices are Divisional Heads. D — DPS is subordinate to CPMG/PMG and not classified as an HOD for Schedule II purposes.

Q9. Consider the following statements about Sr. DDG (PAF):

Statement 1: Sr. DDG (PAF) shall exercise financial powers as contained in Schedule II-A applicable to HOC/HOD.
Statement 2: Sr. DDG (PAF) shall exercise only such financial powers as contained in Schedule II-C relevant to their operational requirements.

Which is correct?

A. Only Statement 1
B. Only Statement 2
C. Both Statement 1 and Statement 2
D. Neither Statement 1 nor Statement 2

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:

Concept:
Sr. DDG (PAF), i.e., Head of Postal Accounts Wing in the Postal Directorate, and General Managers (Finance) shall exercise only such financial powers as contained in Schedule II-C which are relevant to their operational requirements — not the full powers of Schedule II-A.

Q10. Which of the following financial powers under Schedule II-A are exercised by the In-charge of Foreign Post?

A. Only Schedule II-A (HOD) powers in all cases
B. Only Schedule III (Divisional Head) powers
C. Schedule II-A (HOD) / Schedule II-B (Independent Director) / Schedule III (Divisional Head) as the case may be
D. Full powers of Schedule II-A applicable to HOC

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
The In-charge of Foreign Post exercises delegated financial powers equivalent to HoD (Schedule II-A) / Independent Director (Schedule II-B) / Divisional Head (Schedule III), as the case may be — reflecting the varied nature of Foreign Post operations.

Elimination:
A — the In-charge exercises equivalent powers contextually, not always HOD-level. B — not restricted to Schedule III only. D — HOC powers (e.g., exclusively delegated items) are not all extended to Foreign Post In-charge.

Q11. What is the financial power delegated to HOC for ad-hoc payment of arrears of pay and allowances where Circle Accounts Office cannot verify due to destruction of records?

A. Rs. 50,000/-
B. Rs. 1,00,000/-
C. Rs. 2,00,000/-

The corresponding limit for HOD is Rs. 50,000/-.

A — this is the HOD limit, not HOC. C — not the prescribed limit for this item. D — Full Powers apply only to regular Salaries (Sl. No. 1(i)), not ad-hoc arrears.

A (Rs. 1,000/-) — this is the limit for rewards to persons not in Government service in certain categories. B (Rs. 2,000/-) — this is the cash award limit for GDS. D (Rs. 5,000/-) — exceeds the correct threshold.

Q15. Which of the following reward-related powers under Object Head 05 (Rewards) are delegated to HOC ONLY and NOT to HOD?

i. Cash Award to GDS for outstanding courage
ii. Rewards to persons not in Government Service for detection/conviction in criminal cases
iii. Cash Award to postal employees for exceptional courage
iv. Cash Award for Hindi Pratiyogita

A. i and ii only
B. i, ii and iv
C. i and ii only
D. i, ii, iii and iv

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:

Concept:
Cash award to GDS (Rs. 2,000/-) and Rewards to persons not in Government service for furnishing information leading to detection and conviction in criminal cases (Rs. 1,000/-) are delegated to HOC only. Cash award to postal employees (Rs. 10,000/-) and Cash Award for Hindi Pratiyogita are available to both HOC and HOD.

Elimination:
B — Cash Award for Hindi Pratiyogita is not restricted to HOC only. D — the postal employee cash award (Rs. 10,000/-) is not HOC-exclusive. Options need careful differentiation between HOC-only and general HOC/HOD powers.

Q16. The death or injury of a GDS due to enemy action — is it covered under the cash award for GDS under Object Head 05?

A. Yes, with full financial powers
B. Yes, but only up to Rs. 5,000/-
C. No, cases of death or injury due to enemy action will not be considered

Concept:
Cash award to GDS is for display of outstanding courage and devotion to duty in protecting Government property in civil disturbances or in the face of enemy action. However, cases of death or injury due to enemy action will NOT be considered under these orders.
