Answer Key and Explanations for MCQs on **Post Office Regulations, 2024** as established in the context of our exercise.

---

### **Part 1: Explanations (Q1 – Q25)**

**1. (A)** Regulations always come into force on the date they are published in the Official Gazette unless a specific future date is mentioned in the notification itself.
**2. (B)** "Authorised Officer" is a standard legal definition referring to an officer specifically empowered by the DG to execute tasks (like franking licenses, bulk mail acceptance, etc.).
**3. (C)** A "Postal Article" must be transmitted by the Post Office. Courier consignments handled by private operators do not fall under this definition within the Post Office Act/Regulations.
**4. (A)** "The Board" refers to the Postal Services Board (PSB), the apex management body of the Department.
**5. (B)** "Gyan Post" (a hypothetical 2025 amendment) is designed for subsidized transmission of books and educational texts to support literacy.
**6. (B)** While the DG runs the service, the **Central Government** retains the statutory power to fix rates for inland postage under the Act.
**7. (B)** Recall is permitted, but the article must not have been delivered yet, and the prescribed fee must be paid.
**8. (C)** The officer in immediate charge (Postmaster/Mail Office In-charge) has the power to detain suspicious articles for inspection.
**9. (D)** All listed items (excessive coins in uninsured covers, sharp instruments without protection, living creatures not specifically exempted) are general prohibitions.
**10. (B)** Articles violating packaging rules are usually returned to the sender as they cannot be safely transmitted.
**11. (B)** A Franking Machine impresses a "Frank" (stamp) indicating the value of postage paid.
**12. (A)** The Divisional Head (Superintendent/SSP) is the competent authority to issue RMFM licenses.
**13. (C)** Licenses are typically valid for 5 years under the modernized rules (previously varied, but 5 years is the standard for RMFM).
**14. (C)** Poste Restante articles are kept for **1 month** (except VP articles, which are shorter).
**15. (B)** "Late Fee" applies to articles posted in the specific interval between the closing of the mail and the departure of the dispatch.
**16. (C)** A Certificate of Posting only proves the article was handed to the PO; it does not track delivery or offer compensation.
**17. (B)** Standard letter dimensions: L+W+D = 90cm, with no single dimension exceeding 60cm.
**18. (D)** Blind Literature must strictly contain Braille or sound recordings for the blind; **no** ordinary written/printed communication is allowed.
**19. (C)** The weight limit for Book Packets containing printed books is **5 kg**.
**20. (C)** Redirection is done by the office of destination and is **free** for inland letters (not parcels).
**21. (B)** A refused article is not kept; it is returned to the sender immediately.
**22. (B)** Post Boxes allow the renter to pick up fully prepaid unregistered mail (letters, postcards, etc.) from the box.
**23. (C)** Compensation for Loss of Registered Article is 30 SDR or actual value (whichever is less). This aligns with international UPU norms often adopted for inland premium benchmarks.
**24. (C)** The **Postmaster General** (Head of Region/Circle) generally issues the permit for Business Reply Service.
**25. (A)** This is the statutory immunity clause (Section 6 of the PO Act). Officers are not liable unless they act fraudulently or willfully cause loss.

### **Part 2: Explanations (Q26 – Q50)**

**26. (B)** Mail is classified into **First Class** (Letters, Postcards, Letter Cards) and **Second Class** (Parcels, Book Packets, etc.).
**27. (D)** First Class mails get free air transmission. Second Class mails (all listed options) require an air surcharge for air transmission.
**28. (B)** Gold/Bullion value limit in uninsured letters is restricted (often cited as ₹20,000 in updated prohibitory clauses).
**29. (B)** Liquids like turpentine must be packed in a double receptacle: a sealed tin inside a wooden box with sawdust/absorbent.
**30. (C)** Live bees are an exception to the "living creature" ban, provided they are safely packed in suitable receptacles.
**31. (C)** "Obscene" on a postcard (which is open to view) is strictly prohibited and the article is detained.
**32. (A)** Unpaid=Charged on delivery; Breach of conditions=Charged deficiency; Prohibited=Handed to police; Oversized=Charged.
**33. (B)** Private postcards must match official dimensions: Max 14x9 cm, Min 10x7 cm.
**34. (A)** Inland Letter Cards (ILC) must not exceed **3 grams**.
**35. (C)** Book packets can contain manuscripts/books but **no** personal letters or messages in the nature of a letter.
**36. (B)** Pattern/Sample packets are limited to **2 kg**.
**37. (C)** Blind Literature is free of postage/reg fee, but air surcharge is **not** always exempt (except in specific new provisions, but technically air surcharge often applies). However, the answer key C implies the exemption doesn't automatically cover air lift unless specified.
**38. (B)** To be a "Registered Newspaper," it must be published at intervals of not more than **31 days**.
**39. (B)** If posted on non-specified days, the concessional rate is lost; it's treated as a newspaper but charged **double** the deficiency/postage.
**40. (B)** Unregistered parcels are limited to **4 kg**.
**41. (D)** Compulsory registration applies to insured items, parcels >4kg, and any cover marked "Registered."
**42. (A)** Lithium batteries (standalone) are strictly banned in **Air Mail** (dangerous goods) but allowed in Surface Mail under strict conditions.
**43. (A)** Use of fictitious stamps is a crime under **Section 263A** of the IPC.
**44. (B)** Redirection of inland parcels costs **half** the prepaid postage.
**45. (A)** Note the distinction: Poste Restante *parcels* are often kept for shorter durations (7 days) if unclaimed to clear storage space, unlike letters.
**46. (C)** Personal greetings make it a "Letter," violating the "Periodical" rate conditions.
**47. (A)** The mandatory superscription is "Literature for the Blind."
**48. (B)** If a letter exceeds letter weight limits (2kg), it is treated and charged as a **Parcel**.
**49. (B)** Delivery to deceased addresses requires caution; usually, legal proof is needed if there's any doubt, otherwise returned.
**50. (C)** Sharp instruments are allowed only if points/edges are securely covered (e.g., in a sheath).

### **Part 3: Explanations (Q51 – Q75)**

**51. (C)** Delivery is legally complete when the article is handed over and the signature (if needed) is captured.
**52. (B)** Refusal to sign the receipt/AD implies refusal of the article. It is returned to sender.
**53. (C)** Insured articles up to **₹5,000** are delivered at the door. Above that, they must be collected at the PO window (Intimation is sent).
**54. (B)** Minors cannot legally sign; delivery is made to the parent/guardian.
**55. (B)** For uninsured/ordinary registered items, the Postmaster has discretion to deliver to a near relative to avoid harassment, provided there is no dispute.
**56. (B)** Damaged insured articles must be opened and examined at the **PO Window** in the Postmaster's presence to assess liability.
**57. (B)** Modern regulations accept **biometric or digital signatures** on the delivery device (PMA app).
**58. (C)** Poste Restante letters are kept for **1 month**.
**59. (B)** VP articles are kept for **7 days** only (to ensure the sender gets their money or goods back quickly).
**60. (B)** Authority letters are valid for **3 years**.
**61. (A)** If AD is not signed, the article cannot be delivered. It is taken back.
**62. (B)** Mail with no sender address and undeliverable to addressee goes to the **Returned Letter Office (RLO)**.
**63. (A)** Refused=Returned immediately; Unclaimed=7 days; Unclaimed Parcel=7 days.
**64. (A)** Redirection is not allowed if the article has been **opened**.
**65. (C)** Redirection of inland letters is **Free**.
**66. (B)** Redirection of inland parcels is **Half the postage**.
**67. (B)** Instructions for change of address are valid for **3 months**.
**68. (A)** International redirection often incurs new postage because countries do not deliver other countries' forwarded parcels for free (UPU rules).
**69. (B)** Delivery to prisoners is made to the **Jailor** (authorized agent).
**70. (B)** Dissolved firms: Delivery to the **liquidator** or person legally appointed to wind up.
**71. (B)** Window delivery requires a written application to the Postmaster.
**72. (B)** Unpaid articles with sender's address are returned to sender for payment/affixing stamps.
**73. (A)** Only the **Sender** can recall an article.
**74. (C)** Customs duty is paid by the **Addressee** at the time of delivery.
**75. (B)** "Smart Delivery" implies GPS/Geotagging of the delivery location (introduced with the Postman Mobile App).

**95. (B)** COD is available for Registered Parcels, Speed Post, and Business Parcels.

**97. (D)** Bill Mail Service requires a minimum of **5,000** articles.
**98. (A)** National Addressing Grid promotes **Digital Address Codes**.
**99. (B)** Damaged foreign parcels are enclosed in a fresh cover, noted, and weighed.
**100. (A)** The RNI number must be printed on the **first or last page**.

### **Part 5: Explanations (Q101 – Q125)**

**101. (B)** Registration provides a record (receipt), tracking, and limited indemnification.
**102. (D)** All listed items (Insured, Parcels >4kg, "Registered" marked covers) are compulsorily registrable.
**103. (B)** Registration fee is paid via **postage stamps** (or franking) affixed to the article.
**104. (C)** Compensation is **30 SDR** (Special Drawing Rights) or actual value.
**105. (B)** AD is an optional add-on for a small fee.
**106. (A)** Insurance limit at a Branch Office (BO) is **₹600**.
**107. (C)** Insurance limit at HO/SO is **₹1,00,000**.
**108. (A)** Gold, bullion, currency, etc., must be insured.
**109. (B)** Indemnity is limited to the **actual value** (Post Office does not pay profit/over-insured value).
**110. (D)** Transparent covers are **not** allowed for insured articles (contents must be concealed).
**111. (B)** Delay of Speed Post = Refund of **Speed Post charges**.
**112. (C)** Loss of Speed Post = **Double the charges** or ₹1,000 (whichever is less).
**113. (A)** Business Post offers pre-mailing solutions (stuffing, franking, addressing).
**114. (B)** Media Post is an advertisement service (ads on postcards/envelopes).
**115. (B)** Direct Post (unaddressed mail) min quantity is **1,000**.
**116. (B)** Retail Post=Bills; e-Post=Digital-to-Physical; Logistics=Bulk; Bill Mail=Statements.
**117. (B)** PO ID cards are valid for **3 years**.
**118. (A)** Application + Photo + Residence proof is required.
**119. (C)** Addressee must sign, but the reason "signature is the *only* proof for insurance provider" is false. The signature is for PO records; the Department itself is the insurer.
**120. (A)** Insured value must be written in **words and figures**.
**121. (B)** Speed Post and Express Parcel provide online POD.
**122. (B)** Express Parcel is for corporate/business time-bound delivery (surface/air).
**123. (B)** International Tracked Packet is for small packets up to **2 kg**.
**124. (D)** No compensation for sender's negligence, prohibited items, or late claims.
**125. (B)** Yes, Speed Post can be insured (up to ₹1 Lakh).

**154. (B)** Complaints for Registered Articles are preserved for **12 months**.
**155. (B)** Reuse of stamps is an offense; the sender can be prosecuted (IPC 263A).
**156. (D)** Good Friday is a Gazette holiday, but the **mandatory** closed ones (National Holidays) are Rep Day, Ind Day, and Gandhi Jayanti.
**157. (A)** DG=Head of Dept; CPMG=Head of Circle; Supdt=Head of Division; Inspector=Head of Sub-Division.
**158. (C)** Once a "Clean Receipt" is given and the article is accepted, the PO is **not liable**.
**159. (B)** RTI application fee is **₹10**.
**160. (C)** Register of Damaged Articles.
**161. (B)** A foreigner can send a parcel if they provide a hotel address or local reference (traceability).
**162. (A)** Oily/greasy items damage other mail and machinery (AMPC).

**167. (A)** Illiterate payees: Thumb impression attested by a **witness** known to the Postman.

**169. (B)** Section **26** of the PO Act empowers interception for public safety.
**170. (B)** "Care of Postmaster" = **Poste Restante**.
