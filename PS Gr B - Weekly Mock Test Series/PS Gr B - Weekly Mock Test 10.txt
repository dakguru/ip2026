Q1. Under the Supplementary Rules governing Travelling Allowance, which of the following is not included in the definition of 'family' for the purpose of T.A. on transfer?
A. Legitimate children residing with and wholly dependent on the Government servant
B. Stepfather residing with and wholly dependent on the Government servant
C. Widowed daughters residing with and wholly dependent on the parent
D. Stepmother wholly dependent upon the Government servant
Answer: B
Explanation: The scope of the term 'family' explicitly excludes a stepfather for the purpose of T.A. on transfer, although a stepfather is included as a member of the family for Leave Travel Concession (LTC) purposes.

Q2. What is the fraction used to calculate the deduction of a permanent travelling allowance for each day of absence from duty?
A. 1/15 of permanent travelling allowance
B. 1/20 of permanent travelling allowance
C. 1/30 of permanent travelling allowance
D. 1/10 of permanent travelling allowance
Answer: C
Explanation: According to SR-23, permanent travelling allowance calculation involves deducting 1/30 of the permanent travelling allowance for each day of absence when a Government servant is on leave, temporary transfer, or joining time.

Q3. What is the maximum period for which a Conveyance Allowance can be sanctioned by the competent authority at one time?
A. Six months
B. One year
C. Two years
D. Three years
Answer: C
Explanation: Under the conditions for the grant of Conveyance Allowance, it is specified that the allowance shall be granted for a period not exceeding two years at a time.

Q4. Upon which of the following components is the Composite Transfer Grant (CTG) calculated for a transfer involving a change of station of more than 20 kilometers?
A. 80% of last basic pay including NPA and MSP
B. 80% of last basic pay excluding NPA and MSP
C. 100% of last basic pay excluding NPA and MSP
D. 50% of last basic pay plus DA
Answer: B
Explanation: CTG is paid at 80% of the last basic pay for transfers exceeding 20 kms. Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA) and Military Service Pay (MSP) are strictly not included in the calculation of CTG.

Q5. For computing Dearness Allowance (DA), which of the following elements are taken into account?
A. Basic Pay only
B. Basic Pay plus Transport Allowance
C. Basic Pay plus Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA)
D. Basic Pay plus House Rent Allowance (HRA)
Answer: C
Explanation: Dearness Allowance is calculated on the total of Basic Pay plus Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA), where applicable. Allowances like Transport Allowance or HRA are not included in the DA calculation base.
