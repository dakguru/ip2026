Q1. (The PO Regulations, 2024) [Difficulty: Easy]
Which of the following is considered an "accountable item" under the Post Office Regulations, 2024?

B. Registered Letter
C. Unregistered Printed Paper
D. Periodical Post

Regulation 2(1)(a) defines accountable items as those providing a receipt at booking and requiring a signature upon delivery, explicitly including International Registered Letter items.
