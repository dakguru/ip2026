Q1. Under the POSH Act, 2013, which of the following is correct regarding the definition of an "aggrieved woman"?
A. She must be an employee of the workplace where the incident occurred.
B. She must be above 18 years of age.
C. She can be a woman of any age, whether employed or not, who alleges sexual harassment.
D. She applies only to women working in the organised sector.
Answer: C
Explanation: Section 2(a) defines an "aggrieved woman" as a woman of any age, whether employed or not, who alleges to have been subjected to any act of sexual harassment by the respondent.

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the constitution of the Internal Committee (IC) under Section 4 of the POSH Act, 2013:

3. The external member's fees are paid by the appropriate Government.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: A
Explanation: Statements 1 and 2 are correct per Section 4(1) and 4(2). Statement 3 is incorrect because Rule 3 of POSH Rules specifies that the external member's fees are paid by the employer, not the Government.

Q3. What is the maximum tenure for the Presiding Officer and Members of the Internal Committee under the POSH Act, 2013?

B. Three years from the date of nomination
C. Five years from the date of nomination

Answer: B
Explanation: Section 4(4) specifies that the Presiding Officer and every Member of the Internal Committee shall hold office for a period not exceeding three years from the date of their nomination.

Q4. Which of the following statements is/are correct concerning the Local Committee (LC) under the POSH Act, 2013?

Q5. Under Section 9 of the POSH Act, 2013, an aggrieved woman must make a complaint within what time frame from the date of the incident?

Explanation: Section 9(1) mandates that the complaint must be made within three months from the date of the incident, or within three months from the date of the last incident in case of a series of incidents.
