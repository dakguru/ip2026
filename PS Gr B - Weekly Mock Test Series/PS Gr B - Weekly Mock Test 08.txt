Q1. Which constitutional provision serves as the statutory foundation for framing Recruitment Rules for various posts under the Central Government?
A. Article 309
B. Article 311
C. Article 312
D. Article 315
Answer: A
Explanation: Recruitment Rules are statutory rules framed under the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, regulating the recruitment and conditions of service for public services.

Q2. According to the Department of Posts (Multi Tasking Staff) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023, what is the correct GDS quota structure for MTS recruitment?
A. 50% by Seniority-cum-Fitness (5 years service) and 50% by Competitive Examination (3 years service)
B. 25% by Seniority-cum-Fitness and 75% by Competitive Examination
C. 100% Direct Recruitment from the open market
D. 50% by Limited Departmental Competitive Examination and 50% Direct Recruitment
Answer: A
Explanation: The 2023 amendment introduced a 50% quota by Seniority-cum-Fitness from GDS with 5 years of regular service, and 50% by Competitive Examination from GDS with 3 years of service.

Q3. Consider the following statements regarding the Postman and Mail Guard Recruitment Rules, 2023:

Explanation: The driving licence requirement is unique to the Postman cadre and is not applicable to Mail Guards. The recruitment ratio was substituted in 2023 to 50% LDCE from MTS and 50% competitive exam limited to GDS.



Q4. An official in the Department of Posts received a financial upgradation under MACP to Level 6. The substantive post held by the official is Lower Selection Grade (Level 5). Under the recruitment rules, for which promotional grade is the official eligible to be considered?

Q5. What is the mandatory induction training requirement and probation period for a Direct Recruit Postal Assistant under the PA/SA Recruitment Rules, 2022?

Q10. Which of the following clauses is a standard, verbatim provision found across all cadre Recruitment Rules in the Department of Posts?

Q11. According to the CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965, the term "Appointing Authority" in relation to a Government servant includes which of the following?

Q13. Under Rule 10 of the CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965, a Government servant shall be deemed to have been placed under suspension if detained in custody on a criminal charge for a period exceeding:
A. 24 hours
B. 48 hours
C. 72 hours

Answer: B
Explanation: A Government servant is deemed to have been placed under suspension with effect from the date of detention if detained in custody for a period exceeding 48 hours.

Q14. What is the maximum duration for which an order of suspension can be extended at a time, upon review by the competent authority before the expiry of 90 days?
A. 60 days
B. 90 days
C. 120 days
D. 180 days
Answer: D
Explanation: An order of suspension shall be reviewed before the expiry of 90 days. The extension of suspension shall not be for a period exceeding 180 days at a time.

Q15. Which of the following is strictly classified as a "Minor Penalty" under Rule 11 of the CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965?
A. Withholding of increments of pay
B. Compulsory retirement
C. Reduction to a lower time-scale of pay for a specified period
D. Warning
Answer: A
Explanation: Withholding of increments is a minor penalty under Rule 11(iv). Compulsory retirement and reduction to lower time-scale are major penalties. A 'warning' is not a penalty.

Q16. Under the CCS (CCA) Rules, what is the maximum number of disciplinary cases a serving official (like an IP/ASP) can act as an Inquiry Officer for in a year?

Explanation: A serving official acting as an Inquiry Officer has a ceiling of 10 cases in a year, and not more than 2 cases at a time.

Q18. According to Rule 15 of the CCS (CCA) Rules, how many days must a Government servant be given to submit a written representation upon receiving a copy of the inquiry report and the disciplinary authority's tentative reasons for disagreement?

Q19. Under Rule 25 of the CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965, what is the period of limitation for preferring an appeal against an order imposing a penalty?

Q20. Consider the following statements regarding the Revision of orders under Rule 29 of the CCS (CCA) Rules:
