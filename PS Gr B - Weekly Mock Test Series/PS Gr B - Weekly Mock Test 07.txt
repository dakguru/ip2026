Q1. As per the General Principles of APAR maintenance, the overall grade on a score of 1 to 10 is calculated based on specific weightages assigned to different assessment areas. Which of the following represents the correct distribution?
A. 50 percent work output, 30 percent personal attributes, 20 percent functional competency
B. 40 percent work output, 30 percent personal attributes, 30 percent functional competency
C. 40 percent work output, 40 percent personal attributes, 20 percent functional competency
D. 30 percent work output, 40 percent personal attributes, 30 percent functional competency
Answer: B
Explanation:
According to Para 4 of the General Principles for APAR, the overall grade on a scale of 1-10 is based on 40 percent weightage on assessment of work output, 30 percent on personal attributes, and 30 percent on functional competency.
Q2. What is the minimum period of experience required for an officer at the Reporting or Reviewing level to record their assessment on the performance of a Government servant?
A. 1 month
B. 3 months
C. 6 months
D. 12 months
Answer: B
Explanation:
As per Para 8 of the APAR General Principles, officers at both Reporting and Reviewing levels must have at least 3 months of experience supervising the work and conduct of the Government servant before recording their assessment.
Q3. Consider the following statements regarding the computation of the 3-month period relevant for writing APAR entries:

According to Para 12 of the APAR instructions, where an officer takes Earned Leave for more than 15 days, it is deducted from the total period for computing the 3 months. Short-term leave is not deducted.
Q4. If a Reporting or Reviewing Officer is placed under suspension when the APAR has become due, within what timeframe must they write or review the report?

Under Para 13 of the APAR General Principles, an officer under suspension must write or review the report within two months from the date of being placed under suspension or one month from the report's due date, whichever is later.

Q50. Under the post-2019 K. Meghachandra Singh principles, how is the seniority of a direct recruit determined if they are appointed in a year subsequent to the recruitment year?
A. With reference to the recruitment year when the vacancy originally arose
B. With reference to the year of their actual joining or appointment to the post
C. By interpolating them with the promotees of the original vacancy year
D. They are placed below the last direct recruit of the previous five years en-bloc
Answer: B
Explanation:
Para 2.4.6(c) of the DoPT Instructions clearly states that under the new principles, if the year of appointment is subsequent to the recruitment year, seniority is determined with reference to the year of their actual joining/appointment, delinking seniority from the vacancy year.
