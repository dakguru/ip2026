2. The strike period can be regularised or adjusted later by the grant of paid leave or emergency leave.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
C. Both 1 and 2
D. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation:
Rule 23 notes that participation in a strike results in full pay/allowance deductions, and explicitly specifies that the strike period shall NOT be adjusted or regularised by the grant of any kind of leave.



Q96. Under the revised SEDA effective from 26 November 2025, which of the following authorities serves as the Appellate Authority for all major penalties awarded to a GDS working in a Division headed by a PSs Group 'B' officer?
A. Head of the Division
B. DPS / PMG Concerned
C. Chief Postmaster General
D. President of India
Answer: B
Explanation:
Under the revised SEDA dated 26 November 2025, for Category 1 (divisions headed by PSs Group B up to STS level), the Appellate Authority for all penalties moves up to the DPS concerned or the PMG.

Q97. What is the statutory time frame within which the e-Engagement Roll data entry and document uploading for 100% of the existing GDS strength must be completed from its phased rollout on 01 April 2026?
A. Within 3 months

The e-Engagement Roll guidelines (OM dated 30 December 2025) mandate that the roll entry for all existing GDS must be completed in a phased manner within 6 months, at a rate of at least 15% strength per month.

Q98. According to Chapter 22 rules, what is the mandatory deadline by which the annual electronic verification of the e-Engagement Roll must be completed by the concerned Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO) every year?
A. By 30th April
B. By 31st May
C. By 30th June
D. By 31st December
Answer: C
Explanation:
The e-Engagement Roll rules dictate that the roll must be verified immediately after the close of each financial year, and the annual Verification Certificate must be digitally signed by 30th June every year.

Q99. Under the GDS (Compassionate Engagement) Scheme 2023, what is the maximum time limit prescribed for an eligible dependent family member to submit an application from the date of death of the GDS?
A. Within 6 months
B. Within 1 year
C. Within 2 years
D. Within 3 years

The GDS Compassionate Engagement Scheme 2023 mandates that an eligible candidate must submit the application to the sub-divisional IP/ASP not later than two years from the date of death of the GDS.

1. The CCE consists of a total of 3 members, headed by the Head of the engaging Division or Unit.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
