Q1. Which of the following categories of employees is covered under the Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme (CGEGIS), 1980?
A. Persons recruited in the Central Government after attaining the age of 50 years
B. Civilian employees paid from the Defence Services Estimates
C. Persons on deputation to the Central Government from State Governments
D. Part-time and ad hoc employees
Answer: B
Explanation: Civilian employees paid from the Defence Services Estimates are covered under the Scheme. The Scheme does not apply to persons recruited after attaining 50 years of age, persons on deputation from State Governments, or part-time and ad hoc employees.

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme (CGEGIS):

1. The scheme is compulsory for all regular employees, including canteen employees who were conferred the status of Government employees w.e.f. 01-10-1991.
2. The Scheme funds are apportioned in the ratio of 31.25% for the Insurance Fund and 68.75% for the Savings Fund w.e.f. 01-01-1988 onwards.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
C. Both 1 and 2
D. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: A
Explanation: The scheme is compulsory for regular employees including canteen employees. Statement 2 is not correct because the apportionment w.e.f. 01-01-1988 onwards is Insurance Fund 30% and Savings Fund 70%.



Q3. A Group 'C' employee is promoted to Group 'B' on a regular basis on 15th July 2025. What will be his CGEGIS monthly subscription for the month of November 2025?
A. Rs. 30
B. Rs. 60
C. Rs. 120
D. Rs. 15
Answer: A
Explanation: On regular promotion from one Group to another after 1st January in any year, the enhanced subscription is raised only from the 1st January of the next year. The Group 'C' rate of subscription is Rs. 30 per month (w.e.f. 01.01.2020). Therefore, his subscription remains Rs. 30 (the current Group 'C' rate) till the end of December 2025, and rises to the Group 'B' rate of Rs. 60 only from 01.01.2026.

Explanation: The insurance cover may be paid to the nominee or heirs of the missing employee after a period of seven years following the month of disappearance.

Q5. Which of the following statements is/are not correct regarding the recovery of CGEGIS subscriptions?
