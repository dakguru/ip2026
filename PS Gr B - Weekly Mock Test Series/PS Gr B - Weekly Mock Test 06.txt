2. A gap of 5 years is mandatory for a same-category transfer for the second time.
3. Persons with Disabilities (PWD) are granted one additional chance in both categories.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 1 and 3 only
C. 2 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: B
Explanation: Statement 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is incorrect because a gap of 3 years (not 5 years) is mandatory for applying for the same category transfer for the second time.

Q49. (Postal Manual Volume IV) [Difficulty: Moderate]
What is the prescribed maximum station tenure for an Inspector of Posts (IP) under normal conditions?
A. 3 years at a post, 4 years at a station
B. 3 years at a post, 6 years at a station
C. 4 years at a post, 6 years at a station
D. 5 years at a post, 5 years at a station
Answer: B
Explanation: The post tenure for a Superintendent of Post Office/RMS or Inspector of Posts is generally not more than 3 years at a time. However, the station tenure remains a maximum of 6 years at one station.

Q50. (Postal Manual Volume IV) [Difficulty: Hard]
According to the rules regarding the level of Question Paper Setters, which level of officer is authorized to set the paper for the LDCE for Inspector Posts?
A. JTS / STS & above
B. STS / JAG & above
C. JAG / SAG
D. HAG
Answer: C
Explanation: The table detailing the Level of Question Paper setters specifies that for the LDCE Inspector Posts examination, the paper setter must be an officer of the JAG (Junior Administrative Grade) or SAG (Senior Administrative Grade) level.
