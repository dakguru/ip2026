Q1. Who supplies the notice of hours of business in form M-6(c) to each branch office?
A. Head Postmaster
B. Sub-Divisional Inspector (S.D.I.)
C. Superintendent of Post Offices
D. Divisional Engineer
Answer: B
Explanation: A notice of hours of business in form M-6(c) will be supplied to each branch office by the S.D.I., as per Rule 5.

Q2. What is the maximum weight limit for postal articles carried by a village postman or extra departmental delivery agent?
A. 5 kg
B. 10 kg
C. 15 kg
D. 20 kg
Answer: B
Explanation: The weight of postal articles, including parcels, carried by a village postman or extra departmental delivery agent should not exceed 10 kg, according to Rule 9.

Q3. For how many years should the BO Account (PA-6) and BO Journal (PA-5) be preserved after they are fully used?
A. 1 year
B. 2 years
C. 3 years
D. 5 years
Answer: C
Explanation: Under Rule 21, the BO Account (PA-6) and the BO Journal (PA-5) must be preserved for 3 years after they are used up.

Q4. Which colour check slip is used to label an ordinary station paid and unpaid bundle?
A. White
B. Blue
C. Pink
D. Green
Answer: C
Explanation: A check slip is a label tied to the top of a bundle. A pink check slip is used specifically for an Ordinary Station Paid and Unpaid Bundle under Rule 64.

Q5. What does a red ink entry made by the Branch Postmaster in the Village Postman Visit Book (MS-86) signify?
A. The village must be visited daily regardless of mail volume
B. The village is entirely removed from the delivery jurisdiction
C. The village has a changeable letter-box plate
D. The village is not obliged to be visited on every trip but must be visited when marked
Answer: D
Explanation: Entries in red ink in MS-86 indicate that the ABPM is not obliged to visit the village on every trip but must visit the villages marked in red, according to Rule 9.

Q6. Which form serves as a record of all irregularities, mis-sendings, and misdirections noticed in a branch office?
A. MS-87
B. MS-18
C. MS-2
D. Pa-5
Answer: C
Explanation: The error book (MS-2) is maintained in all branch offices to record every serious or petty irregularity committed or noticed, per Rule 36.

Q7. During which period is the half-yearly enumeration return (MS-6) compiled each year?
A. First week of January and July
B. Second week of February and August
C. Third week of March and September
D. Last week of April and October
Answer: B
Explanation: Under Rule 45, the time fixed for making the half-yearly enumeration is the second week of the months of February and August each year (8th to the 21st).

Q8. How must unpaid and insufficiently paid unregistered articles posted in the branch office for local delivery be processed?
A. Delivered without any postage taxation
B. Sent directly to the Account Office for taxation
C. Returned to the sender immediately
D. Taxed with postage and impressed with the postage-due stamp

Explanation: The BPM must ensure such articles intended for delivery from the office itself are properly taxed with postage and impressed with the postage-due stamp, according to Rule 67.

Q9. Up to what maximum monetary value can registered letters and parcels be insured at an authorized branch office?
A. Rs. 100
B. Rs. 500
C. Rs. 600
D. Rs. 1000
Answer: C
Explanation: Registered letters and parcels may be insured at a branch office up to the value of Rs. 600, provided the office is authorized to deliver insured articles, as stated in Rule 93.

Q10. When an office is supplied with both cloth and leather cash bags, under what condition should the cloth cash bag be utilized?
A. When the remittance exceeds Rs. 500
B. When the remittance includes heavy coins
C. When the remittance consists of currency notes only and does not exceed Rs. 500
D. When the remittance is sent to the Regional Postal Store Depot
Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 130 specifies that cloth bags are used for remittances of currency notes only that do not exceed Rs. 500. Leather bags are used for coins or amounts exceeding Rs. 500.

Q11. What action is required if an inland letter marked "Registered" is found in a letter-box without full prepayment of postage and registration fees?
A. It is returned to the sender without processing
B. It is processed as an ordinary unregistered letter
C. It is charged with a fee equal to double the usual registration fee plus the postage due
D. It is confiscated and sent to the Returned Letter Office
Answer: C
Explanation: According to Rule 85, such an article should be registered and charged with a fee equal to double the usual registration fee and the amount of postage due.

Q12. For which specific days must the branch postmaster maintain the Yearly Village Return (MS-88)?
A. The first fourteen days of March
B. The entire month of April
C. The last week of December
D. The first ten days of January

Explanation: Rule 46 dictates that the return showing the number of articles received daily for delivery in each village should be kept during the first fourteen days of March each year.

Q13. When preparing labelled bundles, what defines a "Territorial Bundle"?
A. An express bundle containing exactly 15 articles for immediate sorting
B. A sorting bundle prepared for a state or foreign country when the number of articles is 25 or more
C. A station bundle containing ordinary unregistered articles for a particular station
D. A deferred bundle that can be sorted the following business day
Answer: B
Explanation: As defined in Rule 65, when a sorting bundle is prepared for a state, defined tract, or foreign country and contains 25 or more articles, it is termed a territorial bundle.

Q14. If an article received by the foreign post is unpaid or insufficiently paid, how should the branch office tax it?

D. It should not be taxed with postage by the branch office under any circumstances
Answer: D
Explanation: Rule 66 explicitly states that an article received by the foreign post should not, under any circumstances, be taxed with postage by the branch office.

Q15. In the Branch Office account (Pa-6), under which specific head must the cash collected on account of delivered V.P. articles be credited?

Q22. What is the regulatory stance on the submission of joint petitions by postal employees regarding personal matters?

Q26. If the key to a mail bag lock is lost by an office, what is the authorized procedure to replace it?
