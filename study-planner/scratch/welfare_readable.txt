Circle Welfare Fund for Gramin Dak Sevak (CWFGDS) 
The scheme came into force w.e.f. 01.07.2024 and is known as the Circle Welfare Fund for 
Gramin Dak Sevak (CWFGDS). CWFGDS is to be controlled by the Chief Postmaster 
General. 
In order to decentralize the implementation of the Scheme and to ensure fast decision 
making, it has been decided to delegate the powers at the Regional level in respect of the 
Gramin Dak Sevaks Fund to Regional PMGs. Thus, while the Regional Funds will be 
operated at the Regional Level, there will be only one single Circle Fund, and the

Every Regional PMG should, as on 1st April every year, intimate the amount of fund

payment received and payment made from the fund has been done by an Accounts 
Officer of the Circle nominated by the CPMG. 
Scope of the Scheme 
The Scheme is mandatory and is applicable to all regularly engaged Gramin Dak Sevaks 
working in the Postal Circles. The Scheme will not cover the substitute working in place 
of GDSs on leave arrangement and provisionally engaged GDS. 
Subscription and Recovery 
• 
Subscription Amount: Rs. 40/- per month for every GDS. The annual subscription 
of Rs. 480/- will be recovered in a lump sum in the month of April (TRCA of March) 
every year. 
• 
Newly Engaged GDS: In the case of a newly engaged GDS, the annual 
subscription will be recovered from their first month's TRCA on a proportionate 
basis, i.e., at the rate of Rs. 40/- per month for all the months up to the financial 
year end, irrespective of the date on which the GDS joins in a month. (April 2024 
to June 2024 = Rs. 20/- per month & From July 2024 to March 2025 = Rs. 40/- per 
month) . 
• 
Recovery Process: The Annual subscription will be recovered from all eligible 
Gramin Dak Sevaks by the Accounts Branch of the Head Post Office. The Head 
Post Office will remit the collections to the Treasurer of the Managing Committee 
along with the list of members, category wise, under intimation to their 
SSPOs/SPOs/SSRM/SRM etc.. 
Grant in Aid from Central Welfare Fund 
An amount of Rs. 200/- per GDS per year (on the basis of actual working GDSs) will be 
granted from the Central Welfare Fund to each Circle as a one-time Annual contribution 
on a recurring basis. In the Financial year 2024-25, the contribution from the Central 
Welfare Fund would be made @ Rs. 175/- per GDS (Rs. 25/- per GDS for the period of

Committee Meetings and Fund Utilization 
The managing committee will meet once in three months. All applications received from 
Jan to March will be decided in the meeting in April. All applications received from April 
to June will be decided in the meeting in July. All applications received from July to Sept 
will be decided in the meeting held in October.
