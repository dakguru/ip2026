🎯 Concept

HOC = Chief PMG of a Postal Circle = highest financial authority within the Circle (excluding Directorate-level matters). Schedule II-A is the master delegation — it lists each item of expenditure, the rupee ceiling, the rules/restrictions, and whether IFA consultation is needed.

🔑 Three-Layer Filter for Every HOC Sanction:

1.Is the item listed in Schedule II-A? → If yes, check the limit.
2.Is the limit within HOC ceiling? → If yes, check IFA consultation requirement.
3.Are conditions/restrictions complied with? → If yes, sanction.

🏢 City Class Memory Aid (HRA-style):

X-Class = Metro-tier (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad)
Y-Class = Tier-2 cities
Z-Class = Tier-3 / smaller towns

💡 HOC's renting power [11(i)(a)] = ₹10 Lakh/month UNIFORM for ALL classes — a powerful equaliser. Only HODs face class-wise sub-limits.

🔟 Step-by-Step: HOC Sanctions Renting [Sl. No. 11(i)(b)]

1.Identify need → Office / Office-cum-Residence / Residential / Project area
2.Check approved Schedule of Accommodation (SOA)
3.Obtain Certificate of Reasonableness of Rent from Postal Civil Wing / RCA
4.CPWD Consultation → non-availability certificate of Govt accommodation
5.FRAC Assessment → Departmental Committee assesses rent reasonableness
6.Excluded Cities Check → No private hiring at Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Shimla, Kolkata
7.Lease term → Maximum 5 years
8.Office-cum-Residence → Allot residential portion; recover Standard License Fee
9.CIFA Consultation → Mandatory
10.HOC Sanction → Up to ₹10 Lakh/month for all classes

🏦 Key Conditions & Eligibility

  • Ad-hoc Arrears Power (HOC ₹1L) → Used only when pay fixation was verified by AO but arrear claims cannot be verified due to record destruction.
  • Investigation of >6-year-old arrear claims → Authorised by HOC up to delegated powers.
  • Outsourcing Power [10(xi)(a)] → HOC ONLY; CIFA consultation mandatory; manpower cap = sanctioned vacancy strength.
  • Renting power excludes → Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Shimla, Kolkata (general pool entitlement).
  • Lease cap → 5 years maximum at a time.
  • Plinth area variation → Up to 5% allowed.
  • Foundation Stone [10(xvii)] → Includes shamiana, refreshments, garlands, photographs, illumination — capped at ₹5 Lakh.
