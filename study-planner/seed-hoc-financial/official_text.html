📜 Official Provision — DFPR 2024, Rule 12(2)

The financial powers of officers of the Department of Posts are governed by Rule 12(2) of the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules (DFPR), 2024, effective from 01.04.2024, replacing DFPR 1978.

The Secretary (Posts), in consultation with AS&FA, has delegated revised powers via:

  • Schedule II-A → Heads of Circles (HOC) & Heads of Department (HOD)
  • Schedule II-B → Independent Directors
  • Schedule II-C → Sr. DDG (PAF), GM (Finance) & DAP
  • Schedule III → Divisional Heads

Revised powers replace existing Schedules II-A/B/C and III — effective from 01.04.2025.

⚠️ Note 4 (CRITICAL — HOC-ONLY Items):

Items 4(ii), 4(iii), 4(iv), 11(xix)(a), 15(i), 15(ii), 15(iv), 18(iv), 22, 24(vii), 25(i), 25(ii), 27(iii)–(v), 28(i), 31, 34(i)–(v), 35(iii) and 37 of Schedule II-A shall be exercised ONLY by HOC.

📌 Note 5:

HOC-only items 11(xix)(a), 15(i), 15(ii), 15(iv), 18(iv), 22, 24(vii), 27(iii)–(v) and 28(i) shall also be exercised by Director (RAKNPA) and CGM (PD/BD/PLI).

📋 Conditions on Exercise of Powers:

  • No work may be split into parts to evade higher sanction.
  • For multi-year contracts, total value over entire currency = limit basis.
  • Powers cover both individual estimates AND projects (composite cost basis).
  • HOD remains responsible for correctness, regularity & propriety even when sub-delegated.
  • Subject to availability of funds + observance of all formalities.
