🟢 Scenario 1 — Outsourcing Sanction in Tamil Nadu Circle

The CPMG, Tamil Nadu Circle plans to outsource housekeeping for 11 RMS Divisions. Annual cost estimate = ₹18 Crores.

✅ Check: TN is among the 7 mega circles → HOC limit = ₹20 Crores/annum.

✅ CIFA consultation mandatory; manpower review against vacancies done; manuscript record maintained.

✅ Outcome: HOC sanctions; expenditure within Directorate's allotment.

🟢 Scenario 2 — Renting Office in Z-Class City (Karur)

A new HO requires office accommodation in Karur (Z-Class). Owner quotes ₹45,000/month.

✅ Check: HOC's limit for any class = ₹10 Lakh/month → well within power.

✅ Procedure: SOA-conformance + Reasonableness Certificate + CPWD non-availability cert + FRAC assessment + CIFA consultation.

✅ Outcome: HOC sanctions for 5-year lease.

🟢 Scenario 3 — Cash Award to GDS for Bravery

A GDS BPM in Madurai region foils an armed robbery and saves Govt cash.

✅ Check: Sl. No. 4(ii) → ₹2,000 cash award. HOC ONLY power (HOD cannot sanction).

✅ IFA consultation required (mandatory for all reward items).

✅ Outcome: CPMG sanctions; warrant issued.
