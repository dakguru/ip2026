# CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021 - High-Quality MCQs (Part 2: Q26 to Q50)

Q26. Which of the following conditions mandates the payment of a "Pro-rata Pension"?
A. When an official resigns after 5 years of service.
B. When an official is absorbed in a Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) in public interest.
C. When an official is dismissed but granted compassionate allowance.
D. When an official retires on superannuation but has a pending departmental inquiry.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: A Government servant who has been permitted to be absorbed in a service or post in or under a Corporation or Company wholly or substantially owned or controlled by the Central Government or a State Government shall be deemed to have retired from service from the date of such absorption and shall be eligible to receive retirement benefits (pro-rata pension) based on the qualifying service rendered up to that date.
Elimination: Resignation forfeits pension. Dismissal entails compassionate allowance, not "pro-rata pension". Pending inquiries lead to "provisional pension". 

Q27. In the context of pension authorization, what is the significance of "Form 7"?
A. It is the form for application for Voluntary Retirement.
B. It is the form for assessing pension and gratuity by the Head of Office.
C. It is the form submitted by the legal heir for family pension.
D. It is the nomination form for death gratuity.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: Under the procedural rules of CCS Pension, Form 7 is the critical document prepared by the Head of Office for the comprehensive assessment and calculation of Pension and Gratuity of the retiring employee.
Elimination: Forms 1, 3, etc., handle nominations. Form 4 is typically for family pension applications.

Q28. Regarding the "Commercial Employment after Retirement" rules, which of the following is considered "Commercial Employment"?
A. Setting up an independent professional practice as a doctor or lawyer.
B. Accepting an honorary position in a registered NGO without remuneration.
C. Managing a family agricultural land.
D. Writing and publishing a book on hobby gardening.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:
Concept: Commercial employment explicitly includes setting up practice, either independently or as a partner of a firm, as adviser or consultant in matters in respect of which the pensioner possesses specialized knowledge acquired during government service.
Elimination: Honorary unremunerated NGO work, agricultural management, and literary work (hobbies) are generally exempted from the strict definition of commercial employment requiring prior sanction.

Q29. Consider the following regarding the "Date of Retirement":
1. If an employee's date of birth is the first day of a month, they shall retire on the afternoon of the last day of the preceding month.
2. If an employee's date of birth is any day other than the first day of a month, they shall retire on the afternoon of the last day of that month.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
C. Both 1 and 2
D. Neither 1 nor 2
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: The rules defining the Date of Retirement state that a Government servant whose date of birth is the first of a month shall retire from service on the afternoon of the last day of the preceding month on attaining the age of superannuation. Otherwise, they retire on the last day of their birth month.
Elimination: Both statements are factual and standard procedural rules for superannuation.

Q30. A Government servant who is declared medically unfit for the post they hold, but is fit for another post, is absorbed in a lower post. How is their pension handled upon final retirement?
A. Pension is calculated only on the pay drawn in the lower post.
B. Past service in the higher post is completely forfeited.
C. The past service in the higher post is protected, and they receive an "Invalid Pension".
D. The service in the higher post and lower post are combined, and the pension is calculated based on the highest pay drawn if it benefits the employee.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:
Concept: The rules provide protection for employees reduced in rank due to medical unfitness or administrative reasons (without penalty). The final pension assessment considers the highest pay/average emoluments they drew across their service to protect them from financial detriment caused by medical demotion.
Elimination: Forfeiting service or arbitrarily reducing the pension contradicts the fundamental welfare protection provided for medical incapacitation.

Q31. Under the Right to Information Act (as interfaced with Pension Rules), can the pension calculation sheet (Form 7) be denied to the retiring employee?
A. Yes, it is a classified document.
B. Yes, until the PPO is strictly issued.
C. No, the retiring employee has the absolute right to receive a copy of the calculation sheet.
D. Yes, if the Head of Office deems it confidential.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: Transparency rules embedded within the pension procedure mandate that the Head of Office MUST provide a copy of the pension calculation sheet to the retiring employee prior to the finalization of the PPO.
Elimination: Pension calculations are personal financial data of the employee and cannot be classified or denied to the beneficiary under any circumstances.

Q32. In a situation where a female Government servant is separated from her husband and has filed for divorce, what is her right regarding the "Nomination for Family Pension"?
A. She cannot change the nomination until the final divorce decree.
B. The husband remains the default primary beneficiary by law.
C. She can explicitly request in writing to exclude her legally wedded husband from the family pension in favor of her eligible children.
D. She must nominate her parents instead.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: A recent and crucial amendment in the CCS Pension Rules allows a female Government servant (or pensioner) who is involved in divorce proceedings, or has filed a case of domestic violence against her husband, to make a written request to the Head of Office to grant family pension to her eligible child/children in precedence over her husband.
Elimination: The rigid hierarchy was amended to empower female employees to protect their children over separated/abusive spouses, superseding the old rule where the legal spouse could not be bypassed.

Q33. Which of the following defines the term "Boy Service" under the CCS (Pension) Rules?
A. Service rendered by a minor intern without pay.
B. Service rendered before attaining the age of 18 years.
C. Service rendered during a probationary period.
D. Service in the Boy Scouts.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: "Boy Service" refers to the service rendered by a Government servant before attaining the age of 18 years. Such service does NOT count as qualifying service for the calculation of pension under regular circumstances.
Elimination: It is strictly defined by age (under 18), not by the mode of appointment (internship or probation).

Q34. If a Government servant dies while in service, what is the minimum continuous service required for their family to be eligible for "Family Pension"?
A. 1 year
B. 5 years
C. 7 years
D. There is no minimum service requirement if death occurs in service.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:
Concept: If a Government servant dies while in service, the family is eligible for family pension irrespective of the length of service rendered by the deceased. Even if the employee dies on the very first day of joining duty, the family is entitled to standard Family Pension.
Elimination: Minimum service requirements (like 10 years for regular pension or 5 years for gratuity) do not apply to the basic admissibility of Family Pension for in-service deaths.

Q35. Consider the following scenarios under the principle of "Two Pensions":
1. A person cannot earn two pensions in the same service or post at the same time.
2. A single continuous service can qualify for duplicate pensions under different schemes if the employee pays dual subscriptions.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A. 1 only
B. 2 only
C. Both 1 and 2
D. Neither 1 nor 2
Correct Answer: A
Explanation:
Concept: The fundamental principle regarding conditions subject to which pension is admissible (Rule 7) dictates that a Government servant shall not earn two pensions in the same service or post at the same time or by the same continuous service.
Elimination: Statement 2 is entirely false as duplicate pensions for the same continuous service period under state/central schemes are strictly prohibited.

Q36. When a Government servant retires on Superannuation, the Payment Order (PPO) and related authorities must ideally be issued by the Accounts Officer by which timeline?
A. On the day of retirement.
B. Not later than one month before the date of retirement.
C. Within three months after the date of retirement.
D. Before the last paycheck is cleared.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: The procedural timeline under the CCS (Pension) Rules dictates that the Accounts Officer shall issue the Pension Payment Order (PPO) and the authority for the payment of gratuity not later than one month in advance of the date of the retirement of the Government servant.
Elimination: Delays cause severe financial hardship, so the rules legally mandate the advance timeline, invalidating "after retirement" or "on the day of" options.

Q37. A Government servant is deputed to foreign service. How does this period impact their pension?
A. It causes a break in service.
B. It is counted as qualifying service only if the foreign employer pays pension contributions to the Central Government.
C. It counts as qualifying service, and the Central Government bears the cost unconditionally.
D. It is treated as "Dies-non."
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: Periods of deputation on foreign service count as qualifying service provided the required pension contributions are paid to the Central Government either by the foreign employer or by the Government servant themselves.
Elimination: If contributions are not paid, the period is excluded. It doesn't break service, but it isn't "unconditionally" paid by the government either.

Q38. "Service Gratuity" is paid instead of a monthly pension when the qualifying service of a retiring Government servant is:
A. Less than 5 years.
B. Less than 10 years.
C. Exactly 10 years.
D. Between 10 and 20 years.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: A retiring Government servant who has completed less than 10 years of qualifying service is not eligible for a regular monthly pension. Instead, they are paid a lump-sum "Service Gratuity" calculated at the rate of a half month's basic pay for every completed six-monthly period of qualifying service.
Elimination: Minimum 10 years is the threshold for a monthly pension. Less than 5 years would mean they also miss Retirement Gratuity, but they would still get Service Gratuity.

Q39. If the President of India issues an order withholding a pension under Rule 8 due to grave misconduct, the pensioner can submit an appeal against this order within what timeframe?
A. 30 days
B. 45 days
C. 60 days
D. No appeal lies against an order passed by the President.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:
Concept: An order passed by the President in the name of the President withholding or withdrawing a pension is absolute in the executive branch. Under standard administrative law mapped in the CCS rules, no appeal lies against an order passed by the President. (A review petition may be possible, or judicial review via courts, but no formal administrative "appeal" exists).
Elimination: The President is the highest executive authority; hence, standard timelines for appellate authorities (like 45 days to higher officers) are inapplicable.

Q41. A pensioner receives an overpayment of pension due to an administrative error by the bank. Can this overpayment be recovered from their future pension?
A. Yes, the bank can recover the full amount in one go without notice.
B. No, overpayments due to bank errors cannot be recovered under any circumstances.
C. Yes, but recovery should be made in installments, typically not exceeding 1/3rd of the net (uncommuted) pension, and in accordance with RBI guidelines.
D. Yes, but only with the written consent of the pensioner.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: While banks have the right to recover excess payments made due to clerical errors, the Supreme Court and subsequent guidelines stipulate that recoveries must not cause extreme hardship. The standard limit is recovering in installments not exceeding 1/3rd of the net pension.
Elimination: Sudden full recovery is prohibited. Complete exemption from recovery relies on specific court exceptions (e.g., Group C/D employees where overpayment went on for years without their fault), but as a general rule, structured recovery is applicable.

Q42. Which of the following defines "Dearness Relief" correctly?
A. It is an allowance paid to working employees to offset inflation.
B. It is the inflation offset allowance sanctioned by the Government on the Basic Pension/Family Pension.
C. It is the lump sum paid at the time of retirement.
D. It is a relief fund for pensioners suffering from critical illnesses.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: Dearness Relief (DR) is explicitly the inflation-linked allowance granted to pensioners and family pensioners on their Basic Pension.
Elimination: Working employees receive Dearness Allowance (DA). DR is not a lump sum nor a medical relief fund. 

Q43. An employee was suspended and later dismissed. Which of the following is true regarding their pension?
A. The period of suspension counts as service for pension.
B. The dismissal results in forfeiture of all past service, hence no pension.
C. They are entitled to a provisional pension equal to 50% of basic pay.
D. The suspension period is treated as "leave without pay" and added to service.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: Under the rules, dismissal or removal from service acts as a total forfeiture of past service. All periods of service, including any preceding suspensions, are wiped out for the purpose of pension calculation.
Elimination: Options granting pension or adding service contradict the foundational rule of forfeiture upon dismissal.

Q44. What is the maximum percentage of basic pension that can be deducted for government dues (excluding specific judicial decrees) without the pensioner's explicit consent?
A. 10%
B. 33.3% (One-third)
C. 50%
D. Recovery cannot be made from pension without consent.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: Government dues (like overpaid pay/allowances or outstanding license fees) can be recovered from the pension/dearness relief. However, the maximum recovery is generally capped at one-third of the pension to ensure the pensioner retains a livable income.
Elimination: Complete immunity (D) or extremely high recovery (C) violates principles of natural justice and government guidelines.

Q45. For the purpose of "Family Pension", what is the primary condition for dependent parents to be eligible?
A. They must be over 60 years of age.
B. They must not have any other surviving children.
C. Their combined income from all sources must be less than the minimum family pension plus the dearness relief thereon.
D. They must have lived in the same household as the deceased employee.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: The dependency criteria for family pension dictate that parents, siblings, or widowed/divorced daughters are considered dependent if their gross income from all sources is less than the minimum family pension (currently ₹9,000) plus the Dearness Relief admissible on it.
Elimination: Age limits and household proximity are not the defining legal criteria for dependency under the CCS rules.

Q46. A Government servant who takes "Voluntary Retirement" after completing 20 years of qualifying service realizes a change of heart. Under what circumstance can they NOT withdraw their notice?
A. If the request is made 15 days before retirement.
B. If the appointing authority refuses the withdrawal.
C. If the notice has been formally accepted and the retirement has already become effective.
D. If they belong to Group 'B' services.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: A notice of voluntary retirement can only be withdrawn *before* the intended date of retirement. Once the retirement becomes effective (the date passes and the employee is relieved), the relationship of master and servant is severed, and withdrawal is legally impossible.
Elimination: While the authority can refuse (B), option C is the absolute absolute point of no return. 

Q47. Identify the INCORRECT statement regarding "Invalid Pension":
A. It is granted to a Government servant who retires from service due to a bodily or mental infirmity which permanently incapacitates them.
B. A medical certificate from a specified Medical Board is required.
C. It can be granted even if the incapacitation is only for a specific post but the official is fit for lower posts, and refuses the lower post.
D. The amount of invalid pension is never less than the ordinary family pension.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: If a Government servant is medically unfit for their current post but fit for a lower post, the government endeavors to accommodate them. If they *refuse* the lower post offered to them, they do not get an Invalid Pension; their refusal complicates the process under Persons with Disabilities rules. Invalid pension is for total permanent incapacitation for further service.
Elimination: Options A, B, and D reflect standard statutes governing invalidations.

Q48. The term "Qualifying Service" does NOT include:
A. Duty period.
B. Period of Earned Leave.
C. Period of suspension treated as Dies-non.
D. Period of training immediately followed by an appointment.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation:
Concept: Qualifying service includes all periods spent on duty, leave with allowances, and pre-appointment training (if authorized). However, any period treated as "Dies-non" (which means the period does not count for any purpose) is strictly excluded from qualifying service.
Elimination: Options A, B, and D are standard inclusions in the calculation.

Q49. Under the revised CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021, if a pensioner wishes to commute a fraction of their pension after one year from the date of retirement, what is the mandatory requirement?
A. They must submit a fresh application.
B. They must undergo a medical examination by a specified Medical Authority.
C. They must surrender 50% of their Gratuity.
D. Commutation is strictly prohibited after one year of retirement.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
Concept: Commutation of pension without a medical examination is allowed only if the application is submitted within one year of retirement. If the application is submitted after one year, the pensioner MUST undergo a medical examination by a recognized board to assess their health and life expectancy before commutation is granted.
Elimination: Commutation is not prohibited (D). Submitting just an application (A) is insufficient after the 1-year mark.
