📜 Official Provision — DFPR 2024, Schedule III (Part 2)

Part 2 of Schedule III covers the operational delivery wing of a Divisional Head's powers — items dealing with publicity material, equipment hiring, minor civil & electrical works, legal charges, and the all-important refund powers under "Other Financial Powers of Divisional Heads".

These powers are exercised under Rule 12(2) of DFPR 2024 subject to the binding Para 6 of the Preamble:

"With respect to the financial powers delegated to Divisional Heads, if Government rules, manuals, policies, guidelines, procedures, or directives explicitly mandate IFA consultation, the matter must be referred to the concerned HOD / HOC as required."

⚙️ Operational Triggers in Part 2

  • Publicity & Marketing → Sl. No. 10 (rate charts, mailers, printing).
  • Equipment Renting → Sl. No. 11 (rules based).
  • Building Repairs → Sl. No. 12 (Govt + Rented, very tight ceilings).
  • Legal Costs → Sl. No. 13 (counsel, judgement copies, max ₹2L).
  • Refunds → Sl. No. 14 (5 categories, mostly Full Powers).

📚 Reference Documents Triggered

  • DoP OM No. BDG-20/3/2021-BuildingDOP dated 18.02.2022 — recovery of repair expenses from rent (Para 9(i) of Part-A).
  • GFR provisions for tendering / earnest money refunds.

⚠️ Key Restrictive Conditions [12(ii) & 12(iii) — Rented Buildings]

  1. Expenditure incurred only if landlord refuses to meet charges.
  2. On building release → Govt has the right to remove the installation material added.
  3. Any expenditure on repair/alteration of rented building is to be recovered from rent payable as per DoP OM BDG-20/3/2021-BuildingDOP dated 18.02.2022 (Para 9(i) of Part-A).
  4. Annual limits apply to each building separately.
  5. Reasonable certainty Department will continue in possession for next 5 years.
