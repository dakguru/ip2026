🎯 Concept

Part 2 builds on Part 1 by giving the Divisional Head operational autonomy at the field level — running offices, conducting marketing, fixing minor problems, fighting small legal battles, and resolving customer-side refunds without upward referral.

🔑 Four Domains of Part 2 Powers

Domain A — Publicity & Printing [Sl. No. 10]

  • DH can spend up to ₹20,000/annum on all publicity printing combined (rate charts, mailers, brochures) — a small budget meant for local promotion.
  • Plus ₹2 Lakh/annum for general printing & binding (registers, indents, forms).

Domain B — Equipment Renting [Sl. No. 11]

  • A "soft" power — DH may rent equipment, computers, A/C, furniture etc. as per limits prescribed under applicable rules.
  • No hard cap in Schedule III, but must operate within Nodal Dept / DoP guidelines.

Domain C — Civil & Electrical Works [Sl. No. 12]

  • For Govt Buildings → ₹50,000/case (meaningful pocket for fixing leakages/faults).
  • For Rented Buildings → only ₹5,000/case, AND ONLY IF landlord refuses → cost must be recoverable from future rent.

Domain D — Legal & Refunds [Sl. No. 13–14]

  • Legal Charges → Pay counsel/judgement copies at Govt-fixed rate, max ₹2 Lakh/case.
  • Refunds → All five categories carry Full Powers because they return amounts wrongly held, not fresh expenditure.

🧠 Why "Full Powers" for Refunds?

Refunds return money that doesn't legitimately belong to Govt. DH gets Full Powers (subject to rules), unlike HOC's refund of Advertisement Charges (₹50K cap) which involves judgment. DH refunds [Sl. 14] are automatic/mechanical.

🧠 The "Rented Building" Discipline

DoP OM BDG-20/3/2021 dated 18.02.2022 (Para 9(i)) is uniform across HOC and DH: any expenditure on rented buildings must be recovered from future rents before lease ends.

🧠 "Within DH Contract Competency"

Earnest Money refund is Full Powers, BUT only for tenders within DH's own contract competency. If DH had no power to award, they cannot refund EMD either.

🔟 Step-by-Step Processes

Sanctioning Minor Civil Works on Rented Building [Sl. 12(ii)]

  1. Identify Defect → Damaged wiring / leaking roof.
  2. Contact Landlord → Demand repair under lease.
  3. Landlord Refuses → Document in writing.
  4. Verify Tenancy → Confirm next 5 years possession.
  5. Estimate Cost → Must be ≤ ₹5,000 per case.
  1. Apply Recovery Clause → To be recovered from future rent.
  2. Lease Endorsement → Right to remove installation.
  3. DH Sanctions → Up to ₹5,000/case.
  4. Execute Work → Through approved agency.
  5. Adjust in Rent Bills → Full recovery before lease expiry.

Refund of Earnest Money [Sl. 14(a)]

  1. Identify Tender → Verify within DH's competency.
  2. Tender Outcome → Rejected, or successful executed.
  3. Verify Refund Eligibility → Per GFR + tender terms.
  4. Check Forfeiture → Ensure no breach by tenderer.
  5. Compute Amount → Originally deposited.
  6. DH Sanctions → Full Powers, no IFA.
  7. Issue Refund Order → Cheque / bank transfer.
  8. Update Tender File → For audit trail.

💰 Numerical Data / Key DH Limits (Part 2)

Publicity Printing: ₹20,000 / annum (combined) General Printing: ₹2 Lakh / annum Renting Equipment: As per applicable rules Govt Bldg Repair: ₹50,000 / case Rented Bldg Repair: ₹5,000 / case Legal Charges: ₹2 Lakh / case (max) Refund (5 Categories): Full Powers

🏦 Facilities & Conditions (Key Items)

  • ▪ Publicity Printing [10(i)]: ₹20,000/annum cap is for all activities taken together, NOT per case. Covers: rate charts, mailers, brochures, distribution to POs, marketing executives, customers.
  • ▪ Rented Bldg Repairs [12(ii)]: ₹5,000/case ONLY IF: (a) landlord refuses, (b) cost recoverable from rent, (c) 5-year tenancy certainty, (d) Govt right to remove installations.
  • ▪ EMD Refund [14(a)]: Restricted to tenders within DH's competency only.
  • ▪ Malicious Postage Refund [14(c)]: Original cover/wrapper must accompany application (POIA Manual rule).
  • ▪ Refund of Found Items [14(d)]: For loose items credited to office account from letter boxes/counters.
