🎯 Concept

A Divisional Head (DH) = SSPOs / SPOs / SRM (RMS Division) / Divisional in-charge. He/she is the executive operational head of a Postal/RMS/MMS Division — the level immediately below the Regional/Circle administrative tier.

Schedule III is deliberately compact — it covers only what a Divisional Head genuinely needs for day-to-day operational autonomy. Anything strategic, capital-heavy, or IFA-touching → goes UP to HOD / HOC.

🔑 The "Three Pillars" of Divisional Head Powers (Part 1)

Pillar 1: Establishment Block

Sl. No. 1–7

All "people-related" expenses → FULL POWERS

  • Salaries, Wages, Pensions, Allowances, LTC, Domestic Travel, Foreign Travel.
  • Subject to FRSR / FHB / Nodal Ministry rules + IFA referral via HOD/HOC if mandated.

Pillar 2: Office Expenses Block

Sl. No. 8

Petty operational expenses with specific small ceilings:

  • Non-recurring contingent → ₹10,000
  • Office equipment → ₹5,000
  • Customer meet → ₹10,000
  • Emergent stationery → ₹50,000/annum
EXCEPTION: Utilities (Electricity, Water, Internet, Telephone, Cable) → Full Powers (essential & recurring).

Pillar 3: Rent & Taxes Block

Sl. No. 9

  • Renting of small office buildings → city-class based modest limits (Without FRAC).
  • Rates & Taxes (Municipal/Property/Vehicle) → Full Powers (since these are statutory).

🧠 The "Without FRAC" Cue

  • FRAC = Fair Rent Assessment Committee.
  • DH's renting power is WITHOUT FRAC, meaning DH can rent small office buildings without triggering a FRAC assessment, provided rent stays within X/Y/Z ceilings.
  • Beyond these ceilings → refer to HOC (who can rent up to ₹10 Lakh/month, but with FRAC).

🧠 Why So Many "Full Powers"?

Items like Salaries, Wages, Pensions, Allowances, LTC are already governed by elaborate FRSR/FHB rules with predetermined rates. Granting "Full Powers" doesn't mean DH can spend any amount — it means DH can sanction what is rule-bound, the upper cap being already fixed by the rule itself.

🔟 Step-by-Step Processes

Renting an Office Building Without FRAC [Sl. No. 9(i)]

  1. Identify Need → New/replacement accommodation.
  2. Verify SOA Conformance → Must match approved Schedule of Accommodation.
  3. Determine City Class → X / Y / Z class.
  4. Match Rent to Ceiling → ≤ ₹15K(X) / ₹12K(Y) / ₹8K(Z).
  5. Compare Rates → Align with prevailing market / Govt-approved rate.
  1. DH Sanctions → No FRAC needed.
  2. Submit Quarterly Report → To PMG, copy to DAP.
  3. Inspection Review → Examined during routine inspection.
  4. If rent > ceiling → Refer to HOC (up to ₹10L/month with FRAC + CIFA).

Conducting a Customer Meet [Sl. No. 8(v)]

  1. Identify Purpose → Promote postal products/services.
  2. Plan Budget → Total ≤ ₹10,000 per meet.
  3. Allocate Sub-Components → Hiring ≤₹2K, Snacks ≤₹200/head, Stationery ≤₹100/head.
  4. Estimate Headcount → Compute per-head expenses.
  5. Prepare Folders/Mailers → Use OH-13.
  6. DH Sanctions Expenditure.
  7. Conduct Meet → Document attendance.
  8. Maintain Vouchers for audit.

💰 Numerical Data / Key DH Limits (Part 1)

Non-Recurring Contingent: ₹10,000 / case Hot & Cold Weather: ₹10,000 / case Furniture/Fixtures: ₹2 Lakh / annum / div Customer Meet: ₹10,000 / meet Office Equipment: ₹5,000 / case Emergent Stationery: ₹50,000 / annum Mail (non-rail/air): ₹3,000 / month / case Embedding/Painting: ₹10,000 / case Rent (X/Y/Z): ₹15K / ₹12K / ₹8K per month Hiring for Exams: ₹5,000 / case

🏦 Facilities & Conditions (Key Items)

  • ▪ Foreign Travel: "Full Powers" but subject to prior approval of Secretary (Posts) / MoC / SCoS.
  • ▪ Emergent Stationery: Triggered ONLY after PSD issues non-availability certificate.
  • ▪ Furniture & Fixtures: Threshold = ₹1 Lakh OR 3 years useful life; items beyond threshold → classified under capital OH-74.
  • ▪ Office Equipment: Excludes computers; threshold = ₹1 Lakh / 3 years; items beyond threshold → capital OH-52.
  • ▪ IFA Reference Rule: IFA consultation never goes directly through DH; if rules mandate it, matter is referred to HOD/HOC.
