⚡ EXAM INSIGHT — MUST READ

  • 🔥
    Schedule III applies to Heads of Postal Divisions (SPOs, SSPOs, SRMs).
  • 🔥
    Foundation rule (Para 6): DH does NOT consult IFA directly — matter referred to HOD/HOC if IFA mandated.
  • 🔥
    Customer Meet Total = ₹10,000 with sub-limits: Hiring ₹2,000, Tea/Snacks ₹200/head, Stationery ₹100/head.
  • 🔥
    Renting (Without FRAC) = ₹15,000 / ₹12,000 / ₹8,000 per month for X / Y / Z class.
  • 🔥
    Quarterly report on rentings → To PMG with copy to DAP; reviewed at inspection.
  • 🔥
    Non-Recurring Contingent (DH) = ₹10,000 — note the massive gap with HOC (₹3 Lakh) and HOD (₹1 Lakh).
  • 🔥
    Furniture/Fixtures cap = ₹2 Lakh per annum per division (not "per case").
  • 🔥
    Office Equipment = ₹5,000/case (excludes computers).
  • 🔥
    Emergent Stationery = ₹50,000/annum only after PSD non-availability certificate.
  • 🔥
    Mail carriage by other modes = ₹3,000/month/case (non-rail/non-air only).
  • 🔥
    Hot/Cold weather, Embedding/Painting, Hiring for events → all share ₹10,000 / ₹5,000 ceiling.
  • 🔥
    DH has Full Powers for Salaries, Wages, Pensions, Allowances, LTC, Domestic Travel (rule-bound powers).
  • 🔥
    DH Foreign Travel = Full Powers BUT prior approval of Secretary (Posts) / MoC / SCoS required.
  • 🔥
    DFPR 2024 + Rule 12(2) = Parent rule even for Schedule III.

📌 ULTRA-REVISION POINTS

  1. Schedule III = Powers of Heads of Postal Divisions; Para 6 of Preamble = IFA goes through HOD/HOC.
  2. Salaries / Wages / Pensions / Allowances / LTC / Domestic Travel = Full Powers.
  3. Foreign Travel = Full Powers but prior approval of Secretary/MoC/SCoS required.
  4. Non-recurring contingent = ₹10,000/case (vs HOC ₹3L / HOD ₹1L — drastic gap).
  5. Customer Meet = ₹10,000/meet with sub-limits (₹2,000 hiring + ₹200/head tea + ₹100/head stationery).
  6. Furniture & Fixtures = ₹2 Lakh per annum per Division.
  7. Office Equipment = ₹5,000/case (excludes computers).
  8. Emergent Stationery = ₹50,000/annum only after PSD non-availability certificate.
  9. Renting (Without FRAC): X = ₹15,000 | Y = ₹12,000 | Z = ₹8,000 per month.
  10. Quarterly rental report to PMG with copy to DAP; reviewed at inspection.
  11. Rates & Taxes = Full Powers; Hiring premises (events) = ₹5,000/case.
  12. Mail by other modes = ₹3,000/month/case | Embedding/Painting/Sign Boards = ₹10,000/case.
