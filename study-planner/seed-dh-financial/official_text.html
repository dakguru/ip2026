📜 Official Provision — DFPR 2024, Schedule III

Schedule III of the revised Schedule of Financial Powers delegates financial powers to the Heads of Postal Divisions in the Department of Posts under Rule 12(2) of DFPR 2024 (effective 01.04.2024; revised Schedule effective from 01.04.2025).

Definition (Note under Schedule II): The "Head of Department" includes Secretary PSB, Chief PMGs, CGMs, Director RAKNPA, PMGs, Sr. DDG (PAF), GMs (PAF), GM (CEPT), Chief Engineers and Director (Foreign Post). Divisional Heads are a separate, lower tier with their own Schedule III.

⚠️ Key Governing Principle (Para 6 of Preamble — CRITICAL):

"With respect to the financial powers delegated to Divisional Heads, if Government rules, manuals, policies, guidelines, procedures, or directives explicitly mandate IFA consultation, the matter must be referred to the concerned HOD / HOC as required."

This single sentence is the foundation rule of Schedule III — Divisional Heads do NOT have direct IFA access; whenever IFA consultation is mandated, the matter goes upward to the HOD / HOC.

📋 Scope & Conditions on Exercise of Powers (Para 3 & 4):

  • Powers exercised for incurring revenue or capital expenditure subject to provisions of DFPR 2024, GFR, Procurement Manuals, FR/SR, Departmental Code Books.
  • Subject to availability of funds, observance of formalities, and necessity established on each occasion.
  • No work requiring sanction of higher authority may be split into parts.
  • For multi-period contracts → total currency value = limit basis.
  • Project sanction limits apply to total cost of all constituent estimates.
  • Authorising Gazetted Officers does not absolve the Head of Department of responsibility.
