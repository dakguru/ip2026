Scenario 1 — DH in Karur Division Renting a Small SO Building

SPOs Karur Division needs to rent a building for a new SO in a Z-class town at ₹7,500/month.

Action: Sl. No. 9(i) → Z-class limit = ₹8,000/month → within DH power.

Conditions: SOA conformance, prevailing market rate check, Govt-approved rate verification.

Outcome: DH sanctions; quarterly report sent to PMG with copy to DAP; no FRAC required.

Outcome: DH sanctions; meet conducted; vouchers maintained.
