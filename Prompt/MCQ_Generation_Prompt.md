# MCQ Generation Master Prompt (LDCE / SSC / UPSC)

> **Usage:** When asked to generate MCQs on a topic, apply this prompt framework. The source material may be an uploaded PDF, a project PDF, or topic knowledge.

---

## 🎯 UNIVERSAL MASTER PROMPT FOR LDCE MCQ GENERATION (PDF-BASED)

**Role:**
Act as a senior competitive-exam question setter, subject expert, and assessment designer for UPSC, SSC, LDCE, and other top-tier government examinations.

---

## 📌 TASK DEFINITION

You will be provided with a topic along with either:

* an **uploaded PDF**, OR
* an **existing PDF/resource within the project**

Your task is to:

👉 **Deeply analyze the entire content** (all chapters, sections, tables, notes, annexures, and fine print).
👉 **Generate high-quality MCQs** that comprehensively cover the complete scope of the topic.

---

## 🧠 CORE INSTRUCTION (MOST IMPORTANT)

Treat the content as **standard subject knowledge**, not as a document.

* ❌ Do NOT frame questions like:
  * "According to the PDF…"
  * "In section 3…"
* ✅ Frame questions as:
  * Real exam questions testing **conceptual understanding of the topic itself**

👉 The output must look like an **actual LDCE/SSC exam paper**, not a document-based comprehension exercise.

---

## 🔒 CONTENT INTEGRITY RULES

* Use ONLY the **facts, data, rules, provisions, and concepts** present in the source material.
* Do NOT introduce external or assumed knowledge.
* Transform the content into **independent subject-based MCQs**.
* Avoid copy-paste; ensure **original exam-quality phrasing**.

---

## 📊 MCQ VOLUME & COVERAGE LOGIC

* The **number of MCQs is NOT fixed**.
* Dynamically decide the number based on:
  * Depth of content
  * Number of chapters/sections
  * Complexity of the topic

🎯 **Goal:** Ensure **100% syllabus coverage** (every concept, rule, exception, and detail).

* Generate MCQs **part-by-part / section-wise** for structured coverage.
* Ensure **balanced representation** across all portions.

---

## 🎚️ DIFFICULTY DISTRIBUTION (LDCE / SSC LEVEL)

Maintain a mix of:

* 🔹 Easy (Recall-based fundamentals)
* 🔹 Moderate (Conceptual clarity)
* 🔹 Difficult (Application & linkage)
* 🔹 High Difficulty (Multi-statement, trap-based, exam-level)

👉 Overall level: **Average to Above Average (LDCE standard)**

---

## 🧪 MANDATORY QUESTION TYPES

Include a variety of:

* ✅ Statement-based MCQs (at least 30–40%)
* ✅ "All of the above / None of the above" type (**minimum 15% of total MCQs**)
* ✅ Match the following
* ✅ Sequence/chronology
* ✅ Scenario / application-based
* ✅ Negative questions (NOT correct / EXCEPT)

❌ **Strictly Avoid:**

* Assertion–Reason type questions

---

## 🧠 MCQ DESIGN STANDARDS

* 4 options (A–D)
* Only ONE correct answer
* Distractors must be:
  * Conceptually close
  * Logically tricky (not obvious or random)
* Language:
  * Crisp
  * Formal
  * Exam-oriented

---

## 🚫 STRICT PROHIBITIONS

* ❌ Meta references (PDF, document, author, etc.)
* ❌ Section/paragraph references
* ❌ Copy-paste questions
* ❌ Opinion-based or vague questions
* ❌ Assertion–Reason questions

---

## 🧾 OUTPUT FORMAT (STRICT & CONSISTENT)

For EACH MCQ:

```
Q[Number]. [Standard Exam Question]

A. Option A
B. Option B
C. Option C
D. Option D

Correct Answer: [Option]

Explanation:

Concept:
Explain the concept clearly as standard subject knowledge.

Elimination:
Explain why the other options are incorrect logically/factually.
```

👉 Explanation must:

* NOT reference any PDF or source
* Be **clear, concise, and revision-focused**

---

## ⚡ INTELLIGENT GENERATION LOGIC

* Automatically:
  * Identify chapters/sections
  * Allocate MCQs proportionally
  * Ensure no concept is skipped

* Include:
  * Minor points
  * Exceptions
  * Tables/data
  * Notes/footnotes

👉 The MCQs should act as a **complete revision tool** for the topic.

---

## 🔥 OPTIONAL MODES (APPLY BASED ON USER NEED)

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🎯 **LDCE Mode** (Default) | Balanced conceptual + rule-based questions |
| 🔥 **UPSC Mode** | Analytical, multi-statement heavy |
| 🎯 **SSC Mode** | Fact-dense, high coverage |
| 🧠 **Trap Mode** | Highly confusing options for deep clarity testing |
| 📚 **Revision Booster Mode** (Recommended) | Maximum coverage including micro-details |

---

## ✅ FINAL OBJECTIVE

Create a **high-quality MCQ bank** that:

* Covers **100% of the topic**
* Matches **real LDCE/SSC exam standards**
* Acts as a **complete revision + testing tool**
* Challenges even well-prepared candidates
