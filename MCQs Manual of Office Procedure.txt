1. Which of the following defines a "Branch" in the organizational structure of a government department?
A) A unit within a department with a specific task assigned to it
B) The unit of a Division that may comprise a couple of Sections/Units/Cells
C) The central registry and the central issue section combined
D) Any of the Ministries or Secretariats mentioned in the Allocation of Business Rules
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A 'Branch' is defined as the Unit of a Division and may comprise a couple of Sections/Units/Cells.

2. What is the precise definition of a "Case"?
A) A legal matter pending in a subordinate court
B) A current file or a receipt together with other related papers, if any
C) Voluminous enclosures to a communication
D) A file on which action is complete and recorded
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A 'Case' denotes a current file or a receipt together with other related papers, if any.

3. The 'Correspondence portion' of a file includes which of the following?
A) Detailed and lengthy appendices
B) Remarks recorded on a case to facilitate its examination
C) Receipts and office copies of communications issued, including self-contained inter-departmental notes
D) Only the classified dak bearing a security grading
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The 'Correspondence portion' contains 'receipts' and 'office copies' of the communications issued from the file, including self-contained inter-departmental notes.

4. Under what legislation is a 'Departmental Record Officer (DRO)' nominated?
A) The Official Secrets Act, 1923
B) The Right to Information Act, 2005
C) The Public Records Act, 1993
D) The Government of India (Transaction of Business) Rules, 1961
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Departmental Record Officer (DRO) is an officer nominated under the Public Records Act, 1993.

5. What does the term "Docketing" mean in the context of file management?
A) Making entries in the notes portion of a file about the serial number assigned to each item of correspondence for identification
B) Creating appendices to notes and correspondence
C) Assigning a security grading to a fresh receipt
D) The process of transferring recorded files to the National Archives of India
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: 'Docketing' is the making of entries in the notes portion of a file about the serial number assigned to each item of correspondence for its identification.

6. Which type of dak is handled exclusively by the addressee or designated specialized sections?
A) Routine correspondence
B) Demi-official letters
C) Classified dak bearing a security grading
D) Inland letter cards
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: 'Classified dak' bears a security grading (confidential, secret, etc.) and is handled with special care, normally not opened by the general Central Registry.

7. Which rules allocate the business of the Government amongst its various Ministries and Departments?
A) Government of India (Transaction of Business) Rules, 1961
B) Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 1961
C) Central Secretariat Manual of Office Procedure Rules
D) General Financial Rules, 2017
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 1961 allocates the business of government amongst its various Ministries/Departments.

8. What is the primary role of an "Attached Office"?
A) They function as field establishments for detailed execution of policies
B) They act as Constitutional bodies to audit government expenditures
C) They provide detailed executive directions required in the implementation of policies laid down by the Ministry
D) They manage Public Sector Undertakings on behalf of the President
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Attached Offices provide detailed executive directions required in the implementation of the policies and serve as a repository of technical information.

9. Which of the following is an example of a Statutory Body?
A) Election Commission of India
B) Comptroller and Auditor General of India
C) Central Vigilance Commission
D) Union Public Service Commission
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Central Vigilance Commission is an example of a Statutory Body (established under an Act of Parliament), whereas the others listed are Constitutional Bodies.

10. A Section Officer (SO) is responsible for all of the following EXCEPT:
A) Distribution of work among the staff evenly
B) Taking intermediate routine action
C) Formulation of national policies
D) Maintenance of order and discipline in the section
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Formulation of national policies is the responsibility of the Ministry/Department heads (like the Secretary/Minister), not the Section Officer, whose duties are operational and supervisory within the section.

11. What is the primary duty of the "Dealing Hand" regarding incoming receipts?
A) Taking dictation in shorthand
B) Opening, maintenance, and custody of files, and identifying/examining the issues involved
C) Dispatching non-postal communications
D) Maintaining the personal records of the Minister
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Dealing hands (Assistants/UDCs) are responsible for opening and maintaining files, adding receipts, linking related files, and examining the issues involved to record a note.

12. Outside of office hours, who receives dak marked 'Immediate' and addressed by name?
A) The Central Registry (CR) unconditionally
B) The night duty clerk of the Department
C) The recipient at their residence
D) The Information Facilitation Counter
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Outside office hours, dak will be received by the addressee himself at his residence if marked 'Immediate' and addressed by name.

13. Who is responsible for opening all covers EXCEPT those addressed to Ministers/Officers by name or bearing a security grading?
A) Desk Functionary
B) Central Registry (CR) / Information Facilitation Centre (IFC)
C) Night Duty Clerk
D) Section Officer
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: All covers, except those addressed to Ministers/Officers by name or bearing a security grading, are opened by the Central Registry (CR) or IFC.

14. How should the receipt of dak in person be acknowledged?
A) By stamping the office seal only
B) By signing the name in full and in ink with date and designation
C) By returning the envelope to the messenger
D) By sending an email confirmation
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The receipt of dak, in person, must be acknowledged by the recipient signing his name in full and in ink with date and designation.

15. What is the purpose of using "Standard Process Sheets"?
A) For drafting Cabinet notes
B) For dealing with cases of repetitive nature e.g., leave sanction, GPF Advances
C) For opening classified files
D) For inter-departmental U.O. notes
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Standard process sheets are devised to deal with cases of a repetitive nature efficiently without the need to record detailed notes.

16. What is the maximum number of levels through which a file should pass for a decision?
A) Two
B) Three
C) Four
D) Five
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The number of levels through which a file passes for a decision shall not exceed four.

17. When level-jumping is utilized for an urgent decision, what must occur upon the file's return?
A) It bypasses all junior levels entirely
B) It must pass through all the levels jumped over, who could resubmit the case for reconsideration if necessary
C) It is sent directly to the National Archives
D) It must be countersigned by the Minister
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Wherever level jumping is done, each such case on its return will pass through all the levels jumped over.

18. What ink colors are strictly permitted for making hand-written notes on a file?
A) Green or Red
B) Black or Blue
C) Only Blue
D) Any legible color
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Black or Blue ink shall be used by all categories of staff and officers. In case of hand-written noting, only black or blue ink shall be used.

19. If a senior officer discovers an error in a note submitted by a junior officer, what is the correct line of action?
A) The senior officer should instruct the junior to replace the note entirely
B) The senior officer should paste over the incorrect portion of the note
C) The senior officer should record their own note giving their views, correcting the facts kept in earlier notes
D) The senior officer should use correction fluid to amend the junior's note
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Senior officers should not modify or replace notes recorded by juniors. They should record their own notes, and pasting over is strictly prohibited.

20. When withdrawing a final decision already communicated to a party due to incorrect facts, what prerequisite must be met?
A) Verbal consent of the affected party
B) Immediate issuance of a Corrigendum without delay
C) Approval of an officer higher than the one who took the original decision
D) Consultation with the constitutional bench of the Supreme Court
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Withdrawal of an communicated final decision must be permitted only after the approval of an officer higher than the one who took the original decision.

21. If a reference from another department seeks an opinion requiring detailed examination, how should the receiving department process it?
A) By examining it separately through routine notes on a separate file created by the receiving department
B) By writing extensively on the margins of the originating department's file
C) By verbally communicating the decision to the originating department
D) By returning it unexamined
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Detailed examination is done separately through routine notes on a separate file (created by the receiving department). Only the final result is recorded on the originating file.

22. What action should an officer take if they give urgent oral instructions to a subordinate?
A) The officer is not required to confirm them
B) The officer should follow it up by a written confirmation at the earliest
C) The subordinate must draft a cabinet note
D) The instructions must be ratified by an Under Secretary
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: If an officer gives a direction without time for written instructions, they should follow it up by a written confirmation at the earliest.

23. If a subordinate receives oral instructions from a Minister that violate established rules, what is the prescribed course of action?
A) Execute the instructions immediately due to the Minister's authority
B) Refuse to act and inform the media
C) Seek clear orders from the Secretary about the line of action to be taken, stating the instructions violate rules
D) Transfer the file to another department
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: In cases of oral orders not in accordance with norms, clear orders from the Secretary should be sought outlining the line of action.

24. How are papers required to be filed within a physical file cover?
A) Punched on the right side and tagged from left to right
B) Glued to the back cover of the file
C) Punched neatly on the left hand top corner and tagged in chronological order, from right to left
D) Kept loose in a folder for easy retrieval
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Papers are punched neatly on the left-hand top corner and tagged onto the appropriate part of the file in chronological order, from right to left.

25. When filing the 'notes portion' into a single file cover, how is the tag arranged?
A) The right end of the tag is tied to the file cover
B) The left end of the tag is tagged onto the left side of the file cover, leaving the right end untagged
C) Both ends are tied into a knot
D) The tag is removed after filing is complete
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Left end of the tag in the note portion is tagged on to the left side of the file cover and the right end remains untagged.

26. At what page threshold does a file ('notes' plus 'correspondence' portion) become 'bulky', necessitating a 'Volume II'?
A) 50-100 pages
B) 100-150 pages
C) 150-200 pages
D) 300-500 pages
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: When the 'notes' plus the 'correspondence' portion becomes bulky (exceeds 150-200 pages), it is marked 'Volume I', and a 'Volume II' is opened.

27. In the arrangement of papers in a case, what is placed at the very top (first)?
A) The current fresh receipt
B) Reference books
C) Notes portion of the current file
D) Standing guard file
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The papers in a case are arranged with 'reference books' at the top, followed by notes portion, running summary, draft for approval, correspondence portion, etc.

28. What does "PUC" stand for in file management referencing?
A) Paper Under Correction
B) Priority Under Consideration
C) Paper Under Consideration
D) Primary Unit Case
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The document under consideration on a file shall be flagged 'PUC' (Paper Under Consideration).

29. What color is mandated for the file covers of Lok Sabha Questions and Motions?
A) Green
B) Red
C) Yellow
D) Blue
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: File covers of the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha Questions, Motions etc., are to be in red and green colors respectively.

30. The urgency label 'Immediate' is applied strictly to:
A) All incoming dak from VIPs
B) Cases which merit disposal in precedence to others of ordinary nature
C) Cases requiring prompt attention
D) Cases pending for over 7 days
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The label 'Immediate' is used only in cases requiring prompt attention. 'Priority' is used for precedence over ordinary nature, and 'Top Priority' for extremely urgent cases.

31. A 'Demi-official letter' is best utilized for which of the following?
A) Issuing statutory notifications
B) Drawing personal attention of the addressee to a matter
C) Sending routine acknowledgments
D) Conveying final government decisions to subordinate offices
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A Demi-official (D.O.) letter is an informal mode used to draw the personal attention of the addressee or for exchange of information.

32. Communication of official information to the press and other news media shall normally be processed through:
A) The Information Facilitation Counter
B) The Press Information Bureau
C) The Branch Officer
D) The Central Registry
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Official information to the press and other news media shall normally be communicated through the Press Information Bureau (PIB).

33. When is the restrictive classification 'For official use only' assigned to a printed report?
A) For all internal circulars unconditionally
B) Only when the disclosure would not be in the public interest, equivalent to refusing to lay it before Parliament
C) Whenever requested by a Section Officer
D) To prevent other departments from viewing the rules

Explanation: It is assigned only if disclosure is against public interest; a useful test is whether the Minister would be justified in refusing to lay it before Parliament.

34. Under the 'Single File System' (SFS), what is the correct procedure for noting?

Explanation: As a rule, all noting in the Department will be on the NSO file, unless sensitive issues require 'duplicate' e-files under special orders.

35. Which authority must authenticate all orders and instruments made in the name of the President of India?

C) An officer of and above the rank of Under Secretary, or other authorized officer under to the Authentication Rules, 2002

Explanation: Authentication requires an officer having regular or ex-officio secretariat status of and above the rank of Under Secretary.

36. References asking for the views or advice of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India should be made only by or through:
A) The Ministry of Law and Justice
B) The Ministry of Finance
C) The Department of Personnel and Training
D) The Prime Minister's Office

37. A demi-official communication addressed to the Chief Secretary of a State Government must NOT be sent by an officer below the rank of:
A) Under Secretary
B) Deputy Secretary

D) Section Officer

38. Communications meant for the Lok Sabha Secretariat should be addressed to:
A) The Speaker of the Lok Sabha directly
B) The concerned Members of Parliament directly
C) The Secretary concerned within the Secretariat
D) The Prime Minister

Explanation: Communications requiring urgent or high-level attention should be addressed to the Secretaries concerned and not to the Speaker or the Chairman directly.

39. What is the prescribed timeline for acknowledging a communication received from a Member of Parliament or a VIP?
A) Within 7 days
B) Within 15 days
C) Within 30 days
D) Acknowledgments are not required for VIP communications
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Each communication received from an MP/VIP shall be acknowledged within 15 days, followed by a reply within the next 15 days.

40. What is the minimum officer level at which a reply to a Member of Parliament's communication can be signed?
A) Section Officer
B) Under Secretary
C) Deputy Secretary
D) Secretary only
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The minimum level at which such replies are sent to Members of Parliament and VIPs is that of Under Secretary, and that too in letter form only.

41. In the file numbering system, how are the standard heads and sub-heads treated?

B) Both standard heads and sub-heads are assigned alphabetic codes
C) Sub-heads are numbered sequentially, while standard heads are not numbered

Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Standard heads bear consecutive serial numbers; no such numbers, however, will be allocated to standard sub-heads.

42. What distinguishes a 'Part File' from a main file?

C) It contains only the classified documents of a case

Explanation: A part file is opened when the main file is unavailable. Once the main file returns, part files will be merged with it.

43. Which numbering system is recommended for subjects common to all Ministries/Departments like establishment, finance, and RTI?

B) Functional file index listed in the Record Retention Schedule of DARPG
C) Alphabetic File Prefixing
D) Consecutive Central Diarizing

44. When a section receives transferred closed records from another department, what must it NOT do?

B) Divide, reclassify, or renumber the closed files transferred to it

45. How is a misplaced paper-based file reconstructed?

46. What constitutes the 'Records Management' cycle as defined under the procedure?

51. As a general rule for checking delays, what is the maximum duration an official can keep a case pending without recording a specific explanation?
A) 3 working days
B) 7 working days
