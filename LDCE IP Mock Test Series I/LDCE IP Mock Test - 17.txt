Q1. As per the definitions in the Financial Hand Book Volume I, which of the following best describes the term "Bank"?
A. Any scheduled commercial bank approved by the Ministry of Finance
B. Any office or branch of the Banking Department of the RBI or SBI acting as the agent of the RBI
C. Only the State Bank of India and its wholly-owned subsidiaries
D. Any nationalized bank where a post office maintains its drawing account
Answer: B
Explanation: According to Rule 2(b) of FHB Volume I, the term "Bank" means any office or branch of the Banking Department of the Reserve Bank of India, any branch of the State Bank of India acting as the agent of the RBI, and any other agency appointed by the RBI.

Explanation: As per the exception to Rule 52 of FHB Volume I, petty cases, defined as those involving losses not exceeding Rs. 10,000 each, need not be reported to the Audit Officer or Accounting Authority concerned.

Q5. If a Government servant's pay is attached by a court order in execution of a decree (other than for maintenance), how is the net attachable amount calculated?

B. Gross emoluments minus Allowances minus One-third of Basic Pay

Answer: C
Explanation: According to Rule 175 of FHB Volume I, the calculation of the maximum amount attachable by a Civil Court for decrees other than maintenance is: Net amount attachable = (Gross emoluments (X) - Allowances (Y) - 1000) / 3.
