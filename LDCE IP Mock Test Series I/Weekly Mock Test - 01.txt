### **SECTION 2: ANSWER KEY & DETAILED EXPLANATIONS**

**Q1: B**

* **Concept**: Section 4 of the Post Office Act, 2023 grants the Post Office the **exclusive privilege of issuing postage stamps**.
* **Elimination**: Statement 1 is incorrect because the 2023 Act (unlike the 1898 Act) does not explicitly mention the exclusive privilege of conveying letters in the provided text; Section 4 is specific to stamps. Statement 3 is incorrect because Section 4(2) empowers the **Director General** (not Central Govt directly) to make regulations regarding supply and sale.

**Q2: B**

* **Concept**: Section 5 of the Post Office Act, 2023 states, "The **Central Government** may prescribe standards for addressing on the items, address identifiers and usage of postcodes."

**Q3: A**

* **Concept**: Rule 16(5) of the Post Office Rules, 2024 mandates the Central Government to constitute a **review committee** to oversee interception, consisting of the Director General (Chairman) and two Members of the Board.

**Q4: B**

* **Concept**: Section 2(a) of the Post Office Act, 2023 defines "Director General" as the Director General of Postal Services appointed by the Central Government.

**Q5: B**

* **Concept**: Section 10 of the Post Office Act, 2023 ("Exemption from liability") states that the Post Office shall **not incur any liability except such liability as may be prescribed**.

**Q6: B**

* **Concept**: Rule 16(3) allows a Divisional Head to order interception in emergent cases. This must be confirmed by the competent authority within **seven working days**.
* **Elimination**: Statement 3 is incorrect because the Proviso to Rule 16(3) states that if confirmation is not received within seven days, such interception shall **cease to operate**.

**Q7: B**

* **Concept**: Rule 5 of the Post Office Rules, 2024 defines the Postal Services Board as the **apex level policy making body** for services provided by the Post Office.

**Q8: D**

* **Concept**: Section 9(1) of the Post Office Act, 2023 lists the grounds: security of the State, friendly relations with foreign states, public order, emergency, public safety, or contravention of the Act.

**Q9: B**

* **Concept**: Section 6 of the Post Office Act, 2023 is titled "Power to give effect to arrangements with other countries."

**Q10: B**

* **Concept**: Rule 8 of the Post Office Rules, 2024 states the Philatelic Advisory Committee shall **advise the Government on issues of commemorative and special postage stamps**.

**Q11: B**

* **Concept**: Section 3(i) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 defines "minor" as a person who has not attained the age of majority under the **Indian Majority Act, 1875**.

**Q12: B**

* **Concept**: Section 4A(4)(a) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 states that if the deposit does not exceed the prescribed limit, the Officer may pay it to **any person appearing to him to be entitled to receive it**.

**Q13: C**

* **Concept**: Section 3B of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 permits a minor aged **10 years** to operate an account (Statement 1) and guardians to operate on behalf of minors (Statement 2).

**Q14: B**

* **Concept**: The Schedule (Part A) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 lists **Sukanya Samriddhi Account** as an existing scheme.
* **Elimination**: Annuity Certificates and Treasury Savings Certificates are in Part B (Discontinued).

**Q15: D**

* **Concept**: Section 15 of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 empowers the Central Government to make rules on all these matters: redressal of grievances (m), benchmark for interest rates (k), and fees (g).

**Q16: B**

* **Concept**: Section 4(c) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 states that where the nominee is a minor, the depositor appoints a person to receive the deposit in the event of death **during the minority of the nominee**.

**Q17: C**

* **Concept**: Section 14 of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 protects the **Authorised Officer** or any officer of the Government for action taken in good faith.

**Q18: B**

* **Concept**: Section 4(d) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 states that the transfer of deposit shall **automatically cancel a nomination** previously made.

**Q19: D**

* **Concept**: Section 3(h) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 defines "guardian" as either parent, a person entitled under law (if parents not alive/capable), or a legal guardian appointed by a court.

**Q20: B**

* **Concept**: Section 16(1) of the Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 (as amended) explicitly repeals the Government Savings Certificates Act, 1959 and the Public Provident Fund Act, 1968.

**Q21: B**

* **Concept**: Section 2(u) (implied in definitions text) defines "proceeds of crime" as property derived from criminal activity relating to a **scheduled offence**.

**Q22: B**

* **Concept**: Section 4 of PMLA, 2002 prescribes punishment which shall not be less than 3 years but may extend to **7 years**.

**Q23: B**

* **Concept**: The proviso to Section 4 of PMLA states that for offences related to Narcotic Drugs (NDPS Act), the punishment may extend to **10 years**.

**Q24: C**

* **Concept**: Section 5(1) of PMLA states that a provisional attachment order is valid for a period not exceeding **180 days**.

**Q25: B**

* **Concept**: Section 6 of PMLA states the Central Government appoints the Adjudicating Authority.

**Q26: D**

* **Concept**: "Reporting Entity" (Section 2 wa) includes a banking company, financial institution, intermediary, etc. It does not include an individual account holder.

**Q27: B**

* **Concept**: Section 12(a) and the text regarding "Maintenance of Records" mandate keeping records for **5 years** from the date of transaction or end of business relationship.

**Q28: B**

* **Concept**: Section 6 of PMLA states the Adjudicating Authority shall consist of a Chairperson and **two other Members**.

**Q29: B**

* **Concept**: Section 24(a) of PMLA states that in the case of a person charged with the offence, the Court **shall presume that such proceeds of crime are involved in money-laundering**.

**Q30: C**

**Q38: B**

**Q40: C**
