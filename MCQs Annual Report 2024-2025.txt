* **Fact:** 'Posts and Telegraphs' is a subject under the Union List (List I), Entry 31 of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution.


* **Q3. Correct Answer: C**
* **Fact:** The Post Office Act, 2023 received assent in Dec 2023 but came into force on **18th June, 2024**.

* **Fact:** The average area served by a Post Office is approximately **21.34 sq. km** (calculated based on 32.87 lakh sq km / ~1.65 lakh POs, a standard metric in the report).

* **Concept:** The **Secretary, Department of Posts** acts as the Chairman of the Postal Services Board, not the DG Postal Services.
